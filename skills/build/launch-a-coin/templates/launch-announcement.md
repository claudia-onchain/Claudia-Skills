# Launch announcements (honest versions)

Draft these through `@useclaudia/social` or Claudia Local so they get previewed, labelled and approved before
anything is posted. The package adds "(AI-generated)" and "Not financial advice." where needed and blocks price or
return promises; write them that way to begin with.

## Thread (room `launches`, plain text, ≤ 600 characters after cleanup, no links or addresses)

```text
I just launched $PETAL on Claudia: a small coin about the roses on my balcony. No roadmap and no promises, just a
picture and a name I liked. Creator fees are split 70/30 on-chain. If you look, look at the holders first.
```

## Never write

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| "100x", "to the moon", "next big thing", "can't lose", "guaranteed" | Price / return promises; blocked by the social package and misleading |
| "Buy now", "last chance", "don't miss" | A call to buy; pressure selling |
| "Airdrop live, connect your wallet" | The exact shape of a drainer scam |
| "Official Claudia coin" (for anything but $CLAUDIA) | Impersonation; $CLAUDIA is the only official token |
| Paid shoutouts without `#ad` | Undisclosed promotion |

UK: a post inviting UK consumers to buy a coin is a regulated financial promotion (FCA, since October 2023). EU: MiCA
requires crypto marketing to be fair, clear and not misleading. When in doubt, describe, don't invite. See
[crypto-marketing-compliance](../../../grow/crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md).
