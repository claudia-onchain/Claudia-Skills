# Ad policies that matter for AI creators (checked 2026-10)

Read in step 3, before making creative. Ad policies change several times a year; open the linked page before each new
campaign and note the date in the campaign plan. Summaries, not the full policies.

## Meta (Facebook, Instagram)

**Boost vs Ads Manager.** A boosted post is an ad made from an existing post with fewer objectives, placements and
targeting options; Ads Manager adds objectives such as leads, app promotion and sales, more formats and placements.
https://www.facebook.com/business/help/317083072148603

**Budgets and limits.**
- Minimum daily budgets vary by country, objective, bid strategy and currency; Ads Manager warns when you're below.
  With a cost-per-result goal, the daily budget should be at least 5× the goal.
  https://www.facebook.com/business/help/203183363050448
- Meta may spend up to 75% over the daily budget on some days, but not more than 7× the daily budget in a calendar
  week. https://www.facebook.com/business/help/190490051321426
- **Account spending limit:** set by you, caps total account spend; ads pause when it's reached. Not available on
  prepaid ("available funds") accounts. https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2135167003399680

**AI-generated content.**
- Ads made or significantly edited with Meta's own generative AI tools get "AI info" labels; for a photorealistic
  AI-generated person the label sits next to "Sponsored". Since 1 June 2026 Meta also detects third-party AI media and
  labels it in "About this ad". https://about.fb.com/news/2025/02/gen-ai-transparency-metas-ads-products/
- Political, electoral and social-issue ads must disclose photorealistic images, video or realistic audio made or
  altered with AI; undisclosed ones are rejected.
  https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/SIEP-advertising/SIEP/

**Crypto.** Ads for crypto exchanges and trading, lending, wallets with extra services, mining software or crypto
investment (including affiliate sites) need prior written permission and a recognised regulatory licence or
registration submitted in Meta Business Suite (about 28 jurisdictions accepted, for example the FCA in the UK). Crypto
education and news, storage-only wallets and mining hardware don't need permission.
https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/cryptocurrency-products-and-services

**Political and social issues.** Authorization and a verified "Paid for by" disclaimer are required; ads stay in the
Ad Library for 7 years. In the EU, Meta stopped political, electoral and social-issue ads from October 2025 (EU TTPA
rules). https://about.fb.com/news/2025/07/ending-political-electoral-and-social-issue-advertising-in-the-eu/

**Special ad categories.** Financial products and services (replaced "Credit"), employment and housing ads reaching the
US, Canada and some European countries must be declared; age, gender, postcode, lookalike and some other targeting is
limited or unavailable. https://www.facebook.com/business/help/298000447747885

## TikTok

**Political ads.** "We do not allow paid political advertising"; politicians, parties and PACs can't advertise.
https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-politics-government-and-elections

**AI-generated content.** Significantly edited or AI-generated media (fully AI-made image, video or audio, substantially
altered real footage, voice cloning) is allowed only with the AIGC label or a clear disclaimer, caption, watermark or
sticker; undisclosed AI content is rejected or restricted. Lighting, colour or background edits don't count.
https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-misleading-and-false-content

**Crypto and financial services.** Allowed only in listed markets, usually with a local licence, through a TikTok sales
representative, to 18+ audiences. UK, Ireland and most of the EU/EEA: exchange and custodial-wallet services only; ads for
individual coins and non-custodial wallets prohibited. US/Canada: allowed with licensing; ICOs prohibited. Not allowed
in Promote-only markets. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-financial-services

**Budgets.** Campaign daily budget above $50; ad group daily budget above $20.
https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/budget

**Spark Ads.** Run organic posts as ads, including creators' posts with an authorization code the creator generates in
the TikTok app; engagement counts toward the original post. https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/spark-ads

**Promote** (in-app boosting) follows the same advertising policies, including the political ban.
https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-tiktok-advertising-policies-for-promote

## Reddit

**Political ads.** US federal level only, with certification, a sales representative, a live AMA by the candidate or a
representative linked from every ad, "paid for by" in the copy, and no postcode or gender targeting. AI-generated or
digitally altered content in political ads needs a clear disclosure such as "This ad contains AI-generated content."
https://business.reddithelp.com/s/article/political-advertisements-policy

**Crypto.** Restricted to licensed and registered advertisers (exchanges, wallets, crypto cards and similar) managed by a
Reddit sales representative, in some countries. Never allowed: ads for single tokens or coins, ICOs/IDOs, trading bots,
mixers, pump-and-dump messaging or high-leverage claims.
https://business.reddithelp.com/s/article/financial-cryptocurrency-products-and-services-policy

## X

X's ads policy pages couldn't be verified for this version. Before the first X campaign, read the current ads policies
for political content, financial services (including crypto) and synthetic or manipulated media in the X Ads help
centre, and save the date you checked in the campaign plan.
