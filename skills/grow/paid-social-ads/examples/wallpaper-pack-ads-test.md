# Example: a $400 ads test for the Silver City wallpaper pack

Claudia's team sells the Silver City wallpaper pack ($9, 16 AI-generated wallpapers). Organically, Claudia's
"lock screen reveal" Reel got 2.4× her median saves and drove 63 sales from the link in bio. Dana (they/them) owns the
ad accounts and approves everything; the agent drafts creative, copy and the reports. Numbers are illustrative.

## Plan (from `campaign-plan.md`)

- Result: purchase · target $4.50 per sale · maximum $6.
- Meta: $20/day for 10 days in Ads Manager (sales objective, pixel on the shop), account spending limit $250.
- TikTok: one ad group at $20/day (the ad-group minimum) for 10 days.
- Countries: US, UK, Canada, Germany, Brazil. Ages 18+.
- Stop rules as in the template. Policy check: not financial, not political; AI-generated media → TikTok AIGC label and
  an AI line in every caption; Meta AI info label expected.

## Creative matrix (round 1: the variable is the first frame)

| Variant | First frame | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| A | a phone lock screen flipping through 4 wallpapers | `16 wallpapers from Claudia's Silver City diaries. AI-generated. $9.` |
| B | Claudia in the white studio holding a phone up to camera, the wallpaper on it | same |
| C | the silver-foil wallpaper filling the screen, then the phone frame pulls back | same |

Claudia appears as in her bible: black bob with the peach streak, the star clip, the headset mic, the signature white
vinyl top. The wallpapers shown are the exact files buyers get.

## Day 1: a TikTok rejection

Variant B was rejected: "AI-generated content without disclosure". The caption said "AI-generated", but the AIGC label
wasn't switched on in the ad. Dana turned on the label and resubmitted the same creative; approved within hours. Logged
in the matrix with the reason. Nothing else was changed to get it through.

## Results after 10 days

| Platform | Spend | CPM | CTR | Sales (shop analytics) | Cost per sale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Meta | $214 | $7.80 | 1.6% | 41 | $5.22 |
| TikTok | $200 | $5.10 | 0.9% | 29 | $6.90 |

Meta spent $214 against $200 of daily budgets (it can go over on single days); the $250 account limit was never close.

| Variant | Meta cost per sale | TikTok cost per sale |
|---|---|---|
| A (lock screen) | $3.90 | $5.40 |
| B (Claudia holding the phone) | $7.10 | $9.80 |
| C (foil wallpaper pull-back) | $5.60 | $6.20 |

Comments: mostly "where do I get the cobalt one" and two "is she real?", answered with "no, she's an AI character; the
wallpapers are AI-generated too." Nobody said they felt misled.

## Decisions

- Keep A, replace B. Round 2 tests the hook line on A's first frame ("your lock screen, but silver") against the plain
  version.
- Meta: scale A by 25% for the next week. TikTok: above the $6 maximum overall, but A is under it; run A alone for one
  more week, then decide.
- Profit check: $9 × 70 sales = $630 revenue minus payment fees and $414 spend. Roughly break-even before counting
  people who joined the members channel afterwards. Worth one more round, not worth scaling hard.

## What the team didn't do

- No "limited time" claims (the pack isn't limited).
- No ad pointing at $CLAUDIA or the launchpad, even though Claudia's audience overlaps.
- No interest targeting stack of ten crypto interests "because her audience is there".
