# Paid social ads

Ads buy reach; they don't fix a post nobody wanted. The outcome of this skill is a small, capped campaign that promotes
something already proven to work organically, with creative that passes each platform's 2026 policies (including their
AI-content rules), a spending limit a person set, stop rules decided before launch, and a weekly report in cost per
result. An agent may draft creative, copy and reports; a person owns the ad accounts, the payment method and every
launch.

## When to use this

- A post did well organically and the operator wants to put money behind it.
- Launching a product, membership, template or app page and testing whether paid reach converts.
- Running a creator's post as an ad (Meta partnership ads, TikTok Spark Ads).
- An ad was rejected, or an account was restricted, and someone needs to know why.
- Reading results and deciding whether to scale, change or stop.

Not for: promoting a coin or token (step 3 explains why it's off the table), political or social-issue advocacy, or
anything aimed at under-18s.

## What you need

- A goal with a number: "50 wallpaper-pack sales at under $6 each", "300 waitlist sign-ups at under $2".
- A landing page that works on mobile, with UTM tracking and the platform's pixel or conversion setup if you'll optimise
  for sales or sign-ups.
- Business accounts owned by a real adult person or company: Meta Business Portfolio with an ad account, TikTok Ads
  Manager, X Ads, Reddit Ads. Two-factor authentication on each. The agent never holds ad-account logins or payment
  details.
- A monthly budget the operator can lose without harm. First tests: $150–500 per platform.
- [references/ad-policies-2026.md](references/ad-policies-2026.md) (read in step 3) and
  [references/creative-testing-and-metrics.md](references/creative-testing-and-metrics.md) (read in steps 4 and 8).

## Steps

1. **Pick what to promote from proof.** Boost only content or offers that already beat the account's median organically
   (watch time, saves, clicks, sign-ups). Write the goal, the one action, and the most you'll pay per result in
   [templates/campaign-plan.md](templates/campaign-plan.md). If the organic version flopped, paying for it buys more of
   the same.

2. **Set up accounts and caps before any ad.** A person does this:
   - **Meta:** Business Portfolio → ad account → payment method → **account spending limit** (caps total spend; ads pause
     when it's reached; checked 2026-10). A boost from the app is quick but has fewer objectives, placements and targeting
     options than Ads Manager; use Ads Manager for anything you'll measure.
   - **TikTok:** Ads Manager account (Promote in the app is the lightweight version). Minimum daily budgets are $50 per
     campaign and $20 per ad group (checked 2026-10), so a test needs at least $20 a day per ad group.
   - **X and Reddit:** business ad accounts with the payment method in the operator's name.
   - Write each platform's daily budget, monthly cap and stop rules into the campaign plan.

3. **Check the policies for this ad.** Read [references/ad-policies-2026.md](references/ad-policies-2026.md). The rules
   most likely to bite an AI creator (checked 2026-10):
   - **AI-generated content.** TikTok requires the AIGC label or a clear disclaimer on ads with significantly AI-made or
     AI-altered images, video or audio; undisclosed ones are rejected or restricted. Meta labels ads made with its own AI
     tools ("AI info", next to "Sponsored" for a photorealistic AI person) and, since 1 June 2026, auto-labels detected
     third-party AI media in "About this ad". Put the AI disclosure in the copy as well: `AI-generated character.`
   - **Crypto.** Meta needs prior written permission and a recognised licence for exchanges, wallets with extra services,
     lending and crypto investment ads. TikTok allows some crypto services only in listed markets with licences; ads for
     individual coins are prohibited in the UK and EU. Reddit never allows ads for single tokens or coins. **Never run
     ads for a coin**, including $CLAUDIA or any coin an agent launched.
   - **Political and social issues.** TikTok bans paid political ads. Meta stopped political, electoral and social-issue
     ads in the EU from October 2025 and requires authorization and a "Paid for by" disclaimer elsewhere. Reddit takes
     only US federal political ads with certification. Keep ads off these topics.
   - **Special ad categories (Meta).** Financial products and services, employment and housing ads that reach the US and
     some other countries must be declared and lose detailed targeting.
   - **X:** read X's current ads policies for political content, financial services and synthetic media in the X Ads
     help centre before the first campaign; they weren't verifiable for this version of the skill.

4. **Make 3–5 variants, one change apart.** Read
   [references/creative-testing-and-metrics.md](references/creative-testing-and-metrics.md). Fill
   [templates/ad-creative-matrix.csv](templates/ad-creative-matrix.csv): same offer and landing page, different hooks (or
   different first frames). Vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and Stories; 4:5 or 1:1 for feeds. Every variant:
   - Shows the real product as it is (no AI "improvement" of what the buyer gets).
   - Carries the AI label where the platform has one and an AI line in the copy if the character or scene is AI-made.
   - Makes only claims you can prove, no income or return promises, no fake reviews, no fake UI or "breaking news" bait.
   Hooks and captions: [captions-and-hooks](../../create/captions-and-hooks/SKILL.md).

5. **Use creator posts properly.** To advertise a creator's post, get permission through the platform and pay for usage
   separately: Meta partnership ads (the creator allows boosting on a post tagged with your brand, or shares a partnership
   ad code), TikTok Spark Ads (the creator generates an authorization code in the video's ad settings; you add it in Ads
   Manager). Agree the end date in writing; see [influencer-outreach](../influencer-outreach/SKILL.md).

6. **Target simply and safely.** Adults only (18+, or the platform's higher minimum for your category and market).
   Start broad within the countries you can serve and let the platform's delivery learn; add one interest group as a
   test, not ten. Exclude countries where your product or claims don't comply. Never target by sensitive traits.

7. **Launch with approval, watch spend.** A person reviews every ad in the platform's preview, then
   publishes. For the first 3 days, check spend each day: Meta may spend up to 75% over a daily budget on some days (never
   more than 7× the daily budget in a week), so the account spending limit is the real ceiling. Stop rules, written
   before launch:
   - Spend reaches 2× the target cost per result with zero results → pause that ad.
   - Ad rejected → read the reason, fix the cause, don't resubmit near-identical copy to get past review.
   - Negative comments about being misled or about AI disclosure → pause and fix the copy.

8. **Report weekly and decide.** Fill [templates/weekly-ads-report.md](templates/weekly-ads-report.md): spend, CPM, CTR,
   cost per click, cost per result, frequency, and results from your own analytics (UTMs), not just the platform's. Keep
   the best variant, replace the worst, and change one thing at a time. Scale a winner by 20–30% a week rather than
   doubling overnight. Stop the campaign when cost per result stays above your maximum for two weeks. Results go into
   [kpi-reporting](../kpi-reporting/SKILL.md).

9. **Keep records.** Save the plan, every approved creative with its AI label status, rejections and appeals, and monthly
   invoices from each platform. Ad spend is a business expense the operator will need receipts for.

Ad copy skeleton:

```text
<Hook that matches the first frame>
<What it is, in one plain sentence> · <price or offer>
<AI line if relevant: "AI-generated character" / "AI-generated scenes">
<One action: "Get the pack" / "Join the waitlist">
```

## Check before you finish

- [ ] Ad accounts belong to the operator, with 2FA; the agent has no access to logins or payment details.
- [ ] Account spending limit (Meta) or equivalent caps and daily budgets are set and written in the plan.
- [ ] Nothing advertises a coin, token, exchange or trading product; no political or social-issue messaging.
- [ ] Every ad with AI-made media carries the platform's AI label and an AI line in the copy.
- [ ] Claims are provable; the product shown is the product sold; no fake reviews, testimonials or UI.
- [ ] Creator content runs only with platform permission (partnership ad or Spark code) and paid usage with an end date.
- [ ] A person approved each ad; stop rules were written before launch and applied.
- [ ] The weekly report uses your own tracked results alongside the platform's numbers.

## Pitfalls

- **Boosting a flop.** Paid reach magnifies what's there. Promote proven posts.
- **No spending limit.** Daily budgets flex (Meta can overspend a day by 75%). Set the account-level cap.
- **Hiding the AI.** TikTok rejects undisclosed AI ads; Meta now labels detected AI media anyway. Disclosing up front
  costs nothing and avoids a rejected or restricted account.
- **Resubmitting around a rejection.** Tweaking words to slip past review gets accounts restricted. Fix the cause.
- **Crypto by the back door.** An ad for "Claudia's community" whose landing page pushes a coin is still a crypto ad.
- **Ten variables at once.** Change hook, music, offer and audience together and you learn nothing. One variable per test.
- **Trusting only in-platform conversions.** Check sales and sign-ups in your own analytics too.
- **Running ads on a personal account.** If the person leaves or the account is restricted, the business loses the ads,
  the pixel history and the receipts.
