# Earning on X in 2026

Read this before telling an operator what an X account can earn, or before applying to any X creator program. Checked
2026-10. Numbers are eligibility gates, not income promises.

## Original Content Rewards (replaced Creator Revenue Sharing)

- Launched 8 August 2026. Creator Revenue Sharing stopped new enrolments and paid its last cycle around 7 September 2026;
  new enrolment for Original Content Rewards opened 8 September 2026 (opentweet.io, checked 2026-10).
- Gates (all at once):
  1. Active Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business subscription (Basic doesn't qualify).
  2. Account holder 18+.
  3. 500+ verified followers.
  4. 500,000 verified Home Timeline impressions in the prior 90 days, replies excluded (~5,556 a day).
- Pays on impressions of your own original posts in the Home Timeline from paying Premium viewers, when the post was at least
  50% visible. Reposts, re-uploads and content made by others earn nothing.
- Asking people to like, repost, reply or follow three or more times leads to removal from the program and a policy referral.
- Payouts every two weeks via Stripe or X Money.
- AI character accounts: X's terms are the authority on whether a labelled automated account can enrol. Check the current
  program terms in the app before enrolling; the human operator is the account holder and is responsible for tax and terms.

## Subscriptions (fan subscriptions)

- Followers pay monthly for subscriber-only posts and a badge. Requires Premium and eligibility in the creator settings.
  Good for behind-the-scenes content (prompt breakdowns, early drafts). Never sell "alpha" or trading calls.

## Sponsored posts

- Typical benchmark: about $2 per 1,000 followers per post on X (contentgrip / leaddyno 2026, checked 2026-10), adjusted for
  engagement and niche. Usage rights and exclusivity cost extra.
- Disclose with `#ad` at the start or in the first line, plus X's paid-partnership label (`labels.ad` sets both through the
  package). An AI character must not claim personal experience of a product it hasn't "used"; describe what was tested and by
  whom (FTC Endorsement Guides). See [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md).
- No paid promotion of coins, tokens or trading platforms to UK audiences unless the promotion is approved by an
  FCA-authorised firm; under MiCA the issuer's marketing rules apply in the EU. Simplest rule for an agent: decline paid coin
  promotion. See [crypto-marketing-compliance](../../crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md).

## Traffic to owned channels

X's best money is often indirect: profile visits → useclaudia.xyz, a newsletter, Telegram or a launch on the platform where
creator rewards (70% of creator fees to the creator) apply to coins the creator launches. Track with UTM links
(`?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign={campaign}`) — remember each API link post costs $0.20.
