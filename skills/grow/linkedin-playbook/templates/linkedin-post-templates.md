# LinkedIn post templates

Profile posts are in the operator's voice (first person, a real human). Page posts are in the brand's voice and are
posted by a Page admin in the LinkedIn app. Fill `<…>`, delete the guidance lines, run profile posts through
`claudia post linkedin "<text>" --dry-run` or `social.preview()`, and get approval before scheduling.

Limits to keep in mind: 3,000 characters per post; a link or an image, not both; documents up to 100 MB and 300 pages;
polls 140-character question with 2–4 options of 30 characters.

## 1. Build note (profile)

```text
<one decision or number from this week, ≤ 120 characters>

Context: <what we are building, one line, naming the AI character and that it is AI>.

What happened: <2–3 short lines with the real numbers>.

What we changed: <the decision, one line>.

What I'd tell another team: <the lesson, one line>.

<a question you will answer today, e.g. "How do you decide when an agent is allowed to post on its own?">
```

Example:

```text
We cut our AI character from 7 posts a day to 4. Follows per 1,000 views went from 0.2 to 1.9.

Context: we run Claudia, an AI creator character. A person approves every post before it goes out.

What happened: the extra posts competed with each other and nobody had time to answer replies.

What we changed: fewer posts, spaced out, and every real question answered within two hours.

What I'd tell another team: measure follows, not likes.

How do you decide how often an agent should post?
```

## 2. Lesson with numbers (profile)

```text
<number> <things> we learned running an AI <character | agent> for <period>:

1. <lesson with a number>
2. <lesson with a number>
3. <lesson with a number>

The one I'd skip if I started again: <one line>.

Full write-up in the document above / in our newsletter (link in comments).
```

Keep it to 3–5 points. If you need more, it is a document post.

## 3. Document post outline (Page or profile, posted in the app)

Design the pages with [carousel-design](../../../create/carousel-design/SKILL.md). Export a PDF, 1080 × 1350 px pages.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Title that promises one outcome: `<How we …> in <n> steps` · brand mark · "AI-assisted, reviewed by <name>" |
| 2 | The problem in one sentence and one number |
| 3–8 | One step per page: a heading, two lines, one image (AI images labelled on the page) |
| 9 | The mistake we made, and the fix |
| 10 | Where to learn more: `<url>` · who runs this: `<team>` · "Not financial advice" if coins appear |

Post text above the document: two lines on why it matters, and a question.

## 4. Poll (profile)

```text
Question (≤ 140 chars): <a real choice your audience makes, e.g. "Would you let an AI agent post for your brand?">
Option A (≤ 30): <Yes, with approval>
Option B (≤ 30): <Only drafts>
Option C (≤ 30): <No>
Option D (≤ 30, optional): <Already do>
Duration: <1 day | 3 days | 1 week | 2 weeks>
Follow-up: share the result and what you learned in a post the day it closes.
```

## 5. Page announcement (brand voice, posted in the app)

```text
<What is new, in one line, ≤ 120 characters>

<Who it is for, and what they can do now, 2 lines.>

<One image of the character or the product, alt text "AI-generated image: …">

<Where to try it: link or "link in comments">

Claudia is an AI character. Posts are AI-assisted and reviewed by our team.
```

If the announcement involves a coin launch, read
[crypto-marketing-compliance](../../crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md) first: facts only, no returns, "Not
financial advice."

## Comment-reply guide (operator, by hand)

- Answer in order: questions, thoughtful disagreement, people sharing their own experience, then thanks.
- Reply in your own words. The agent may suggest points in a private note; you type and send.
- Never paste the same reply twice. Never ask commenters to "comment to get the PDF".
- Don't argue about a coin's price. Point to the education post and leave it.
- Spam and scams: hide, report, block.
