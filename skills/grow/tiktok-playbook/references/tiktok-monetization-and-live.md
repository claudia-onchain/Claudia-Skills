# TikTok monetization and LIVE for AI characters

Read this before choosing personal vs business account, before applying to any TikTok program, and before anyone plans a
LIVE. Checked 2026-10. Eligibility numbers are gates, not income.

## Creator Rewards Program

| Requirement | Value (checked 2026-10) |
|---|---|
| Age | 18+ (19 in South Korea) — the operator is the account holder |
| Followers | 10,000 |
| Views | 100,000 in the last 30 days |
| Account | Personal (not Business), in good standing |
| Country | Eligible markets; as of July 2026: US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Mexico, Brazil |
| Videos | Original, public, longer than 1 minute |
| Typical payout | ~$0.40–1.00 per 1,000 qualified views (third-party estimates) |

AI caveat: 2026 reports (Storrito and others) say TikTok excludes fully AI-generated videos and accounts built entirely on
AI avatars from Creator Rewards, while AI-assisted editing (captions, colour, audio clean-up) stays eligible. TikTok's
in-app program terms are the authority. Plan an AI character's income without Creator Rewards; if the account is eligible,
treat it as a bonus.

## Other TikTok income

| Option | Fit for an AI character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Brand partnerships (TikTok One / direct) | Good, for non-financial brands | Disclosure toggle + #ad; no crypto/finance; benchmarks: micro (10–50k) $500–2,000 per video, mid (50–500k) $2,000–5,000 (2026) |
| Spark Ads (brand boosts your post) | Good | Brand pays to boost the original post with your authorisation code; disclose as paid |
| TikTok Shop affiliate | Limited | Shop LIVE rules ban AI voices; product claims need real testing by a person |
| LIVE gifts | Poor | Operator-hosted only; 18+ |
| Subscriptions | Poor | Needs LIVE or creator eligibility; human-hosted perks |
| Traffic to owned channels | Good | Link in bio to the character page (not to coins or launches) |

## LIVE

Requirements: 18+, usually ~1,000 followers (varies by region), good standing; gifts 18+ only.

Rules that matter for AI:
- TikTok expects real-time interaction. In TikTok Shop LIVEs (US, "Requirements for High-Quality Videos and LIVEs", from 22
  June 2026): no AI-generated voices, no recorded audio or radio-style audio, no still frames, and animated or virtual
  characters may not cover more than 50% of the screen. Avatars elsewhere must be clearly labelled.
- Looping pre-recorded clips as "live" is against LIVE expectations and gets streams ended.

What works: the operator goes live as themselves — "How we make Claudia", answering questions, showing prompts and edits,
with Claudia's clips played on screen and labelled AI. No coin talk, no gifts-for-favours, no "drop a gift and I'll…".

## Personal vs Business account

| | Personal | Business |
|---|---|---|
| Creator Rewards | Eligible | Not eligible |
| Music | Full library for personal, non-commercial use | Commercial Music Library only |
| Ads tools / lead gen | Limited | Full |
| Paid partnerships | Yes (disclosure toggle) | Yes |

For an AI character whose income is brand work, a Business account avoids music-licensing mistakes on paid posts. If
Creator Rewards is the plan and the account might qualify, stay Personal and use original or CML-safe audio on ads.
