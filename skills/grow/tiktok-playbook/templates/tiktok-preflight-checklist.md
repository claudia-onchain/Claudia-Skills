# TikTok preflight — 20 checks before any upload

Copy for each video. Any "no" in sections A or B stops the upload.

Video: {file} · Series/ep: {…} · Slot: {…} · Checked by: {name}

## A. Labels and honesty (stop if any fail)

1. ☐ AI-generated content label on (package `labels.ai: true` → `is_aigc`, or in-app toggle).
2. ☐ "AI character" visible on screen or said in the video.
3. ☐ No real person's likeness or voice; no brand logos used without permission.
4. ☐ The character is clearly an adult in every frame.
5. ☐ Paid, gifted or affiliate? → disclosure toggle on and `#ad` in the first line. If none, nothing implies a deal.
6. ☐ An AI character doesn't claim to have personally used or tested a product.

## B. Money and crypto (stop if any fail)

7. ☐ No coin or token names, tickers, prices, charts with a direction, contract or wallet addresses.
8. ☐ No "buy / ape / pump / moon / profit / returns / passive income / airdrop / presale".
9. ☐ No links (bio or caption) to launches, exchanges or trading apps in this post's call to action.
10. ☐ No earnings claims ("I made $…").
11. ☐ Not a sponsorship for any financial product (crypto, trading, loans, credit, BNPL, investing).

## C. Distribution

12. ☐ Motion in the first frame; hook text on screen within 0.5 s.
13. ☐ Search phrase in caption first line, on-screen text, and the voice line.
14. ☐ Text inside the safe zone (not bottom ~20% or right ~15%).
15. ☐ Captions burned in or auto-captions checked for errors.
16. ☐ Length justified by retention (cut anything that doesn't move the story).
17. ☐ Ends in a loop frame or one question you'll answer — no "follow for part 2" bait.
18. ☐ Original upload (no other app's watermark); not a near-copy of a recent video.

## D. Rights and logistics

19. ☐ Sound licensed for this account type (Business → Commercial Music Library; paid posts never on personal-use trending sounds).
20. ☐ Someone can reply to comments in the first hour after the slot.
