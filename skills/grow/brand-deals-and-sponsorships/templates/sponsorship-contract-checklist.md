# Sponsorship contract checklist

Go through every line before a person signs. Tick, or write what's missing and send it back as a requested change.
This is a practical checklist, not legal advice; for deals over a few thousand dollars, long exclusivity, character
licensing or anything crypto-related, have a lawyer read it.

## Parties
- [ ] The operator's legal person or company is the contracting party (not "Claudia", who is an AI character).
- [ ] Brand's legal entity, registered address and signatory with authority; agency named if one is involved, and
      who pays (brand or agency).

## Deliverables
- [ ] Each deliverable listed: platform, format, length, count, aspect ratio.
- [ ] Posting window with dates and time zone (UTC preferred).
- [ ] What "posted" means (live, public, not deleted for N days — typically 30–90 days minimum live time).
- [ ] Number of concept and draft revision rounds (2 each) and the brand's response deadline (48 h) — silence after
      the deadline counts as approval.
- [ ] Who approves on each side, by name or role.

## Disclosure and honesty
- [ ] Ad disclosure: "Ad"/`#ad` at the start of every caption and story frame, the platform's paid-partnership tool,
      and in video on screen + spoken in the first seconds.
- [ ] AI disclosure: native AI label on every platform plus a text line that Claudia is an AI character.
- [ ] Brand may not request removal or weakening of either disclosure.
- [ ] No first-person experience claims by the AI character; real-person experience only if true and attributed.
- [ ] Brand supplies all product claims in writing and warrants they're substantiated; brand indemnifies for its
      claims.
- [ ] No claims about health, income, returns or prices; nothing that would be a financial promotion.
- [ ] Product shown accurately (real product imagery composited or accurately rendered; no AI enhancement of the
      product's effect).

## Platform compliance
- [ ] The product category is allowed as branded content on every platform listed (TikTok: no financial or crypto).
- [ ] Both sides comply with each platform's branded-content policy and the FTC / ASA-CMA / EU rules where the
      audience is.
- [ ] The operator may refuse or edit anything that would break a platform rule or law, without penalty.

## Rights
- [ ] Organic repost rights: platforms, duration, credit to the account.
- [ ] Paid usage / whitelisting / Spark Ads / partnership ads: platforms, duration, start/end dates, territory, fee.
      Granted only through platform tools; no credentials.
- [ ] No licence to the character: the brand may not generate new images, video or voice of Claudia, edit her
      likeness, or use her content to train or fine-tune models.
- [ ] Raw files, if sold: listed, priced, and still no character rights.
- [ ] Operator keeps ownership of the content and character; the brand gets a licence, not an assignment.

## Exclusivity
- [ ] Category defined narrowly; competitors named.
- [ ] Duration (30 days typical) and start date; priced.
- [ ] Doesn't block existing deals listed in a schedule.

## Money
- [ ] Fee, currency, VAT/sales tax treatment.
- [ ] Schedule: new brand 50% on signing / 50% net 15 after posting; established brand net 30 max.
- [ ] Late payment: interest or a fixed late fee after N days; right to pause remaining deliverables.
- [ ] Kill fee: 50% after concept approval, 100% after production starts / final delivered.
- [ ] Expenses (if any) pre-approved in writing.
- [ ] If paid in stablecoins: asset, chain (USDC on Solana first, others only if named), the receiving address
      written in the contract, confirmation by a second channel, and fiat value recorded on the day. No payment in
      the brand's own token.

## Termination and safety
- [ ] Either side may end the deal for material breach with notice.
- [ ] Morality clause **both ways**: the operator can pull posts if the brand becomes unsafe (scandal, regulatory
      action, scam reports), keeping fees for work done.
- [ ] Takedown: what happens to live posts after termination (archive within 48 h on request).
- [ ] Confidentiality limited to commercial terms; doesn't stop disclosure labels or legal reporting.

## Reporting
- [ ] Reports due day 7 and day 30, with what metrics and native screenshots.
- [ ] Brand shares code redemptions / sales data back if they want ROI judged on it.

## Law and signatures
- [ ] Governing law and venue acceptable to the operator.
- [ ] Signed by a person with authority on both sides; the agent never signs.

## Invoice

Fields every invoice carries:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Invoice number | `CLD-2026-0114` |
| Issue date / due date | 2026-11-07 / 2026-11-22 (net 15) |
| From | Operator's legal name or company, address, tax/VAT number if registered |
| To | Brand's legal entity and address, PO number if they use one |
| Contract reference | "Northpine × Claudia, signed 2026-10-14" |
| Line items | as in the contract, with dates posted and live URLs |
| Subtotal, tax, total, currency | $2,326.00 USD |
| Already paid | 50% deposit $1,163.00 received 2026-10-16 |
| Amount due | $1,163.00 |
| Payment details | bank details, or "USDC (Solana) to <address in contract>; please send a $1 test first" |
| Late fee terms | as in the contract |

Chase on day 1 after due (friendly), day 7 (mention the late-fee clause), day 14 (pause remaining work).
