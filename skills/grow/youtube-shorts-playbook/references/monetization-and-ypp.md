# Shorts monetization and YPP, now and from 1 February 2027 (checked 2026-10)

Read this when planning toward monetization, answering "when can we earn", or budgeting a channel. Income figures are
estimates; never present them to viewers or partners as promises.

## Today (until 31 January 2027)

| Tier | Requirement | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| Fan funding (expanded YPP) | 500 subscribers + 3 public uploads in 90 days + **3 M** valid public Shorts views in 90 days **or** 3,000 watch hours in 12 months | Channel memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat/Stickers, Shopping |
| Full YPP | 1,000 subscribers + **10 M** valid public Shorts views in 90 days **or** 4,000 valid public watch hours in 12 months | Ad revenue (watch page + Shorts feed), everything above |

Shorts views from the Shorts feed count; views of Shorts don't count toward the 4,000 watch hours. Either path is enough.

Also required: 18+ (or a guardian), living in a YPP country, an AdSense account, 2-step verification, no active Community
Guidelines strikes, and a channel review that checks the inauthentic-content and reused-content rules.

## From 1 February 2027 (announced 10 August 2026)

| Change | Detail |
|---|---|
| New applicants | 1,000 subscribers + **20 M** Shorts views in 90 days **or** 8,000 watch hours in 12 months |
| Existing partners | Stay in; must accept updated terms by 31 Jan 2027 |
| Activity requirement (all partners) | 1,000 watch hours in 365 days **or** 1 M Shorts views in 90 days **or** 2 long videos / 5 Shorts per 90 days |
| Shorts revenue floor | A monthly Shorts payout needs ≥ 10 M views in the trailing 90 days |
| Fan funding | Unchanged: 500 subs + 3,000 hours or 3 M Shorts views |

Planning implication: a channel that will reach 10 M Shorts views in a 90-day window before February 2027 should apply
before the change; otherwise plan for 20 M or the long-form path (8,000 hours) and lean on long videos linked from Shorts.

## How Shorts ad revenue works

1. Ads between Shorts in the feed go into a monthly pool per country.
2. Part of the pool pays for music licensing on Shorts that used music.
3. The rest is split by each creator's share of eligible engaged views.
4. Creators keep **45%** of their allocated amount.

Shorts RPMs reported by creators in 2026 are usually a few cents per 1,000 views (roughly $0.01–$0.10, varying by audience
country and niche; vidIQ, aituber 2026 roundups) — Shorts ad revenue is small for most channels. Fan funding, brand deals and long-form ad revenue
usually matter more. See [../../monetization-streams/SKILL.md](../../monetization-streams/SKILL.md).

## Risk factors specific to AI characters

- Channel review can reject AI channels that look templated (see the policies reference). Prepare the production log.
- Finance-advice content from an AI persona is a stated non-monetizable pattern in 2026 commentary; avoid it entirely.
- Undisclosed synthetic media can lead to YPP suspension.
- Music claims on > 1 min Shorts can block a Short in some countries.

## Readiness checklist before applying

- [ ] Thresholds met in the Studio "Earn" tab (not estimated from third-party tools)
- [ ] Every realistic Short has the synthetic disclosure on
- [ ] No reused content from other channels; no watermarks
- [ ] At least 3 distinct series; no runs of near-identical uploads
- [ ] No finance advice, coin picks, giveaways or return claims anywhere on the channel (including old uploads and community posts)
- [ ] Channel About says the character is AI and who runs it
- [ ] Production log covering the last 30 uploads
