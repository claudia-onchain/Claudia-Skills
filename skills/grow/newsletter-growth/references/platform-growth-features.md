# Newsletter platforms: the growth features that matter

Read this in step 2 when choosing or auditing a platform, and in steps 5–6 before setting up referrals or
recommendations. Checked 2026-10 on each platform's own pricing and help pages. Plans and names change often (beehiiv
renamed Boosts in 2026); re-check before you commit.

## beehiiv

| Feature | What it does | Plan (checked 2026-10) |
|---|---|---|
| $0 plan | Up to 2,500 subscribers | — |
| Paid plans | Lite and Pro (Enterprise custom); limits rise with plan and list size | see beehiiv.com/pricing |
| Referral Program | Readers get a personal link; milestones unlock rewards you define | all paid plans |
| Recommendations | You recommend other newsletters at sign-up and they recommend you | all plans |
| Paid recommendations (formerly Boosts) | You pay per confirmed subscriber another newsletter sends you, or earn by recommending others | Lite and Pro |
| Ad Network | Sponsored placements matched to your newsletter | paid plans actively sending on beehiiv (beehiiv's own pages conflict on which plans; check the Ad Network FAQ) |
| Magic links | One-click subscribe link that pre-fills the reader's address; works only inside emails sent from the platform you choose, not on websites | — |

Email Boosts and Direct Links were deprecated on 10 April 2026.

## Substack

| Feature | What it does | Notes (checked 2026-10) |
|---|---|---|
| Recommendations | Recommend other publications in your sign-up flow and on your site; they can recommend you | Substack calls it its main growth engine |
| Notes | Public short-form posts in the Substack Home feed; not emailed | can't be turned off |
| Subscriber referrals | Rewards at 2, 10 and 25 referrals by default (comp paid subscriptions on paid publications); optional public leaderboard | not on private publications |
| Gift subscriptions | Paid subscribers can gift up to 5 | — |
| Fees | 10% of paid subscription revenue, plus Stripe fees | — |
| Imports | Only people "who explicitly opted-in to receive emails from your specific publication"; purchased, scraped or harvested addresses and imported social graphs are banned; Substack can deny an import and ask for the opt-in URL | substack.com/content |

## Kit (formerly ConvertKit)

| Feature | What it does | Plan (checked 2026-10) |
|---|---|---|
| $0 plan | Up to 10,000 subscribers | — |
| Creator Network / Recommendations | Creators find and recommend each other at sign-up | Recommendations appear on all plans (Kit's comparison table didn't fully render; confirm in your account) |
| Paid Recommendations | Pay or earn per subscriber | not on the $0 plan |
| Newsletter referral system (SparkLoop) | Reader referral program | Pro |
| Newsletter sponsorships | Sponsor marketplace | plan and eligibility not confirmed; check in the account |

## Picking one

| If you… | Lean towards |
|---|---|
| Want a built-in social feed and readers who discover through recommendations | Substack |
| Want referral programs, paid recommendations and an ad network on one dashboard | beehiiv |
| Sell products, courses or digital downloads alongside the newsletter, with complex automations | Kit |

Moving later is possible (all three import and export subscribers), but referral counts, recommendation ties and
archive URLs don't move cleanly. Pick for the next 12 months.
