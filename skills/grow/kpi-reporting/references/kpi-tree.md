# KPI tree and targets

Read this in Step 1 when setting up reporting, and at each monthly report when targets are reset.

## Choosing the north star

One metric, chosen by what the account is for. It must be (a) something people do, not something the platform shows,
(b) countable every week, (c) hard to fake.

| Account goal | North star | Why not the obvious one |
|---|---|---|
| Grow a platform (Claudia → useclaudia.xyz) | Weekly sign-ups or activations via UTM | Followers don't sign up |
| Sponsorship income | Median views per post on the 2 main platforms | Brands price per post, from medians |
| Community | Weekly active members (Telegram/Discord: people who posted or reacted) | Member count includes the departed |
| Education (Juno) | Saves + sends per 1k views | Saves and sends mean "useful enough to keep or share" |
| Launch (time-boxed) | Sign-ups or waitlist joins in the launch window | Impressions spike and vanish |

Avoid: followers, total impressions, total likes as a north star. Keep them as context lines.

## Drivers and inputs

Every driver must be something a week of work can move, and every input something the operator or agent controls.

```text
North star
├── Reach that fits        median views/post · X impressions/post · non-follower share of views
│     inputs: posts/week per platform · hooks tested · Trial Reels · posting window
├── Depth                  ER by reach · sends per reach · saves per 1k views · completion · viewed-vs-swiped
│     inputs: length · first 2 seconds · series format · captions/on-screen text
├── Follow-through         follows per 1k views · profile visits per 1k views · returning viewers
│     inputs: bio line · pinned posts · series promise ("part 2 tomorrow")
├── Conversation           replies by the account to real people · reply-to-follow rate
│     inputs: reply windows per day · questions in captions (see engagement-and-replies)
└── Action                 CTR · conversion (sign-ups ÷ clicks) · cost per sign-up
      inputs: link placement · CTA wording · landing page speed · offer
```

## Targets from your own history

1. Take the last 4 complete weeks. For each driver, compute the weekly median.
2. Target band for the next 4 weeks: floor = last 4-week median; stretch = median × 1.15 (× 1.25 for accounts under
   5k followers where small numbers move fast).
3. Never set a target above the best week of the last 8 weeks unless a deliberate change (new format, new platform)
   justifies it, and write that change next to the target.
4. Reset monthly. A missed target is information, not a failure; write the reason.

Example (Claudia, TikTok, 4 weeks to 2026-09-28): weekly median views/post 10.9k, 11.4k, 12.6k, 12.1k → median 11.75k.
Target October: floor 11.75k, stretch 13.5k.

## Leading vs lagging

- Leading (move first, check weekly): completion, viewed-vs-swiped, sends per reach, follows per 1k views.
- Lagging (move later, check monthly): follower count, sign-ups, sponsorship enquiries, Creator Rewards or YPP
  eligibility progress.

If leading metrics improve for 3 weeks and lagging don't move in 6, the problem is downstream (bio, landing page, offer),
not the content.

## Platform eligibility thresholds as milestone KPIs (checked 2026-10)

Track these as milestones, not weekly targets:

- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days or 4,000 watch hours in 12 months; new
  applicants from 1 Feb 2027 need 20M Shorts views or 8,000 hours. Fan funding: 500 subscribers + 3M Shorts views in
  90 days or 3,000 hours.
- TikTok Creator Rewards: 18+, 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, eligible country, videos ≥ 1 minute
  (fully AI-generated content reportedly ineligible; check in-app terms).
- X Original Content Rewards: Premium, 18+, 500 verified followers, 500,000 verified Home Timeline impressions in
  90 days (replies excluded).
- Instagram Trial Reels: professional account, public, follower minimum varies (200–1,000 reported).
- Telegram Ad Platform revenue share: public channels with 1,000+ subscribers.
