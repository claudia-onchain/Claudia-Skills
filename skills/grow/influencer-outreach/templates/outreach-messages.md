# Creator outreach messages

One message, one person. Replace every `<…>` with something specific; if you can't name a real post of theirs that you
watched, don't send. A person sends each message or approves it first. At most 10 new messages a day, one follow-up
after 5–7 days, then stop. Keep first messages under 130 words; no links in a first DM.

---

## 1. First message, email

Subject: `<Brand>: paid idea for your <series or format name>`

```text
Hi <first name>,

I'm <your name> at <brand>. We make <product in one plain line>. <If relevant: Our spokesperson, <character>, is an AI
character, clearly labelled as AI on every post.>

Your <specific post, e.g. "desk-tour video from 3 October"> is why I'm writing: <one specific reason, e.g. "the bit where
you rebuilt your posting week got more questions in the comments than the gear did">.

Idea: <one deliverable, e.g. "one 45-60 s TikTok where you set up a posting week with our template">. <Offer: "We'd pay
<amount> plus access to <product>" or "We'd like to send you <product>, no obligation to post">. Window: <dates>.

If it's a fit I'll send a one-page brief. If not, no problem at all.

<name>, <role>, <brand>
```

## 2. First message, business DM (Instagram / TikTok / X)

```text
Hi <name>, <your name> from <brand> (<product in 5 words>). Your <specific post> made me think of a paid idea for your
<series/format>: <one-line concept>, <window>. Is email the best place to send a short brief? No worries if not.
```

## 3. Gifted offer (no obligation)

```text
We'd like to send you <product / access> to keep, with no obligation to post. If you do post about it, please start with
"Ad" and say <brand> sent it, and switch on <platform tool>. Your honest view is fine, including if it's not for you.
```

## 4. Paid offer (after they reply)

```text
Thanks, <name>. Here's what we'd propose:
- Deliverable: <format, length, platform>, posted <window>.
- Fee: <amount> <currency>, paid <trigger, e.g. "within 15 days of posting">.
- Draft review: one round, we reply within 48 h.
- Disclosure: "Ad" at the start, <platform tool> on<, and a line that <character> is AI-generated>.
- Usage: organic only. If we want to run it as an ad, that's a separate fee and an end date.
The full brief is attached. Happy to adjust the format to what works for your audience.
```

## 5. When they ask "what's your budget?"

```text
For <deliverable> we've set <range>. We price from median views over the last 30 days, so if you can share a screenshot
of your analytics we'll come back with a firm number within a day.
```

## 6. Follow-up (once, after 5–7 days)

```text
Hi <name>, just checking this reached you. Happy to drop it if the timing's wrong.
```

## 7. Closing politely (not a fit after checks)

```text
Thanks for sending the numbers, <name>. This round is aimed at a <country / audience> audience, so we'll pass for now.
We'll keep your details for future campaigns if that's OK.
```

## Never send

- The same text to many people with only the name changed.
- Anything asking them to skip `#ad`, hide that the product or character is AI, or claim results they didn't have.
- Offers involving coins, token allocations or "early access" to a token.
- Messages from an automated DM tool.
