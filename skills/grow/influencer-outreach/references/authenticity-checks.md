# Authenticity checks: is this audience real?

Read this in step 4. Everything here uses public profiles plus the creator's own analytics screenshots. No scraping, no
third-party "audit" tools that log in as the creator. A check takes about 10 minutes per creator; fill one row of
`templates/creator-scorecard.csv` as you go.

## Why it matters

- Under the FTC's Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule, selling or buying fake indicators of social media influence
  (followers, views, likes made by bots or hijacked accounts) to misrepresent influence for a commercial purpose is
  banned (16 CFR 465.8, in force since 21 October 2024; the FTC sent warning letters under the rule in December 2025,
  checked 2026-10). Hiring a creator who has some fake followers isn't itself a breach, but paying for an audience that
  doesn't exist is wasted budget and a reputational risk.
- Platforms remove fake engagement and can cut reach for accounts that use it, so the views you paid for may vanish.

## The six checks

### 1. Views vs followers (the fastest signal)

Take the **median** views of the last 10 posts on the platform you'll pay for (median, so one viral post doesn't hide
the rest).

| Platform | Look harder if median views are… |
|---|---|
| TikTok | under ~2% of followers on every post |
| Instagram Reels | under ~2% of followers on every post |
| YouTube Shorts | under ~1% of subscribers, or likes higher than a normal share of views |
| X | impressions under ~1% of followers |

These are working thresholds, not platform rules. Big old accounts drift low naturally; a young account at 300k
followers and 800 views a post is the pattern to drop.

### 2. Growth shape

Ask for (or look at) the follower graph for 90 days. Real growth is steady with bumps after specific viral posts.
Warnings: a jump of thousands in a day with no post that explains it; a staircase of identical jumps; a big drop
(platforms purging fakes).

### 3. Comment quality

Read 30 comments across three recent posts.

| Real | Warning |
|---|---|
| Specific questions about the content | "Nice pic" plus a fire emoji, "Great content!" over and over |
| The creator replies, threads develop | The same 10–30 accounts within minutes on every post (engagement pod) |
| Disagreement, jokes, references to earlier posts | Comments that don't match the post's language or topic |
| Commenters have their own posts and history | Commenters with no photo, no posts, random-digit handles |

### 4. Audience data from the source

Ask for screenshots from native analytics, dated within 7 days:

- Top 5 countries and cities, age bands, gender split.
- Median or average views per post for 30 days.
- Share of views from followers vs non-followers (TikTok, Instagram).

Check that the countries match your market and the age bands are adults. A creator whose audience is mostly under 18
is a no for anything with age limits and a caution for everything else.

### 5. Past sponsored posts

Scroll back 3–6 months. Every post with a brand, discount code or affiliate link should show "Ad"/`#ad` or the
platform's paid-partnership label. Undisclosed ads now mean undisclosed ads on your campaign later.

### 6. Red-flag content

Drop on any of: coin shilling or return promises, gambling promotion, harassment or pile-ons, impersonation, "backup
account of …" (ban evasion), AI content presented as real people or events without a label.

## Scoring

| Check | Pass | Warn | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Views vs followers | in range | borderline | far below on 8+ of 10 posts |
| Growth shape | steady | one unexplained jump | repeated unexplained jumps |
| Comments | mostly real | mixed | pod or bot pattern |
| Audience data | shared, matches | shared, partial match | refused, or mismatch |
| Past ads | all disclosed | one miss long ago | pattern of misses |
| Red-flag content | none | — | any |

Any **fail** = drop. Two or more **warn** = only gifted, never paid, until a second look in 30 days.

## Asking for data without insulting anyone

```text
Before we talk budget, could you share a screenshot of your last 30 days in <platform> analytics (views, top countries,
age bands)? We ask everyone the same thing so we can match creators to the right campaign.
```
