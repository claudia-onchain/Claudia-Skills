# Example: a creator round for a Claudia Local template

Claudia's team is launching a new "Content creator" template for Claudia Local: a persona, skills and starter tasks
that help a solo creator plan and draft a posting week. They want six creators to show it in use. Claudia herself is
the brand's AI spokesperson; every creator post must say so. Budget: $3,200 in fees and $600 reserved for usage.
No gifting: Claudia Local is open source, so the fee is the only payment, and it is what the disclosure covers.

All creators below are fictional.

## Step 1: profile

- Promoting: the Content creator template · for: solo creators aged 22–40 who post 3+ times a week · action: install
  Claudia Local and load the template (tracked by the landing page visit and the docs page).
- Platforms: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels.
- Styles: desk tours, "how I plan my week", tool tutorials.
- Exclusions: coin content of any kind (this campaign has nothing to do with $CLAUDIA, and the brief says so).

## Steps 2–4: long list to shortlist

From 38 candidates (found by searching "content calendar" and "creator workflow" on each platform and from people who had
already posted about Claudia Local), 15 scored ≥ 65 on fit. The authenticity check cut it further:

| Creator | Platform | Followers | Median views (10) | Views % | Comments | Data shared | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| @plannedby.rae | TikTok | 41k | 12,400 | 30% | real questions | yes, US/UK/CA 25–34 | paid |
| @deskhours | YouTube Shorts | 88k | 21,000 | 24% | real | yes | paid |
| @notesbynour | Instagram | 23k | 6,100 | 27% | real, replies to all | yes | paid |
| @thecalmcreator | TikTok | 410k | 2,300 | 0.6% | same 20 accounts on every post | refused | drop |
| @workflow.wes | TikTok | 15k | 5,800 | 39% | real | yes, 60% PH/IN | gifted only (audience mismatch for the US launch) |
| @studio_ines | Instagram | 64k | 9,900 | 15% | mixed | yes | paid |
| @growth.grind.daily | Instagram | 120k | 3,100 | 2.6% | generic | yes | drop: two undisclosed affiliate posts last month |

`@thecalmcreator` had the largest following and would have cost the most. Median views at 0.6% of followers, a comment
pod and refused analytics made the decision easy.

## Steps 5–6: offers and messages

Four paid offers at $500–900 each, priced from median views and production effort (about $45–80 per 1,000 median
views; tutorials take longer to make than talking-head clips), plus one fee + bonus
($300 + $5 per tracked install after the first 40). Messages were written one by one; example sent to @plannedby.rae by
email:

```text
Subject: Claudia: paid idea for your Sunday planning videos

Hi Rae,

I'm Sam at Claudia. We make Claudia Local, an open-source agent and studio that runs on your own computer. Our
spokesperson, Claudia, is an AI character, clearly labelled as AI on every post.

Your Sunday planning video from 28 September is why I'm writing: half the comments asked how you batch your captions.

Idea: one 45-60 s TikTok where you plan next week with our new Content creator template, showing what it drafts and
what you change. We'd pay $750. Window: 20-31 October.

If it's a fit I'll send a one-page brief. If not, no problem at all.

Sam, partnerships, Claudia
```

Five of six replied within a week; one follow-up went to the sixth, who declined.

## Step 7: the brief (key lines)

- Claims allowed: "runs on your own computer"; "drafts wait for your approval before anything posts"; "you bring your
  own model keys, or run a local model". Source: the Claudia Local README.
- Don't say: "saves you hours" (no evidence), anything about $CLAUDIA or trading, or that Claudia "uses" anything.
- Disclosure: `Ad · Paid partnership with Claudia. Claudia is an AI character; the template drafts are AI-generated.`
  plus TikTok "Disclose commercial content → Branded content" / Instagram "Paid partnership" / YouTube paid-promotion
  checkbox.
- Usage: organic only; partnership-ad rights for @plannedby.rae added later at $250 for 30 days.

Takeaways written into the tracker: tutorial-style creators with real comment threads beat reach; the fee + bonus deal
was the cheapest per install; the missing label on @notesbynour's post was caught by the 24-hour check and fixed the
same morning. Next round: rehire three, add two creators like @workflow.wes in the US.
