# Rebuilding trust after an incident (30 days)

Trust comes back from kept promises and visible change, not from volume. This plan assumes the post-mortem is done.

## Principles

1. **Do what the statement said, on the date it said.** Every promise in a holding statement is a test.
2. **Show the fix working.** "We added a check" is a claim; a post that shows the check catching something is proof.
3. **Be the same character.** Claudia's voice (warm, wry, cosy-city) comes back gradually; no forced cheerfulness the day
   after, no grovelling for a month either.
4. **Be honest about what she is.** An AI character run by a small team. Incidents often start with people feeling
   misled about who is speaking; every statement and every correction should make that clearer, not blurrier.
5. **No shortcuts.** No bought followers, no giveaways to change the subject, no engagement pods, no new account. These
   are against platform rules and people notice.

## Day-by-day

| When | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0–1 | Holding statement, correction, answer the top 3 questions once each | Promotions, coin posts, jokes about the incident |
| Day 2–3 | Publish the public post-mortem summary (what happened, impact, 3 fixes, dates) | Defensive threads, "haters" talk |
| Day 3–7 | Half cadence, everything human-approved; character and lifestyle content; one "how our approvals work" post | Sponsored posts (move them; tell sponsors why) |
| Day 7 | Status update: which fixes are live | Declaring it "over" |
| Day 8–21 | Normal cadence returns; coin content only if the incident wasn't financial, else after day 14 with AMBER approval | Big launches |
| Day 30 | Close the incident: final note in the log, check every action item is done | Re-litigating it publicly |

## Public post-mortem summary (template text)

```text
What happened on [date]: [one sentence]. It was live for [N] minutes and reached about [N] people.
Why: [one or two sentences, no blame on individuals, no "the AI did it"].
What we changed: 1) [fix, live since date] 2) [fix] 3) [fix].
I'm an AI character; a small team runs the platform and approves what I post. That approval missed this one. It's
tighter now. Questions → [official channel].
```

## Metrics that say it is working

Track these daily for 30 days against the 30 days before the incident (see
[kpi-reporting](../../kpi-reporting/SKILL.md)):

| Metric | Healthy recovery |
|---|---|
| Negative-keyword share of replies (scam, rug, fake, liar) | Back to baseline within 14 days |
| Unfollows per day | Spike ends within 72 h; net follower change positive by day 21 |
| Engagement rate on normal posts | Within 80% of baseline by day 14 |
| Repeat questions about the incident | Falling week over week |
| Impersonator reports filed vs taken down | Every report resolved or escalated |
| Sponsor pipeline | No cancellations after day 14; sponsors told the facts directly |

If a metric doesn't recover, look for an unkept promise or an unanswered question before changing the content plan.

## When to talk about it again

Only when it helps someone: a safety guide ("how to spot a fake Claudia account"), a note when the last action item
ships, or a yearly transparency post. Not as content for its own sake.
