# Platform reporting paths (checked 2026-10)

Where to report impersonation, scams, hacked accounts and deepfakes. Forms change; if a link fails, start from the
platform's help centre and search "impersonation". Claudia is a brand/character, so report as the **brand's authorised
representative** (the team that operates her), with proof of control of the official accounts and website.

Before filing, have ready: the impostor's profile URL and handle, screenshots with UTC times, the official account URLs,
a short statement ("This account impersonates the AI character Claudia, operated by [team], official accounts
listed at useclaudia.xyz"), and for some forms a government ID of the representative or business documents.

## Off-platform

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Phishing or fake "Claudia" websites | Google Safe Browsing: https://safebrowsing.google.com/safebrowsing/report_phish/ · the domain registrar's abuse contact (find it with a WHOIS/RDAP lookup) · hosting provider abuse contact |
| Fake app listings | App Store / Google Play "report a concern" on the listing |
| Look-alike tokens on Solana | Report the mint to the wallet blocklists the community uses (for example Phantom's public blocklist process) and to the launchpad where it was created; post a warning with the real mint copied from the official page |
| Copyright (derived images/videos of Claudia) | The platform's copyright form (DMCA notice in the US); keep originals with Content Credentials as proof |
| Fraud with victims (US) | FBI IC3: https://www.ic3.gov · FTC: https://reportfraud.ftc.gov |
| Fraud with victims (UK) | Report Fraud (replaced Action Fraud on 4 Dec 2025; England, Wales, NI; Scotland: Police Scotland 101): https://www.reportfraud.police.uk · FCA ScamSmart warning list submissions: https://www.fca.org.uk/scamsmart |
| Fraud with victims (EU) | The national police cybercrime unit; the national financial regulator for investment scams |

## Tips that speed things up

- File one report per impostor per platform, then add evidence to the same ticket rather than opening new ones.
- Ask followers to report too, once, with the exact steps; don't ask them to harass the impostor.
- Keep a running list of past impostor handles; platforms act faster on repeat patterns.
- Verification badges and linked official accounts (X's automated label linked to the managing account, consistent
  links from useclaudia.xyz to each profile and back) make impersonation reports easier to win.
