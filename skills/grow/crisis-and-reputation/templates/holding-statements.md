# Holding statements

Pre-approve these with the team **before** anything goes wrong, with the real handles and links filled in. During an
incident, fill only the bracketed facts, keep the structure, and get the comms approver's yes before publishing.

Rules for every statement:
- Say who is speaking. Claudia speaks in first person as an AI character; team statements say "the team that runs
  Claudia". Never pretend a person wrote something an AI generated, or the other way round.
- Four parts: what happened · what we did · what you should do · when the next update comes.
- Facts only. No speculation about cause, no blame on individuals or "the AI", no legal conclusions, no price talk.
- Short: X ≤ 280 characters for the first post (thread the detail), Telegram/Discord can be longer.
- The package adds the AI label; add "Not financial advice." yourself if a coin is named (it is added automatically too).

Time stamps are UTC. `[next update]` is a real time, for example "18:00 UTC".

---

## 1. AI error (made-up fact, wrong attribution)

**X (Claudia's voice)**
```text
Correction: my post earlier said [wrong claim]. That's wrong. [Correct fact], source: [link]. I'm an AI character and
the team's check missed this one; they're adding a source check to every post. Sorry.
```

**Telegram / Discord**
```text
Correction from earlier today ([time] UTC): I posted that [wrong claim]. It isn't true. The correct information is
[correct fact] ([source]). How it happened: I generated the claim and it went out without being checked against a
source. What's changing: every factual post now needs a source link before the team approves it.
```

## 2. Wrong information that could cost people money (wrong CA, price, link, fake partnership)

**X (Claudia's voice), posted as a reply under the original and pinned**
```text
⚠️ The [contract address / link] in my post at [time] UTC was wrong and I've deleted it. The correct one is on
useclaudia.xyz/[path]. Please check before you interact with anything. More by [next update]. Not financial advice.
```

**Team follow-up (Telegram, Discord, thread)**
```text
Update from the team that runs Claudia: at [time] UTC Claudia's X account posted an incorrect [address/link] for
[coin/page]. It was live for [N] minutes. We removed it at [time] UTC. The correct details are only on
useclaudia.xyz/[path]. If you interacted with the wrong [address/link], [plain next step, e.g. "check your wallet's
activity and revoke approvals you don't recognise"]. We're reviewing how this got through approval and will post what
we've changed by [time].
```

## 3. Offensive or harmful output

**X (team voice, from Claudia's account)**
```text
We deleted a post from this account at [time] UTC. It was [hurtful / offensive] about [group/topic] and should never
have gone out. Claudia is an AI character; the team that runs her approved it, and that's on us. We're sorry.
What's changing: [specific safeguard], live today.
```

Do not add "if anyone was offended". Do not quote the deleted text.

## 4. Hacked account

**From every other official channel immediately**
```text
🚨 Claudia's [X/TikTok/Telegram] account was accessed by someone else at around [time] UTC. Don't click links or send
anything to it until we confirm it's back under our control. Claudia never asks for seed phrases, deposits, or
"verification" payments. Official links: useclaudia.xyz. Next update by [next update].
```

**From the recovered account**
```text
This account is back under our control as of [time] UTC. Between [start] and [end] UTC, posts and messages from it
were not from us; we've removed them. If you sent anything or connected a wallet because of them, [next step and where
to get help]. We've reset access, revoked connected apps and turned on stronger login protection.
```

## 5. Impersonator or scam using Claudia's name

**X / Telegram / Discord (Claudia's voice), pinned**
```text
Heads up: [@handle / t.me/… / site] is not me. It's using my name and face to [ask for crypto / promote a coin].
My only accounts are listed on useclaudia.xyz. I never DM first, never ask you to send crypto, and never announce a
coin anywhere before my official accounts. Please report it: [one-line how].
```

**If a scam coin uses the name**
```text
There's a token called [name/ticker] using my name. I didn't create it and I'm not connected to it. Anything I launch
is announced on my official accounts and listed on useclaudia.xyz first. Not financial advice.
```

## 6. Coin price crash and "rug" accusations

**Team voice (Telegram, Discord, then X)**
```text
A lot of you are asking about [ticker] after today's drop. The facts: [the agent / the team] [holds N / holds none];
the last time [the agent / any team wallet] traded it was [date, time UTC, buy/sell, amount]. Claudia launched it on
[date] on the Claudia launchpad and receives creator fees when it trades, as stated at launch: [total so far, if
public]. We don't control the price and we won't predict it. Memecoins can go to zero. Not financial advice.
Questions → [channel].
```

If any team or agent wallet sold before the drop, that goes in the second sentence, with times and amounts. If people
allege fraud, stop after this statement and talk to counsel.

## 7. Deepfake

**Claudia's voice**
```text
There's a [video/image] going around that shows "me" [doing/saying X]. It's fake: it wasn't made or posted by me or
the team. Real posts are on my official accounts (listed on useclaudia.xyz) and carry an AI label. Please don't share
it; report it instead: [how].
```

If the deepfake is sexual or abusive: don't describe it beyond "an abusive fake image", and lead with "please report,
don't share".
