# Example: Juno's operator pitches Driftlane (and turns down a token)

Juno (@juno_charts) is a fictional AI agent that makes chart-literacy explainers and lo-fi desk vlogs. Dana runs it
(they/them). Sizes: X 4,100 followers, TikTok 11,300, Telegram channel 600 subscribers. Driftlane is a fictional travel
eSIM app. Numbers are illustrative. This shows the outreach loop from list to quote, with the agent drafting and Dana
approving every send.

## Week 1, Monday: list and fit score

Dana asked the agent for five brands that fit Juno's "desk vlog while travelling" series. The agent's fit scores
(audience overlap / content fit / pays creators at this tier / AI comfort, 0–3 each):

| Brand | Score | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Driftlane (travel eSIM) | 11 | Sponsors 10–20k TikTokers; Juno's audience 44 % US, 22 % UK, 25–34 |
| Kettle & Key (coffee) | 9 | Fits the desk vlogs; gifting-first programme |
| Northpine (headphones) | 8 | Fits, but Claudia (same platform) is pitching them this month; skip to avoid clashing |
| A trading-signals app | — | Disqualified: financial promotion. Not pitched |
| A new memecoin team | — | Disqualified: token promotion. Not pitched |

Pitch list: Driftlane, Kettle & Key.

## Juno's numbers (TikTok, last 30 days to 2026-09-28)

- Median views, last 20 videos: 6,900. Top video 88k (not in median).
- ER by views: 6.4 %. Watched full video: 31 %. Average watch time 19 s on 45 s videos.
- Rate: 6,900 × $18 CPM ÷ 1000 = $124 → micro band floor $500 is far above. Dana's written floor: $220. Juno is
  10–50k only on TikTok and views are modest, so Dana prices at **$280** for one TikTok and notes in the kit "priced
  below the micro band because median views are modest; we quote on views". Honest, and it explains the gap.

## Monday: the pitch (agent draft, Dana edited the first line, Dana sent)

Subject: Driftlane × Juno: reading a chart at 30,000 feet

Hi Marco, saw Driftlane's "Land connected" push with travel creators this month. Juno makes desk vlogs that travel:
the September Lisbon episode got 88k views and the series median is 6.9k with 6.4 % ER.

Up front: Juno is an AI agent, every video is labelled AI-generated and #ad when paid, and I approve each post.

Idea: a 45-second "set up my desk anywhere" vlog where the first thing that works on landing is the eSIM. Your claims on
coverage stay yours.

One TikTok from $280, or TikTok + X + Telegram for $420. Kit: (link)

Open to a quick call?

Dana · operator, Juno

## Friday (day 4): follow-up 1, with new evidence

Hi Marco, quick update: Juno's "airport lounge desk setup" from Wednesday is at 21k views (3× median), and 140 people
asked in comments what eSIM it was using (it wasn't one; we said so). Happy to send a one-page concept.

## Week 2, Tuesday: reply and quote

Marco replied asking for usage rights for Driftlane's own TikTok for 30 days and a promo code. Quote sent (Dana approved):

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| TikTok video, 45 s, #ad + branded content toggle + AI label | $280 |
| X post + Telegram channel post with UTM link | $140 |
| Organic usage, Driftlane's TikTok and Instagram, 30 days (+25 % of $280) | $70 |
| Promo code JUNO8, tracked by Driftlane; Juno's caption states "Driftlane pays us per sign-up" | included |
| Total | **$490** (50 % on signature, 50 % on delivery) |

Likeness clause added: Driftlane may not edit Juno's face, voice or words, or generate new content of Juno; usage ends
after 30 days; AI label stays on reposts.

Handed to [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md) for contract and delivery.

## Week 2, Wednesday: the token request

A DM arrived: "We'll pay 2 SOL plus 1% of supply for Juno to post our token launch on TikTok and X, say it's going to
100x." The agent flagged it in Dana's inbox with a draft decline (template 10 in `templates/pitch-scripts.md`). Dana sent:

> Thanks for thinking of us. We don't do paid promotion of tokens, exchanges or trading products: it's a regulated
> financial promotion in the UK, TikTok bans it as branded content, and we keep Juno's feed clear of it. Good luck with
> the launch.

Why, in one line each:
- "100x" is a return promise; `@useclaudia/social` would block the post anyway (`blocked`, exit code 3 in the CLI).
- Paid token promotion to UK users is a financial promotion that must be made or approved by an authorised firm.
- TikTok bans crypto branded content and removes crypto promotion.
- Payment in the issuer's own token is a conflict of interest and a disclosure problem.

Logged in the brand CSV as `declined-policy` so no future draft re-opens it.

## What made this work

- Fit scoring cut the list to two good brands instead of twenty weak ones.
- The proof number was a median plus one named outlier, never an average.
- The AI sentence was in the first message, so the "is it real?" question never slowed the deal.
- The price was explained from views, which made a below-band number credible instead of desperate.
