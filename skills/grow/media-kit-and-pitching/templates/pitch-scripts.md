# Pitch scripts

Fill every `{{…}}`. Each message is written for one named person and sent by a person from a real inbox. Keep cold
pitches to 90–140 words. Keep the AI sentence in every first message.

## 1. Cold email to a partnerships inbox

Subject: {{product}} × {{character}}: {{one_line_idea}}

Hi {{first_name}},

{{specific_observation_about_their_campaign_or_launch}}. I run {{character}}, an AI creator on {{platforms}}:
{{proof_number}} (last 30 days, platform analytics).

Full disclosure up front: {{character}} is AI-generated, every post is labelled as AI and as #ad when paid, and a
person approves each sponsored post.

Idea: {{format}} on {{platform}}, {{scene_description}}, live in {{timing}}. Your claims stay yours; she doesn't pretend
to have used the product.

Packages start at {{from_price}}. Media kit: {{kit_link}}

Worth a 15-minute call next week?

{{operator_name}} · {{operator_role}}, {{operator_company}}

## 2. Warm intro request (to a mutual contact)

Hi {{mutual_name}}, would you be comfortable introducing me to {{target_name}} at {{brand}}? One line on why: we're
pitching a {{format}} with {{character}} (AI creator, {{proof_number}}) around {{their_launch}}. Here's a forwardable
two-liner: "{{operator_name}} runs {{character}}, a labelled AI creator with {{proof_number}}. They have an idea for
{{brand}}'s {{product}} and asked for an intro." No pressure if not.

## 3. DM to a brand account that lists DMs as a contact route

Hi {{brand}} team, {{operator_name}} here, operator of {{character}} (AI creator, labelled on every post). Your
{{campaign}} caught our eye. We have a {{format}} idea for {{product}}, {{proof_number}} median. Who's the best person
for creator partnerships? Happy to send a one-page concept to an email instead.

## 4. Follow-up 1 (day 4–5): add evidence

Subject: Re: {{product}} × {{character}}

Hi {{first_name}}, one update since my note: {{new_result_or_seasonal_hook}}. The {{format}} idea still fits
{{their_timing}}. Shall I send the one-page concept?

## 5. Follow-up 2 (day 10–12): smaller ask

Hi {{first_name}}, if a full package is too much this quarter, a single {{smaller_format}} from {{smaller_price}} works
as a test, with UTM tracking so you can judge it on clicks. Interested?

## 6. Close the loop (day 21)

Hi {{first_name}}, I'll stop here so I'm not crowding your inbox. The kit stays current at {{kit_link}} if timing
changes. Thanks for reading.

## 7. Reply to "send your rates"

Hi {{first_name}}, thanks! Kit attached ({{kit_link}}). Two options that usually fit a launch like yours:
- {{option_a}}: {{price_a}}
- {{option_b}}: {{price_b}}
Usage rights and exclusivity are quoted separately. What's the goal (awareness, sign-ups, sales) and the go-live window?
Then I can tighten the quote within 2 business days.

## 8. Reply to "Is she real?"

She's an AI character. Every image, video and voice of {{character}} is generated, and every post carries the platform's
AI label. {{operator_company}} operates the account and a person approves every sponsored post. Happy to jump on a call
with you to walk through how we make and review content.

## 9. Reply to "Can we skip the #ad / AI label?"

We can't, sorry. Paid posts have to be labelled as ads in the US (FTC), the UK (CMA/ASA) and the EU, and the platforms
require the AI label on realistic generated people. Unlabelled posts get taken down, and the fines can land on both of us.
The label rarely hurts results as much as a removal would. Happy to make the creative strong enough that it doesn't matter.

## 10. Reply to a crypto or token promotion request

Thanks for thinking of us. We don't do paid promotion of tokens, exchanges or trading products: it's a regulated
financial promotion in the UK, TikTok bans it as branded content, and we keep {{character}}'s feed clear of it. Good luck
with the launch.
