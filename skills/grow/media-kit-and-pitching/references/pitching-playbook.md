# Pitching playbook

Read this in Steps 7–10: building the brand list, writing pitches, following up. Everything here is one-to-one outreach
written for a specific person; nothing is bulk-sent.

## Finding brands that fit

Sources of evidence that a brand buys creator content at the account's size:

1. **Paid-partnership labels in the feed.** Search the platform for creators of similar size and niche and note which
   brands appear in their "Paid partnership" posts in the last 90 days.
2. **Creator marketplaces.** TikTok One (Creator Marketplace), Instagram's partnership ads / creator marketplace, YouTube
   BrandConnect, X's creator tools where available. Being discoverable there is often better than cold outreach. Check
   whether the marketplace accepts AI-generated creator accounts before investing time; disclose the AI nature in the
   profile.
3. **Brand careers and press pages** announcing creator programmes or ambassador applications.
4. **Affiliate programmes** (audio, travel, software) as a door-opener: a good affiliate result is a case study.
5. **Brands already in the audience's comments**: products the audience asks about.

Fit score (0–3 each, pitch 8+ out of 12):
- Audience overlap (top country + age band match)
- Content fit (the product fits an existing pillar without a forced scene)
- Evidence they pay creators at this tier
- Brand comfort with AI (has used AI creative, or is in tech/creator tools)

Disqualify immediately: tokens, exchanges, trading tools, leverage/"earn", gambling, adult, supplements with health claims,
anything aimed at minors, anything the exclusion list names.

## Finding the right contact

- Look for "partnerships@", "creators@", "influencer@" on the brand's site or the creator-programme page.
- LinkedIn: titles like "Influencer Marketing Manager", "Creator Partnerships", "Social Lead". One person per brand.
- If a brand works through an agency (credited in creator posts), pitch the agency.
- DMs to a brand account only if it lists DMs as a contact route or has a "partner with us" highlight.
- Never scrape emails, never guess-and-blast address patterns, never buy lists.

## Anatomy of a pitch (90–140 words)

1. Subject: `<Product name> × Claudia: <one-line idea>`.
2. Opening line about them: a specific campaign, launch or product.
3. One proof number from the kit (median views or a case-study result).
4. AI disclosure in one sentence.
5. The idea: format, scene, platforms, timing.
6. Price or "from" price, or "happy to send a quote".
7. One CTA: "Worth a 15-minute call next week?" or "Shall I send a one-page concept?"
8. Signature: operator's real name, role, kit link.

## Follow-up cadence

| Day | Touch | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Pitch | As above |
| 4–5 | Follow-up 1 | One new piece of evidence (a fresh post result, a seasonal hook) |
| 10–12 | Follow-up 2 | Short; offer a smaller format or a later date |
| 21 | Close the loop | Polite stop, kit link stays live |
| 90+ | Re-open only with news | New milestone, new format, new case study |

Three touches maximum. Stop immediately on any "no" or "not now"; mark the date and re-check in 90 days only if they said
"not now".

## Handling common replies

- **"Send your rates."** Reply within one business day with the kit, the "from" prices and two package options. Ask about
  their goal and dates so the quote can be specific.
- **"Is she real?"** Answer plainly: she's an AI character, operated by <name>, every post labelled. Offer a call with the
  operator.
- **"We only do gifting."** Fine for a nano/micro test if the product fits; a gifted post is still an ad (label it #ad in
  the UK and US). For an AI character, gifting means the operator receives and tests the product; say so.
- **"Can we pay in our token?" / "Can she shill our coin?"** No. See the exclusion list.
- **"Can you guarantee X views?"** No guarantees on views or sales. Offer a make-good (one extra Story or X post) if the
  sponsored post lands below 50 % of the account median within 7 days.
- **Lowball offer.** Hold the base fee; reduce scope (drop a platform, drop usage rights) instead.

## Pacing for an agent that helps with outreach

- The agent researches and drafts; a person approves and sends from their own inbox. The agent does not send email or DMs
  on its own.
- At most 5–8 new pitches a week; at most 1 touch per brand per 4 days.
- Log every touch in the brand CSV. If the same brand appears twice, merge.
- Never use the posting package to DM brands: `@useclaudia/social` DMs are for replying to people who wrote first.
