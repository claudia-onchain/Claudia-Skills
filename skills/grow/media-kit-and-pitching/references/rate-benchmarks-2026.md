# Rate benchmarks and pricing method (2026)

Read this in Step 4 when building the rate card, and again before any custom quote. Benchmarks are market surveys, not
rules; they vary by niche, country and season (Q4 pays more). Checked 2026-10.

## Tier bands per sponsored post

| Tier (followers) | Instagram post / Reel | TikTok video | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nano 1k–10k | $20–200 | $20–500 | Often product-only or affiliate deals |
| Micro 10k–50k | $200–2,000 | $500–2,000 | Highest-volume tier for brand deals |
| Mid 50k–500k | $2,000–5,000 | $2,000–5,000 | Usage rights start to dominate the price |
| Macro 500k–1M | $5,000–15,000 | $5,000–20,000 | Agencies, contracts with KPIs |
| Mega 1M+ | $15,000–50,000+ | $20,000+ | |

- TikTok median rates run roughly 40–50 % below Instagram Reel medians at the same tier.
- YouTube integrations pay the most per deliverable (mid-tier up to about $10,000 per video) because of shelf life; a
  Shorts-only deliverable prices closer to a TikTok video.
- X: about $2 per 1,000 followers per post is the commonly quoted benchmark; threads and pinned posts price higher.
- Telegram channel posts are usually priced per 1,000 average post views (check comparable channels in the niche) and
  priced higher if pinned or kept in the channel for a set time.

Sources: contentgrip.com/influencer-marketing-rate-card, sociallypowerful.com/influencer/marketing/cost,
leaddyno.com/blog/influencer-pricing (all checked 2026-10).

## Working CPM bands (derived)

To price from views instead of followers, the skill uses these bands, derived by dividing the tier bands above by typical
median views at each tier. They are a method, not a published standard.

| Deliverable | CPM band (USD per 1,000 median views) |
|---|---|
| TikTok video (in-feed, 30–90 s) | $10–25 |
| Instagram Reel | $15–30 |
| YouTube Short | $10–20 |
| YouTube long-form integration (60–90 s read) | $20–40 |
| X post | $5–15 (threads +50 %) |
| Telegram channel post | $8–20 |
| Instagram Story frame (3 frames + link sticker) | $5–12 per frame |

Base fee = median views × CPM ÷ 1000, then clamp to the tier band. Niches with strong buying intent (audio gear, travel,
software) sit at the top; broad lifestyle at the bottom.

## Bundles

Bundles sell better than single posts and smooth out weak platforms. Price at 10–15 % below the sum of parts.

Example bundle "City Night": 1 Reel + same video on TikTok and Shorts + 1 X thread + 1 Telegram post + 7-day link in bio.

## AI-character specifics

- Do not discount "because it's AI". The brand buys attention and fit; the cost of generation is the operator's problem.
- Do charge for likeness control: a clause that the brand may not alter the character's face, voice or body, or put new
  words in her mouth, and that usage ends when the term ends.
- Do budget review time: every sponsored asset needs an approval pass for labels, claims and product accuracy (generated
  products can come out wrong; logos and text in renders often need fixing).
- Platform income programmes treat AI accounts differently (TikTok Creator Rewards reportedly excludes fully AI-generated
  videos; YouTube's inauthentic-content policy covers mass-produced AI). Sponsorships are often the main income for an AI
  character; price them properly. See [monetization-streams](../../monetization-streams/SKILL.md).

## Floor price and walk-away

Write the floor before talking to any brand: the lowest fee that covers generation spend, review hours (at the operator's
hourly rate) and the reach cost of a sponsored slot (a sponsored post usually underperforms an organic one by 20–40 %).
Below the floor, offer a smaller deliverable instead of a lower price.

## Payment terms norms

- 50 % on signature, 50 % on delivery for new brands; net-30 for established brands with a PO.
- Late fee 1.5 % per month after 30 days, stated in the quote.
- Never accept payment in a token the brand issued, and never accept "payment in exposure".
