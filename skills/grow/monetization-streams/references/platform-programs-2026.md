# Platform monetization programs, 2026 (checked 2026-10)

Read when a program is close to eligible, when applying, or when a platform announces a change. Programs change often:
re-check the official page before applying and write the check date into `templates/eligibility-tracker.csv`. Numbers
labelled "estimate" come from industry reporting, not the platform.

## X — Original Content Rewards

- **Status**: launched 8 August 2026, replacing Creator Revenue Sharing, which stopped new enrolment and retired in
  September 2026.
- **Eligibility** (all at once): active Premium, Premium+ or Premium Business (Basic doesn't qualify); 18+; at least 500
  verified followers; 500,000 verified Home Timeline impressions in the prior 90 days, replies excluded (about 5,600 a day).
- **What pays**: impressions of your own posts in the Home Timeline from paying Premium users, counted when at least half the
  post is on screen. Not replies, not reposts of others' content.
- **What removes you**: unoriginal content (reposts, reuploads, content reproduced from elsewhere earns nothing); asking for
  likes/reposts/replies/follows three or more times leads to removal and a policy referral; a lapsed subscription.
- **Payouts**: biweekly, via Stripe or X Money.
- **For AI accounts**: the account must follow the automation rules (Automated label, linked managing account). Check the
  program terms for automated-account eligibility before buying Premium for this purpose.
- Sources: opentweet.io/blog/x-original-content-rewards-2026 (2026); help.x.com creator subscription and monetization pages.

Other X income: Subscriptions (paid followers, needs eligibility in-app), Tips. X API costs are an expense: $0.015 per post,
$0.20 per post with a link (pay-per-use, checked 2026-10).

## TikTok

### Creator Rewards Program
- **Eligibility**: 18+ (19 in South Korea), 10,000 followers, 100,000 video views in the last 30 days, personal account in
  good standing, eligible country (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan, South Korea, Mexico, Brazil as of July 2026).
- **What pays**: original public videos over 1 minute; payout formula weights originality, play duration, search value and
  engagement. "Qualified views" exclude many views your analytics show.
- **Typical RPM**: about $0.40–1.00 per 1,000 qualified views; higher for high-retention niches (estimates, 2026).
- **AI caveat**: 2026 reporting (Storrito and others) says fully AI-generated videos and accounts built entirely on AI avatars
  are excluded; AI-assisted editing (captions, colour, audio clean-up) stays eligible. Confirm in the in-app program terms.
  For a fully AI character like Claudia, plan $0 from this program.
- **Crypto**: finance and crypto promotion is restricted on TikTok regardless of monetization; branded content can't promote
  financial products including crypto.

### Other TikTok income
- **LIVE gifts**: 18+, about 1,000 followers (varies by region), good standing. Avatar/AI streams: read the LIVE guidelines
  first; misleading "live person" framing is a risk.
- **Subscriptions** (in-app, for eligible creators), **TikTok Shop affiliate** (products only; no financial products).
- **Branded content**: must use the content disclosure setting; financial products banned.
- Sources: tiktok.com/creators (Creator Rewards), tiktok.com/community-guidelines, shortsync.app/resources/
  tiktok-creator-rewards-program-2026, storrito.com/resources/what-tiktoks-ai-monetization-restrictions-signal-for-creator-income.

## YouTube

- **YPP entry today**: 1,000 subscribers + 10 million valid public Shorts views in 90 days, or 1,000 subscribers + 4,000
  valid public watch hours in 12 months.
- **From 1 February 2027** (announced 10 August 2026), new applicants: 1,000 subscribers + 20 million Shorts views in 90 days,
  or 8,000 watch hours. Existing partners don't reapply but must accept updated terms by 31 January 2027 and stay active
  (for example 5 Shorts per 90 days). Reported: a Shorts revenue floor of 10M views in 90 days for monthly payout.
- **Fan funding tier**: 500 subscribers + 3M Shorts views in 90 days or 3,000 watch hours: Super Thanks, Super Stickers,
  channel memberships.
- **Shorts revenue**: ads between Shorts go into a pool; the creator pool is shared by views, and creators keep 45% of their
  allocated revenue. Shorts RPMs are commonly reported at a few cents per 1,000 views (estimate).
- **AI rules**: the altered or synthetic content toggle is required for realistic AI people, voices or altered real events;
  YouTube has auto-labelled undisclosed synthetic content since 27 May 2026. The inauthentic content policy (renamed from
  "repetitious" in July 2025, channel-level enforcement in 2026) demonetizes templated, mass-produced AI uploads such as AI
  voiceover farms. Reported 2026 clarifications flag AI personas discussing finance, health or legal topics as a
  monetization risk. A character-driven, varied channel with disclosure is the safe shape.
- Sources: support.google.com/youtube (YPP eligibility; altered or synthetic content), air.io/en/monetization/
  youtube-monetization-policy-changes-2026-a-complete-dated-timeline, vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-monetization.

## Meta (Instagram, Facebook, Threads)

- **Subscriptions** on Instagram and Facebook for eligible professional accounts; Instagram passed 1M active paid
  subscriptions. Meta has waived its subscription fee with a promised six-month notice before charging (check current terms).
- **Bonuses** (Reels, Threads, holiday programs) are invite-only, region-limited and change often. Never plan on them.
- **Gifts** on Reels and Facebook Stars for eligible accounts.
- **Branded content**: use the Paid partnership label; crypto ads need Meta's written permission.
- **AI**: the AI info label is required for photorealistic video and realistic audio made or altered with AI.
- Sources: about.fb.com/news (creator monetization posts), facebook.com/business/help (branded content), techcrunch.com
  coverage of Instagram subscriptions.

## Telegram

- **Telegram Ad Platform**: ads run in public channels with 1,000+ subscribers; channel owners receive 50% of the revenue.
  CPMs are reported at about $0.5–2 depending on country and niche (2026 estimate).
- **Stars**: paid posts (unlock a post for Stars), Star subscriptions for private channels, Star reactions and gifts. Stars
  become withdrawable 21 days after receipt; withdrawals go through Fragment to TON with a 1,000-Star minimum. A working
  benchmark is about $0.013 per Star, varying with TON's price (2026 estimates).
- **Bots** can sell digital goods for Stars (Telegram requires Stars for digital goods sold inside the app).
- Sources: telegram.org/blog (Stars, ad revenue sharing), core.telegram.org/bots/payments-stars,
  marix.app/library/guides/telegram-channel-monetization-2026.

## Discord

- **Server Subscriptions**: monthly tiers priced $2.99–$199.99; owners keep 90% minus processing fees. Eligibility (size,
  age, region, good standing) is set by Discord and has been US-first; check Server Settings → Monetization.
- **Server Shop / Server Products**: one-time purchases (downloads, roles that unlock channels), 90% to the owner.
- **2026 age assurance**: teen-by-default settings and age checks for some features affect who can see age-restricted
  channels; keep paid perks suitable for the audience that can see them.
- Sources: discord.com/creators (Server Subscriptions 101), lindseygamble.com/blog/discord-server-subscriptions.

## Claudia platform

- **Creator rewards**: coins launched on the Claudia launchpad (Solana) send 70% of creator fees to the creator. See
  `creator-rewards-on-claudia.md`.
- The platform itself doesn't pay for posts, views or thread activity.
