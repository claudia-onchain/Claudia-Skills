# Direct revenue streams: pricing and terms (checked 2026-10)

Read before pricing a deal, a product or a membership. Direct streams are where an AI character can earn reliably, because
the operator sets the terms. All ranges are 2026 market benchmarks (contentgrip.com, sociallypowerful.com, leaddyno.com,
2026) and are starting points, not entitlements.

## 1. Brand deals and sponsorships

Benchmarks per deliverable (one post or video):

| Tier | Instagram | TikTok | YouTube | X |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nano 1–10k | $20–200 | $20–500 | — | ≈ $2 per 1,000 followers |
| Micro 10–50k | $200–2,000 | $500–2,000 | — | same |
| Mid 50–500k | $2,000–5,000 | $2,000–5,000 | up to ~$10,000 per integration | same |
| Macro 500k–1M | $5,000–15,000 | $5,000–20,000 | higher | same |

Add-ons usually priced on top: usage rights (brand reposts or runs it as an ad) +25–100% depending on term (30/90/365 days);
exclusivity in a category +20–50%; whitelisting / Spark Ads access; rush delivery; extra revisions beyond two.

How to price an AI character honestly:
- Price on proven results, not followers: median views per post over the last 30 days × a CPM the brand accepts
  ($10–30 per 1,000 views for short video is a common negotiating range) is a defensible anchor.
- An AI character can't claim to have used or tested a product. Write "Claudia styled this look with Northpine headphones"
  (true, as a creative choice), not "I've worn these every day for a month".
- Always disclose `#ad` / Paid partnership **and** that the content is AI-generated.
- Coins, exchanges, trading apps and other financial products: decline for TikTok, decline for UK audiences, and get advice
  before anything else. Default: decline.

Process, contracts and red flags: [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md); the kit
that wins deals: [media-kit-and-pitching](../../media-kit-and-pitching/SKILL.md).

## 2. Affiliate

- Commission ranges: physical retail 1–10%, fashion/beauty 5–20%, software and digital products 20–50% (often recurring).
- Fit beats rate: link only what the content actually features.
- Disclosure: "#ad" or "affiliate link" right beside the link, in the post itself; a bio note alone is not enough (FTC;
  CMA/ASA count affiliate links as advertising).
- Track with UTM-style sub-IDs per platform (`subid=tiktok-ep4`) so the ledger can attribute income.
- Don't: crypto exchange or trading-app referral links (UK: incentives to invest are banned in crypto promotions; TikTok:
  financial promotion banned); link cloaking that hides the destination.

## 3. UGC licensing (content for a brand's own channels)

- The creator produces assets; the brand posts or runs them as ads. Typical small-creator pricing: $150–1,500 per short
  video, plus usage term and paid-media rights.
- For AI characters: deliver as "AI-generated character content". The brand must keep the AI label in its ads (Meta AI
  info, TikTok AIGC). Never produce "customer testimonial" style videos: an AI presented as a satisfied customer is a fake
  review under the FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR 465), whatever the disclosure.
- Contract points: usage term, channels, territories, edits allowed, whitelisting, no use in financial promotions, removal on
  request after term, credit line.

## 4. Digital products

| Product | Fits | Price range |
|---|---|---|
| Wallpaper / banner packs | lifestyle characters (Claudia) | $5–15 |
| Presets / LUTs / colour recipes | photo and video audiences | $9–29 |
| Prompt packs and character bibles (for the operator's own original characters) | creator audiences | $12–49 |
| Guides / mini-courses (education only) | educational agents (Juno) | $15–49 |
| Templates (Notion, calendars, media kits) | creators | $9–29 |

Rules: say it's AI-made; say exactly what's included (counts, sizes, formats); state the licence (personal / commercial);
offer a refund window; never sell "signals", trade calls or "systems" that imply returns. Prompt packs must not recreate
real people or copyrighted characters. Sell where the audience already is: Telegram Stars paid posts or a bot,
Discord Server Shop, or a storefront.

## 5. Memberships

- Telegram Star subscriptions, Discord Server Subscriptions, YouTube channel memberships, Instagram subscriptions.
- $3–10/month is the common band; one clear perk beats a list of vague ones ("an extra diary episode every week",
  "monthly behind-the-scenes prompt breakdown").
- Don't promise what an autonomous agent might not deliver; schedule perks like any other content and have a person check
  delivery monthly.
- No paid "alpha", signals or trade calls. That is regulated activity in many countries and banned on TikTok.

## 6. Services

- Agent setup for others, content production, workshops. Price per project; written scope; payment upfront or in
  milestones. Not posted as content, so disclosure is a contract matter, but mention client work honestly if you show it.

## 7. Tips and gifts

- X Tips, Telegram Stars reactions and gifts, YouTube Super Thanks, TikTok LIVE gifts. Nice to have; plan $0.

## What not to promise, ever

- To the audience: income ("I make $X, you can too"), returns, "passive income" from a product or coin.
- To brands: guaranteed views, sales or follower growth. Offer deliverables and past medians.
- To members: perks the agent can't reliably produce.
- To holders of any coin: anything.
