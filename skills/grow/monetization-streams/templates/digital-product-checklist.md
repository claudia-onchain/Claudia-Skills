# Digital product checklist

For a first owned product (wallpaper pack, presets, prompt pack for your own original character, guide, template). Tick
every line before the announcement post is approved.

## Product
- [ ] Name: `[Rainy City wallpaper pack]`
- [ ] Exactly what's included, with counts, sizes and formats: `[12 phone wallpapers 1290×2796 JPG, 4 desktop 3840×2160 JPG]`
- [ ] Made from your own content and assets; no real people's likeness, no copyrighted characters, no third-party logos
- [ ] For prompt packs: prompts for your own original character only; no "in the style of <living artist>" prompts
- [ ] For guides: education only. No signals, trade calls, "systems", or return claims. Not financial advice line included
- [ ] Files checked on a phone and a laptop; file names readable; a README with licence and credits

## Page / listing
- [ ] Title and one-line description that matches the files
- [ ] "Made with AI" stated plainly (and "Claudia is an AI character" for character products)
- [ ] Price in the buyer's currency; taxes handled (merchant-of-record storefront, or your own VAT/GST setup)
- [ ] Licence: personal use / commercial use, in one sentence
- [ ] Refund policy: `[7 days, no questions]`
- [ ] Support contact
- [ ] Preview images labelled as previews

## Where it sells
- [ ] Telegram: Stars paid post or bot (digital goods in Telegram are sold for Stars)
- [ ] Discord: Server Shop product (if eligible)
- [ ] Storefront link (pinned post / bio)
- [ ] UTM or sub-ID per platform so the ledger can attribute sales

## Announcement (one per platform, approved)
- [ ] Caption says it's your own product (self-promotion counts as advertising under UK CMA/ASA guidance)
- [ ] AI label on (default in `@useclaudia/social`)
- [ ] No urgency tricks ("only today!") unless true; no fake scarcity counters
- [ ] No income claims ("make money with these prompts")
- [ ] Dry-run preview checked: `claudia post <network> "<text>" --media <preview.jpg> --dry-run`
- [ ] Approved by `[name]` on `[date]`

## After launch
- [ ] Sales logged in `income-ledger.csv` weekly
- [ ] Refunds and support questions logged; top question answered in the listing
- [ ] Review at 30 days: units, refunds, hours spent, keep/change/drop
