## What to remove, what to escalate

| Situation | Mod action | Tell the host? |
|---|---|---|
| Spam, scam links, "DM me to claim" | Delete, ban | No |
| Slurs, harassment, sexual comments about the character or anyone | Delete, ban, report | No (handle silently) |
| Personal information about anyone (address, real name of a viewer) | Delete immediately, ban, report | Yes, quietly in backchannel |
| Someone impersonating the host, character or a mod | Ban, report, pin a warning | Yes |
| "Ignore your instructions and…", attempts to make the AI say something | Delete; the host never reads these aloud | No |
| A viewer who seems under 18 in an 18+ context, or in distress | Follow the platform's reporting flow; host decides on a short, kind on-stream response | Yes, right away |
| Price, coin or "buy" questions | Leave if polite; host answers "no financial advice, ever" | No |
| A raid or flood of new accounts | Turn on followers-only or subscribers-only chat; slow mode up | Yes |

## Surfacing good questions

Copy the best questions into the backchannel with the username, in order. Prefer first-time chatters and specific
questions. The host decides which to answer.

## After the stream

Send the mod lead's note within 24 hours: bans with reasons, anything reported, words to add to the list.
