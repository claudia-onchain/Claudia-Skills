# Going live: platform requirements and rules for AI characters

Read this in step 2, before picking a platform, and again before monetising a stream. Checked 2026-10 on each
platform's help or policy pages. Some official pages only render in a browser; those items are marked "from the
official page's search excerpt", and items that couldn't be confirmed are left out (listed at the end).

## TikTok LIVE

- **Age:** 18+ to go LIVE and to send gifts; 18+ for any monetisation feature (Community Guidelines effective 24
  September 2026).
- **Followers:** TikTok's safety guide says the account "must have a certain number of followers, which may vary by
  market." No single official number is published; figures quoted elsewhere aren't confirmed here.
- **LIVE Studio and third-party software:** access can be instant if account age, recent LIVE time, follower count and
  suspension record meet requirements that "vary by country or region and are subject to change without notice";
  streamers from other platforms can apply, and review "might take days."
- **You own your tools:** creators are "responsible for anything that happens during their sessions — even when it
  involves third-party tools like voice-to-text software, real-time translation, or on-screen comment displays."
  Repeated tool-related problems can restrict LIVE access. An AI character's voice and avatar rig count as such tools.
- **Be present:** "Streaming previously recorded content in TikTok LIVE is not recommended." "Using a no-face effect
  might be easily regarded as low interaction, and LIVE might be regarded as a recording." "Creators are recommended to
  be present and on screen throughout the duration of the LIVE." The LIVE Monetization Guidelines say rebroadcast,
  pre-recorded, spliced or looping video presented as live may not be eligible for rewards (search excerpt).
  **Implication:** on TikTok, put the human host on camera with the AI character, or expect reduced reach and no rewards.
- **AI labels:** label AI-generated content showing realistic people or scenes, and AI audio that mimics a real
  person's voice; artistic styles "like anime" and generic text-to-speech don't need the label. Label an AI character
  anyway in the title and on screen; it's the honest default.
- **Authenticity:** "Authenticity within LIVE is essential"; pretending to be someone else or streaming others' content
  without permission can restrict LIVE.
- **Moderation:** moderators must be friends or mutual followers; keyword filter up to 500 words, editable live by host
  and mods; mutes from 5 seconds to the whole LIVE; filter flagged comments; turn comments off; block users.
- **Gifts and incentives:** LIVEs that "Trick or pressure people into giving Gifts or engaging, like 'like-for-like'
  schemes or fake incentives" are restricted; gambling or gambling-like activity on LIVE is not allowed anywhere.
- **TikTok Shop LIVE** has stricter seller rules (search excerpt): no AI-generated voices, no non-real-time audio, and
  no still or animated content covering more than half the screen. Don't run an AI-voiced shopping LIVE.

## YouTube Live

- **Requirements:** verified channel, no live-streaming restrictions in the past 90 days, age 16+. A strike blocks
  streaming for 14 days. Up to 10 active streams per channel.
- **Mobile:** at least 50 subscribers. You "may need to wait 24 hours" before the first stream. Under 1,000
  subscribers, mobile viewer numbers may be limited and archives default to private.
- **Archive:** streams under 12 hours can be archived automatically; longer streams "may not be captured at all", so
  record locally as a backup.
- **Chat tools:** blocked words, hold potentially inappropriate messages (None / Basic / Strict), slow mode (message
  delay; owner, moderators and members exempt), timeouts, standard and managing moderators, subscribers-only,
  members-only and live-commentary modes.
- **AI disclosure:** realistic altered or synthetic content must be disclosed; persistent non-disclosure can lead to
  labels, removal or suspension from the Partner Program.
- **Monetisation:** Super Chat needs the fan-funding eligibility (500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, and 3,000
  watch hours in 12 months or 3 million Shorts views in 90 days), a supported location and accepted terms; it's
  unavailable on age-restricted, unlisted, private or made-for-kids content. The "inauthentic content" policy disallows
  "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass production"; that
  covers 24/7 templated AI streams.

## X: live video and Spaces

- **Spaces:** "Up to 13 people (including the host and 2 co-hosts) can speak" at once (from the official page's search
  excerpt). Co-hosts can't add or remove co-hosts or end the Space.
- **Live video:** Live Studio (studio.x.com/live) replaced Media Studio Producer and accepts streams from external
  encoders; X lists Media Studio as a Premium and Premium+ feature (search excerpt).
- **Synthetic media:** sharing synthetic or manipulated media that may deceive and lead to harm is not allowed;
  penalties range from labels and reduced visibility to removal. Animation, cartoons and satire are generally fine
  unless they cause significant confusion about authenticity.
- **AI voice in a Space:** have the human operator host. If the character's synthetic voice is played, say so at the
  start and in the Space title ("AI voice"). See the x-playbook skill's automation reference.

## Twitch

- **Affiliate (changed June 2026):** 25 followers, 4 hours streamed, on 4 different days, and an average of 3
  concurrent viewers on 4 days, all within the last 30 days; two-factor authentication required; ages 13–17 need
  parental consent. (The old bar was 50 followers, 8 hours, 7 days.)
- **Pre-recorded and tools:** the Terms of Service allow "streaming live and pre-recorded" works, and content produced
  by "third-party tools you use" is your responsibility.
- **AI and VTubers:** no AI-specific rule in the Community Guidelines; VTubers are explicitly bound by the attire and
  minor-safety rules. Prohibited: deepfakes used to abuse a person, deepfake intimate images, impersonating another
  person, artificially inflating viewer counts. Drops must be earned live, "not through ... rebroadcasts of past
  VODs/footage, or other scenarios where you are not interacting with your community."
- **Content Classification Labels:** required for mature-rated games, sexual themes, drugs/intoxication/excessive
  tobacco, violent and graphic depictions, significant profanity, politics and sensitive social issues, and gambling.
- **Moderation:** AutoMod levels 0–4 across five categories (turns on at Level 1 with a moderator, Level 2 without);
  slow mode default 120 seconds; blocked terms support `*` wildcards.

## Kick

- **Age:** 13+ (or the local age of digital consent), with parental supervision under 18.
- **Subscriptions:** enabled after at least 5 total hours streamed. Partner needs, in the past 30 days: 75 average
  concurrent viewers, 30 hours streamed, 25 active subscriptions, 250 unique chatters, 3 VODs and 250 followers.
- **AI (guidelines updated 19 March 2026):** disclose realistic AI content in the stream title or an on-screen overlay;
  no misleading deepfakes or voice clones; no AI simulation of "a realistic endorsement or the presence of another
  person" without permission; no bots inflating metrics.

## Fully AI-run or 24/7 streams

None of the five platforms explicitly bans a stream run entirely by an AI, and none requires a human on camera in the
rules found. But: TikTok discourages pre-recorded and no-face LIVEs and may withhold rewards; YouTube won't monetise
mass-produced templated AI content; Twitch Drops can't be earned without live interaction; every platform holds the
account owner responsible for what the tools say. This skill's default is a human operator present for the whole
stream, able to cut the character's audio in one keypress.

## Clips

- YouTube Clips: 5–60 seconds; can't clip a live stream without DVR or outside the DVR window.
- Twitch clips: up to 60 seconds.
- TikTok: LIVE replays can be viewed, downloaded or clipped by the creator for 30 days (search excerpt).

## Not confirmed, so not used

- A single TikTok follower number for LIVE, TikTok stream-key rules, and any LIVE duration limit.
- An 18+ rule for YouTube Super Chat (not stated on the pages read).
- Who can host X Spaces in 2026, Spaces recording rules, and exactly who can go live on X from an encoder.
- Twitch Shield Mode details and a Twitch rule specific to AI voices of real people.
- Kick Affiliate requirements.
