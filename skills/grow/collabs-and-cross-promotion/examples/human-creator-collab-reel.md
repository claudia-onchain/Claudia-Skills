# Worked example: Claudia × a human travel creator (Instagram collab Reel)

Mara (@mara.wanders, fictional) is a human slow-travel creator: 52,000 Instagram followers, median Reel views 18,000,
audience mostly UK, US and Portugal, ages 22–34. Claudia's Instagram is smaller (12,000 followers, median 14,000 views
because her Reels travel well to non-followers).

## Why Mara

Score 92/100: near-identical audience with a different itch (Mara shows the real city, Claudia imagines it), real
comment conversations, 1.3× size ratio, discloses her own brand deals, posts twice a week for two years.

## The pitch (script 1, sent by Claudia's operator from the business email)

> Hi Mara, I'm Claudia — an AI influencer (clearly labelled as AI on every post). A small team runs my account and
> reads every reply. Your night-train Reel from Lisbon stuck with me — the way you held the window shot for four
> seconds before the city lights came on. Idea: a two-part collab Reel. You shoot 8–10 seconds of the real Alfama
> street at dusk; I do the same street as an AI-made scene, same framing; we cut between them and post it as one
> collab post. Your side: the footage + approving the edit. Mine: edit, captions, cover, and you keep the files.
> Window: first week of November. Your audience would know I'm AI from the first line of the caption. No worries at
> all if it's not a fit.

Mara said yes with one condition: the caption must make clear which half is real. That became the hook.

## The piece

- 22-second Reel. 0–2 s: text "One of these is real." Real/AI alternate every 3 s, with a small "REAL" / "AI" tag in
  the corner of every shot. Last 3 s: both side by side, "Mara is human. I'm not."
- Caption (5 hashtags max on Instagram):

```text
Same street, two ways: Mara filmed the real Alfama at dusk, I made the AI version. Tags in the corner tell you which
is which. Which one would you rather walk down? 

Collab with @mara.wanders — Mara is human, I'm an AI influencer. AI parts are AI-generated.
#lisbon #alfama #slowtravel #aiart #travelreels
```

- Meta AI info disclosure switched on (the AI frames are photorealistic). No `#ad`: no money, product, affiliate link
  or brand involved, and no incentive beyond the shared content.

## Publishing

Instagram collab invites are sent from the app, so Claudia's operator uploaded the Reel in Instagram, added Mara as
collaborator, switched on the AI label, and posted at 11:30 UTC on a Tuesday (Instagram's strongest window in Sprout
Social's 2026 data is Tue/Wed 11–13 local; the audience is mostly UK). Mara accepted within 20 minutes. Claudia's
Telegram and X got one link-free mention each via `claudia post`, previewed and approved.

## Results at day 7

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Views | 214,000 (61% non-followers of either account) |
| Sends (DM shares) | 3,900 |
| Follows from the Reel | Claudia +2,310, Mara +1,140 |
| Comments | 1,020, many guessing real vs AI |
| Story-link clicks (tagged) | 480 |

The "which is real" mechanic drove sends. The team noted the risk: a format built on "can you tell?" can drift into
deception. The rule they kept for future posts: **every AI shot is tagged on screen**, and the caption says which is
which.
