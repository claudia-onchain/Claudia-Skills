# Partner fit scoring

Read this in step 3, when cutting a long list of 20–40 candidates down to 5–8 worth pitching. Everything here can be
done by looking at public profiles and the partner's own shared analytics; nothing needs scraping or automation.

## The 100-point score

| Factor | Points | How to score it |
|---|---|---|
| Audience adjacency | 0–25 | 25 = same audience, different need (travel creator × eSIM tips). 15 = overlapping interest (charts × desk setups). 5 = loosely related. 0 = unrelated or your audience would be confused. |
| Engagement quality | 0–20 | Read the last 20 comments on 3 recent posts. 20 = specific, conversational comments and the creator replies. 10 = mixed. 0 = emoji walls, "nice pic 🔥" repeated, or comments in languages that don't match the audience. |
| Size ratio | 0–15 | Use **median views** of their last 10 posts ÷ your median. 15 = 0.5×–2×. 10 = 0.3×–0.5× or 2×–3×. 5 = 3×–10× (only if you bring something rare). 0 = outside that. |
| Brand safety and compliance | 0–15 | 15 = labels ads (#ad / Paid partnership), labels AI, no scams. 8 = unclear. 0 = any red flag below. |
| Format fit | 0–10 | 10 = already does the format (LIVEs, duets, threads, AMAs). 5 = adjacent. 0 = never has. |
| Reliability | 0–10 | 10 = posts on a steady cadence for 90+ days, answers DMs/emails. 5 = bursty. 0 = dormant > 30 days. |
| Geography and language | 0–5 | 5 = top-3 countries overlap with yours. 2 = one overlaps. 0 = none. |

**Cut-offs:** ≥ 75 pitch first; 60–74 pitch if the idea is strong; < 60 skip. Re-score partners every quarter.

## Healthy engagement bands (rough, 2026)

Engagement rate (ER) = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ views (short video) or ÷ followers (static posts).
Industry benchmark reports for 2026 put typical short-video ER in these ranges; use them only to spot outliers, not as
targets (checked 2026-10; benchmarks vary by source and niche):

| Platform | Typical ER by views | Look harder if |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 3–9% | > 20% on most posts, or < 1% |
| Instagram Reels | 2–6% | > 15% with generic comments |
| YouTube Shorts | 2–5% | likes ≫ views pattern |
| X | 0.5–3% of impressions | many replies from accounts created this month |

## Red flags (any one = no)

- **Bought followers or engagement:** sudden follower jumps of 10k+ in a day with no viral post; follower count far
  above median views (e.g. 300k followers, 800 views); a large share of followers with no profile photo, no posts and
  random-digit handles.
- **Engagement pods:** the same 10–30 accounts comment within minutes on every post, often with long generic praise.
- **Undisclosed ads:** product placements, discount codes or affiliate links with no `#ad` / "Paid partnership".
- **Coin shilling:** posts promising returns, "100x", "next gem", countdowns to a pump, unlabelled paid token promos,
  or pushing UK/EU audiences to buy (see [crypto-marketing-compliance](../../crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md)).
- **Impersonation or deceptive AI:** an AI account that claims to be human, or that uses a real person's likeness.
- **Harassment, hate or pile-on history**, or a pattern of public feuds.
- **Ban evasion:** "backup account of @…" in the bio.
- **Minors:** the creator or the core audience is under 18 (Claudia only works with adults and adult audiences).

## Questions to ask the partner (in the brief, not the first DM)

1. Top 3 countries and age bands from your analytics (a screenshot is fine).
2. Median views on your last 10 posts on the platform we'll use.
3. Have you done collabs before? What worked?
4. Are you comfortable collaborating with an AI character, with that clearly labelled?
5. Any brands or coins you're contracted to that we should avoid mentioning?

## Size strategy

- **Peers (0.5×–2×)** are the best first collabs: equal effort, equal upside, and more likely to repeat.
- **Punching up (3×+)** works only with a concrete, rare offer: a finished asset pack, an exclusive, a format they can't
  make themselves. Expect a < 10% reply rate.
- **Lifting smaller accounts** is good for the brand and community; do it on purpose, a few times a quarter, without
  expecting reach back.
