# Collab DM and email scripts

Each script is a starting point for **one** message to **one** person. Replace every `<…>` with something specific to
them; if you can't fill `<their post>` with a real post you watched, don't send it. A human sends these, or approves
each one before it goes. Max 5 new pitches a day, one follow-up after 5–7 days, then stop.

Keep it under 120 words. No links in a first DM on Instagram or TikTok (they look like spam); offer to send a one-pager.

---

## 1. First pitch to a human creator (Instagram / TikTok business DM or email)

Subject (email): `Collab idea: <concept in 5 words>`

```text
Hi <first name or handle>, I'm Claudia — an AI influencer (clearly labelled as AI on every post). <Operator name> and a
small team run my account and read every reply.

Your <their post, e.g. "night-train Reel from Lisbon"> stuck with me — <one specific reason: the sound design, the
pacing, the comments asking about the route>.

Idea: <concept, e.g. "a two-part Reel: you shoot the real city at dusk, I do the same street as an AI-made scene, and we
post it as one collab post">. Your side: <effort, e.g. "8–10 s of footage + approve the edit">. My side: <effort, e.g.
"edit, captions, cover; you get the files to keep">. Window: <dates>.

Your audience would know I'm AI from the first line of the caption. No worries at all if it's not a fit.

— Claudia (sent by <operator name>)
```

## 2. First pitch to another agent's operator

```text
Hi <operator name> — I run Claudia (@claudia_onchain), an AI influencer on useclaudia.xyz. Juno's <specific post, e.g.
"thread on why wicks lie"> is the clearest chart explainer I've seen this month.

Idea: a one-week "chart week" — Juno explains one pattern a day, Claudia turns it into a 20-second visual. Planned
posts only: 4 posts and up to 6 replies each, all pre-approved by both of us, both labelled AI, no coins or price talk.

Happy to send a one-page brief. If the timing's wrong, no problem.

— <your name>, for Claudia
```

## 3. Telegram channel / Discord server admin (shout-out swap)

```text
Hi <admin name>, I run Claudia's Telegram channel (<n> members, mostly <audience>). Your <specific thing: weekly
desk-setup thread / Sunday photo prompt> is the kind of thing my members ask for.

Would you do a one-time swap? One native post each on <date> at <time UTC>, pinned 24 h, wording approved by both.
I'll share join numbers from my invite link afterwards so you can see what it did.

— <your name>
```

## 4. Follow-up (once, 5–7 days later, same thread)

```text
Hi <name>, just floating this back up once in case it got buried. If <concept> isn't for you, all good — I won't
chase again. 🙂
```

## 5. They said yes

```text
Brilliant — thank you. I'll send the one-page brief today with dates, who posts what, the disclosure lines (I label
everything as AI), and the tracked link. Anything you never want mentioned or shown?
```

## 6. Saying no to an inbound pitch

```text
Thanks for thinking of Claudia, <name>. It isn't a fit for us right now (<honest short reason: "we keep coins out of
collabs" / "we're booked through November" / "our audiences barely overlap">). I'll keep your work in mind.
```

## 7. Answering an inbound pitch that might be a sponsorship in disguise

Use when the "collab" includes payment, gifted product, an affiliate link, or a coin.

```text
Thanks, <name>! Because this includes <payment / product / a token>, it would be a paid partnership on our side, so
it'd be labelled #ad and go through our sponsorship process (rates, brief, approvals). Shall I send our media kit?
```

Then switch to [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md). If it's a token promotion
aimed at buyers, decline (see [crypto-marketing-compliance](../../crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md)).

## Never send

- The same text to more than one person.
- "Follow for follow", "let's support each other's posts", "join our engagement group".
- Promises of follower counts or views.
- Anything that hides that Claudia is AI or who runs her.
