# Giveaway announcement and winner posts

Copy, fill the `<placeholders>`, and keep every line that is marked required. Links point to the full official rules
(templates/official-rules.md, hosted on your own site or a pinned doc).

## Announcement post (Instagram / TikTok / X / Threads caption)

```text
GIVEAWAY: win <prize, specific: "a signed print of the cobalt studio set, 40 x 50 cm">. (required: say what the prize is)

How to enter: <one action, e.g. "comment with the outfit you'd put me in next">. One entry per person.
No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances. (required for sweepstakes)
Open to <eligibility: "residents of the US (excl. <states>), the UK and Canada, 18+">. (required)
Closes <date, time, time zone>. Winner picked <at random / by judges using the criteria in the rules> on <date>
and announced here by <date>. (required)
Full rules: <link>. (required)
This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, <Instagram / TikTok / X>. (required on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and Reddit; recommended everywhere)

<AI character> is an AI character run by <operator>. <If a brand supplied the prize: "Prize provided by <brand>. #ad">
#giveaway
```

Do not add: "tag 3 friends to enter" or "share / repost to enter" (Facebook bans requiring or rewarding sharing,
reposting or tagging; Instagram bans encouraging inaccurate tags), "whoever reposts most wins" on X, "subscribe and
like 10 videos" on YouTube (metric manipulation), or any line that asks people to pay, buy, hold a token, connect a
wallet or share a seed phrase. See references/platform-promotion-rules.md.

## Reminder post (24 h before close)

```text
24 hours left to enter the <prize> giveaway: <entry action>. No purchase necessary. Closes <date time tz>.
Rules: <link>
```

## Winner announcement

```text
The <prize> giveaway is closed. <n> eligible entries.
Winner: <@handle, only if the rules said you'd announce it and the winner agreed>. Congrats!
How we picked: <"random draw with a public seed: <seed>, entry list hash <first 12 chars>, method in the rules" /
"judged by <names> on <criteria>">.
We'll only ever contact the winner from this account, and we'll never ask for money, passwords or a seed phrase.
```

## Winner DM (send only to the winner, from the main account)

```text
Hi <name>, you won the <prize> giveaway (<link to announcement>). To claim, reply by <date time tz> with:
- the name and postal address for delivery <or the details the prize needs>
- confirmation you're 18+ and live in <eligible region>
You'll never be asked for a payment, a password, a wallet connection or a seed phrase. If you don't reply by the
deadline, the prize goes to the next entry drawn, as the rules say.
```

## Impersonator warning (pin during the giveaway)

```text
Scam warning: fake accounts copy giveaway posts and DM "winners" asking for a fee or a wallet connection.
We only announce winners in this post and only DM from <@handle>. We never ask for money or keys.
```
