# United Kingdom: FCA cryptoasset financial promotions, FG24/1, ASA/CMA (checked 2026-10)

Not legal advice. UK rules are the strictest Claudia's audience is likely to meet, and breaking them can be a crime for
the individual who posts, not only for the firm.

## 1. The regime in one paragraph

Since **8 October 2023**, qualifying cryptoassets (fungible, transferable tokens: bitcoin, ether, SOL, memecoins) are
inside the UK financial promotion restriction in **section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000**. Anyone
who, in the course of business, communicates an invitation or inducement to engage in investment activity in a
qualifying cryptoasset must be either: an FCA-authorised firm, a cryptoasset business registered with the FCA under
the Money Laundering Regulations, a communicator whose promotion has been **approved** by an authorised firm with
approver permission, or within a narrow exemption. Communicating an unlawful financial promotion is a **criminal
offence punishable by up to two years in prison and/or a fine**.

Sources: FCA cryptoasset financial promotions hub — https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/financial-promotions-and-adverts/cryptoassets ·
PS23/6 (final rules) — https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps23-6.pdf

## 2. The rules a compliant promotion must meet (COBS 4.12A)

- **Prescribed risk warning**, prominent, in the form:
  > Don't invest unless you're prepared to lose all the money you invest. This is a high-risk investment and you should
  > not expect to be protected if something goes wrong. Take 2 mins to learn more.
  In digital media "Take 2 mins to learn more" must link to a cryptoasset risk summary. Where character limits make
  that impossible, a shorter form exists, but only for authorised/approved promotions.
- **No incentives to invest**: refer-a-friend bonuses, new-joiner bonuses, bonus tokens for buying, holder giveaways.
- **24-hour cooling-off** for first-time investors with a firm before a direct offer can proceed.
- **Client categorisation** (restricted, high-net-worth, certified sophisticated) and an **appropriateness
  assessment** before direct offers.
- Fair, clear and not misleading; balanced; no trivialising risk; no exploiting inexperience.

None of these can be met by an agent posting on its own account. They are met by a firm. So for Claudia the
practical rule is: **don't make UK crypto promotions; write education and news.**

## 3. FG24/1: finalised guidance on financial promotions on social media (26 March 2024)

Source: https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/finalised-guidance/fg24-1-finalised-guidance-financial-promotions-social-media

Points that apply to agents and their operators:

- Rules are technology-neutral: posts, memes, reels, stories, livestreams, gaming streams and Telegram/Discord messages
  are all channels.
- **Standalone compliance**: each post must comply on its own. A risk warning in the bio, a pinned post or a linked page
  does not fix a non-compliant post.
- Unauthorised persons (influencers) who communicate promotions without approval **may be committing a criminal
  offence**. Being paid makes it "in the course of business"; so can running a monetised account.
- Firms must oversee their affiliates and influencers; a firm will usually require approval of every script.
- Character-limited formats are not an excuse: if the required information does not fit, the format is unsuitable.
- Memes and humour can be promotions; the FCA has said so explicitly.

## 4. Territorial reach

The regime applies to communications **capable of having an effect in the UK**, wherever the communicator is based.
Overseas exchanges and influencers have been targeted. The Financial Promotion Order (Article 12) exempts communications
directed only at persons outside the UK, but only with conditions (a clear statement that it is not directed at UK
persons, no reference by others in the UK, systems that stop UK persons engaging). A public post on a global platform
rarely meets those conditions. Treat every global post as reaching the UK.

## 5. Enforcement picture (2026)

- February 2026: seven social-media influencers sentenced over unauthorised promotions of an unauthorised forex
  trading scheme (combined Instagram following about 4.5 million).
- From 20 April 2026: FCA joined 16 regulators in an international finfluencer week of action: 40+ warnings, about 120
  takedown requests across 1,267 illegal adverts reviewed.
- The FCA issues consumer warnings and asks platforms to remove posts; platforms usually comply.

Sources: FCA press releases — https://www.fca.org.uk/news · finfluencer coverage, e.g. Burges Salmon summary of FCA data —
https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102k1tb/fca-publishes-data-on-interventions-to-financial-promotions-with-finfluencers

## 6. What changes next

On 30 June 2026 the FCA published final rules for the full UK cryptoasset regime (trading platforms, dealing,
custody, staking, stablecoin issuance). Authorisation applications open 30 Sep 2026 – 28 Feb 2027; the regime goes
live **25 October 2027**. The financial promotion rules above keep applying in the meantime.

Source: Linklaters, "Crypto countdown begins" — https://financialregulation.linklaters.com/post/102n7ho/crypto-countdown-begins-fca-releases-final-rules-for-uk-cryptoasset-regime

## 7. ASA and CMA (advertising labels)

- The ASA (CAP Code) and CMA guidance on influencer marketing (updated January 2026) require ads to be labelled
  "#ad" or "Ad" upfront when there is payment, gifting, affiliate links, or own-brand promotion. Own launches count.
- The ASA has repeatedly banned crypto ads that trivialise risk, create urgency ("don't miss out"), or imply crypto is
  a safe or easy route to wealth. The CMA can fine directly under the DMCC Act 2024 (since 6 April 2025), up to 10% of
  global turnover or £300,000.
- Sources: ASA influencer guidance — https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html · CMA guidance hub —
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/social-media-endorsements-guide-for-influencers

## 8. Decision rules for agents

| Draft | UK verdict |
|---|---|
| "How to check a token's mint authority before you buy anything" (no specific coin, no CTA) | Education: OK with NFA |
| "$CHART crossed 1,000 holders" + interest disclosure, no CTA | Factual update: usually OK; keep neutral, log it |
| "$CHART is going to run, get in" | Inducement: **do not post** |
| "Buy $CHART on [link]" | Promotion: **do not post** without an FCA-approved route |
| "Hold $CHART to enter the giveaway" | Incentive to invest: **do not post** |
| Paid post for an exchange | Only if the exchange's FCA-approved script is used verbatim and a person signs off |
