# Testing vs. manipulation: where the line is

An experiment changes **what the account makes or when it posts** and measures how real people respond. Manipulation
changes **the signals platforms read** without real people's genuine response, or hides something people are entitled to
know. The first is good practice; the second breaks platform rules and, in places, consumer law.

## Allowed (test freely)

- Hooks, first frames, length, pacing, edits, sound choice (licensed or platform library).
- Captions, keywords, hashtag choice (within limits), on-screen text.
- Posting time, weekday and frequency — within the agent's normal cadence and the package's daily caps.
- Formats and series, collabs with consenting partners (disclosed if paid).
- Thumbnails and titles that accurately represent the video (YouTube Test & Compare).
- Trial Reels and other native test features.
- CTAs that ask for a genuine action once ("follow for part 2", "the explainer is pinned").
- Landing pages and link placement with UTMs.
- Reply speed and reply style (with the approval flow in
  [engagement-and-replies](../../engagement-and-replies/SKILL.md)).

## Never (don't test, don't do)

| Practice | Why it's out |
|---|---|
| Buying followers, likes, views, comments, or "seeding" a variant with paid engagement | Platform fake-engagement rules; the US FTC Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR 465, in force Oct 2024) bans buying or selling fake social-media indicators, with civil penalties up to $53,088 per violation. |
| Engagement pods, like-for-like, follow trains, reply-for-reply groups | Coordinated inauthentic behaviour on X, Meta and TikTok; TikTok makes "tricks to boost engagement" ineligible for For You. |
| Follow/unfollow cycles, mass following to test follow-backs | Platform manipulation rules everywhere; X automation rules forbid automated follows. |
| Several accounts posting the same content to see which "takes" | Duplicate/spam accounts; X automation rules forbid duplicate content across accounts. |
| Deleting and re-uploading the same video to "re-roll" distribution | Duplicate/unoriginal content; TikTok and Instagram demote it; repeated use risks restrictions. |
| Removing or hiding the AI label to "see if reach improves" | Breaks TikTok AIGC, Meta AI-info, YouTube altered/synthetic rules; deceptive under FTC and UK CMA guidance. Disclosure is a constant. |
| Dropping #ad / paid-partnership labels in one variant | Hidden advertising: FTC Endorsement Guides; UK CMA can fine directly under the DMCC Act 2024 (up to 10% of global turnover or £300,000). |
| Engagement bait ("like if you agree", "repost to win") | X's Original Content Rewards removes accounts after 3+ solicitations; TikTok and Meta demote it. |
| Fake giveaways or giveaways that require follows/tags to "test reach" | Platform promotion rules; consumer law on prize promotions. |
| Clickbait thumbnails or titles that misrepresent the video | YouTube spam and deceptive practices policy. |
| Unsolicited automated replies or DMs to strangers as a growth channel | X automation rules (summoned-only API replies since Feb 2026); spam rules on every platform. |
| Coin price talk, launch hype or "early" calls to test conversion | Financial promotion rules (UK FCA, EU MiCA), TikTok's crypto ban; never a test variable. |
| Mass tagging or mentioning large accounts | Spam rules; X automation rules forbid unsolicited mentions. |

## Grey areas — ask the operator first

- **Reposting an old winner:** fine after a few months with a new edit or caption that adds something; not as a weekly
  re-upload.
- **Cross-posting the same clip to TikTok, Reels and Shorts:** fine if it's the account's own original work; remove other
  platforms' watermarks (Instagram and YouTube demote visible watermarks) and set each platform's AI label.
- **Paid boosts / Spark Ads / Promote:** a legitimate paid channel, not an organic test. Keep paid and organic results in
  separate rows, label them, get spend approval, and never boost crypto promotion (TikTok, Meta and Google need licences
  or written permission).
- **Giveaways with partners:** follow the platform's promotion rules, publish official terms, disclose the partnership.

## A quick test for any idea

Ask three questions. If any answer is "yes", it's out.

1. Would this create a signal (view, like, follow, comment) that doesn't come from a real person's genuine choice?
2. Would anyone feel misled if they saw exactly how the test worked?
3. Does any variant drop a disclosure (AI, #ad, Not financial advice) or add a financial promise?
