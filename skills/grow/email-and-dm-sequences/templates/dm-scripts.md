# Opt-in DM scripts

Every script here starts with the other person: they DMed first, commented a keyword, or pressed Start on a bot.
None of them is for cold outreach. The agent may draft; a human approves at least the first reply in every new
conversation. Check the platform limits in references/dm-platform-rules.md before you switch anything on.

## 1. Comment-to-DM keyword (Instagram private reply: one message, within 7 days of the comment)

Post caption line (required, so people know what they're opting into):

```text
Comment GUIDE and I'll DM you the <name of the guide> (one message, no spam). <AI character> is an AI character run by <operator>.
```

The one private reply:

```text
Hi <first name>! Here's the <guide name> you asked for: <link>
<AI character> is an AI character; <operator> runs this account and reads replies.
Want the weekly version by email? <sign-up link> (only if you want it).
```

No second message unless they reply. If they reply, you have 24 hours to answer.

## 2. Someone DMs first with a question

```text
Hi <first name>, thanks for the message. Quick answer: <answer in 1–3 lines>.
More detail here if useful: <link>.
(I'm <AI character>, an AI character. A human on the team, <operator>, checks these messages.)
```

## 3. Someone asks for prices, collabs or business

```text
Thanks! For <collabs / bookings / business>, the best route is <email address or form link>, where <operator> handles
it personally. Rates and availability are there: <link>.
```

## 4. Telegram bot welcome (sent only after the user presses Start)

```text
Hi <first name>, I'm <bot name>, the bot for <AI character> (an AI character run by <operator>).

What I can send you:
/guide — the <guide name>
/weekly — a short weekly update (you can stop it any time)
/stop — stop all messages from this bot

I never ask for wallets, keys, passwords or payments in chat.
```

`/stop` reply:

```text
Done. You won't get any more messages from this bot. Press Start again any time if you change your mind.
```

## 5. Launch note to people who asked to be told (opt-in list only)

Use only for people who explicitly asked ("tell me when it's out") in the last 30 days, and keep a record of who asked.

```text
Hi <first name>, you asked me to tell you when <thing> was out: it's live here: <link>.
That's the only message about it. <AI character> is an AI character; <operator> runs this account.
```

## 6. Someone says stop, no or unsubscribe (any wording)

```text
Got it, no more messages from me. Thanks for letting me know.
```

Then tag them `do-not-message` everywhere you track contacts, and remove them from any DM list or bot audience.

## 7. Scam or abuse in DMs

Do not reply. Report and block in the app. If it impersonates you or the character, pin a warning post
(see [crisis-and-reputation](../../crisis-and-reputation/SKILL.md)).

## Never send

- A DM to anyone who didn't message, comment the keyword, or press Start.
- A follow-up after they stopped replying.
- A link to connect a wallet, claim tokens, or "verify" anything.
- The same message to a list of handles, from any number of accounts.
