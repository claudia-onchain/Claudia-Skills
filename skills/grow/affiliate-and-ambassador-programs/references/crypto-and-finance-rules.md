# Crypto and finance rules for referral programs (checked 2026-10)

Read in step 1 when a program, product or offer touches tokens, exchanges, wallets with trading, trading apps, loans,
credit or investments. The default answer for affiliates and ambassadors is **no**. Full rules:
[crypto-marketing-compliance](../../crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md).

## United Kingdom (FCA)

- Promoting qualifying cryptoassets to UK consumers is a regulated financial promotion (rules in force since
  8 October 2023, PS23/6). They apply on social media; breaching them can be a criminal offence.
  https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/cryptoassets/marketing-uk-consumers
- **Incentives to invest are banned**, "such as 'refer a friend' or new joiner bonuses". An exchange's referral bonus is
  exactly this. https://www.fca.org.uk/publication/policy/ps23-6.pdf
- **Finfluencers:** an unauthorised person who promotes regulated products may commit a criminal offence (FG24/1). The
  FCA has charged and sentenced influencers for unauthorised promotions (seven sentenced in February 2026).
  https://www.fca.org.uk/news/press-releases/influencers-fined-issuing-unauthorised-financial-promotions

## European Union (MiCA)

- Marketing communications for crypto-assets must be clearly identifiable, fair, clear and not misleading, consistent
  with a published white paper, and carry the statement that they haven't been reviewed or approved by a competent
  authority (MiCA Article 7). An affiliate posting for an issuer is doing that issuer's marketing.

## United States

- The FTC's endorsement rules apply in full; securities and commodities rules may also apply to promotions of tokens.
  The SEC charged Kim Kardashian in 2022 for promoting a crypto token without disclosing that she was paid. Treat paid
  token promotion as out of bounds.

## Platforms

- **TikTok:** the archived US/Canada branded content rules prohibit cryptocurrency and NFTs; the current global policy
  restricts financial services to listed markets and bans get-rich-quick offers. Treat crypto referrals on TikTok as
  not allowed.
- **Meta, X, Reddit:** crypto *ads* need approval or licences (see
  [paid-social-ads](../../paid-social-ads/SKILL.md)); organic affiliate posts still need disclosure and fall under the
  laws above.

## What this means in practice

| Offer | Answer |
|---|---|
| Exchange or trading-app referral link with a sign-up bonus | No (UK incentive ban; TikTok; trust) |
| "Ambassador" for a token: posts in exchange for tokens or fees | No |
| Affiliate link to a hardware wallet (a physical product, no trading feature promoted) | Possible, with `#ad`; no claims about investing, no UK incentive wording |
| Commission on your own non-financial product, paid out in USDC | Fine; the payout currency doesn't make the product financial |
| Claudia launchpad creator rewards | Not an affiliate program; never pitched as income for anyone |
