# Example: an affiliate program for the Silver City wallpaper pack

Claudia's team sells the Silver City wallpaper pack (12 phone and 4 desktop wallpapers from her diaries, AI-generated,
$9, 14-day refunds) through their own store. They want creators who make phone-setup and aesthetic-desk content to sell
it on commission, and to make three of them ambassadors. In the same week, an exchange offers Claudia a referral deal.
All creators and companies named here are fictional.

## Step 1: screen

- Product: a digital download, no financial element. Allowed everywhere.
- Fit: phone-setup and desk-aesthetic audiences already ask "where's that wallpaper from?" under Claudia's posts.
- The exchange offer (`"$50 in BTC for every friend who signs up with your code, 40% of their trading fees to you"`):
  a referral bonus for a crypto exchange. UK: a banned incentive to invest. TikTok: not allowed. Trust: Claudia's
  audience didn't follow her for trading. Declined the same day with the template reply from
  [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md).

## Step 2: terms (key lines from `program-terms.md`)

| Term | Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Commission | 30% of net sale ($2.43 on a $9 sale after a ~10% payment fee) | digital goods have room; enough to be worth a story |
| Attribution | last click, 30 days | simple |
| Lock | 30 days after sale (refund window is 14) | no commission on refunds |
| Payout | monthly on the 15th, minimum $25, USDC on Solana or PayPal | small affiliates roll over |
| Ambassadors | 3 people, $150/month + 40% commission, 4 posts a month, 3-month term | the three best from the test round |

## Step 3: tracking

```text
https://shop.useclaudia.xyz/silver-city?ref=aff-014&utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=affiliate&utm_campaign=silvercity-2026-10&utm_content=aff-014
```

Codes: `SILVERNOOR`, `SILVERWES`, … (10% off). Every link was clicked once from a phone and once from a desktop; the
store showed the referral both times.

## Steps 4–5: recruiting and the kit

Twelve invitations, each written to one person who had already posted about phone setups; nine accepted, one failed the
authenticity check (median views at 0.8% of followers, refused analytics). Each got the affiliate kit with:

- Approved claims: "16 wallpapers: 12 phone (1179×2556) and 4 desktop (3840×2160)", "AI-generated from Claudia's
  Silver City diaries", "$9, 14-day refund".
- Disclosure: `Ad · affiliate link: I earn a commission if you buy through this link. Wallpapers are AI-generated.` plus
  the platform label.

## Step 6: Claudia's own post

Claudia's account posts about the pack too. Because it's her own product, that's self-promotion: still "Ad", no
affiliate wording:

```text
Ad · my own shop: the Silver City wallpaper pack is out. 16 wallpapers from the diaries, AI-generated. $9.
```

## Step 7: the first monthly check

| Affiliate | Posts checked | Disclosure | Fraud flags | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| aff-014 (@notesbynour) | 3 | ok | none | — |
| aff-017 | 2 | one TikTok missing "Ad" | none | warned; fixed in 2 hours |
| aff-021 | 1 | ok | 11 orders from the same card as the affiliate's own payout | self-referral: commissions voided, removed |
| others | 9 | ok | none | — |

## Step 8: payout

October commissions locked on 30 November and were paid on 15 December. The batch: 8 affiliates, $612.40 in total, the
largest $188.20 (under the $500 per-affiliate cap). Dana from the team approved the batch. Two new USDC addresses were
confirmed by email reply from the affiliate's known address, then a $1 test transfer each; signatures and the USD value
on the day went into the ledger.

## Step 9: the quarter

- Cost per sale through affiliates: $2.43 commission + $0.40 average discount = $2.83 on a $9 product.
- Top three affiliates: 61% of affiliate sales (made ambassadors for the next term).
- Refund rate on affiliate sales: 3% (store average 4%).
- Disclosure compliance after warnings: 100% of checked posts.
