# Membership platforms: rules, fees and limits (checked 2026-10)

Read in step 2 when choosing a platform, and again before applying. Programs change several times a year; re-check the
linked page and write the date into `templates/membership-plan.md`.

## YouTube channel memberships

- **Eligibility:** 500 subscribers and 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, plus 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours
  in the last 365 days or 3M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days; 18+; living in a country where memberships are
  available; Commerce Product Module accepted; channel not set as made for kids; not a music channel under SRAV.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72857
- **Terms deadline:** channels still on the older Commerce Product Addendum must accept the Commerce Product Module in
  Studio by 31 January 2027, or stop earning from these features from 1 February 2027.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009
- **Levels:** up to 6, each with 1–5 perks. Perks include loyalty badges, custom emoji, members-only posts, videos,
  Shorts, live streams, live chat, members-first videos and a recognition shelf.
  https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7544492
- **Prices (US):** $0.99 to $499.99 across 25 price points. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10119895
- **Revenue share:** 70% of net revenues from memberships (taxes excluded). https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902
- **Perks not allowed:** downloads of content that's on YouTube, in-person 1:1 meetings, perks given to random members
  (contests, lotteries, sweepstakes), perks aimed at children. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7636690
- **AI:** disclose meaningfully altered or generated photorealistic content (Studio → "AI use"); members-only videos
  included. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491

## X Subscriptions

Read from X's help centre via search copies; X blocks automated fetches, so confirm in the app before applying.

- **Eligibility:** 18+, verified email, two-factor authentication, ID verification, active Premium (or Premium Business
  / Organizations), account at least 3 months old, a post in the last 30 days, 2,000+ active Premium followers and 5M
  organic impressions in the last 3 months. https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/subscriptions-policy
- **Share:** up to about 97% of gross until $50,000 in lifetime X earnings, then up to 90%, after in-app purchase fees.
- **Payout:** X Money or a verified Stripe account, $50 minimum.
- **Features:** subscriber badge on replies, subscriber-only posts and Articles. Prices are chosen from X's presets.

## Patreon

- **Fees:** 10% platform fee for pages published after 4 August 2025 (legacy creators keep 5%, 8% or 11% unless they
  unpublish and republish), plus payment processing (2.9% + $0.30 for cards, Apple Pay and US PayPal; 3.9% + $0.30 for
  non-US PayPal), a currency conversion fee and a payout fee.
  https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/22581195376909-Creator-fees-FAQ
- **No-cost membership:** fans can join without paying; posts can be for no-cost members, all members, or public.
- **iOS:** creators on legacy billing must switch to subscription billing by 1 November 2026. New in-app iOS memberships
  pay Apple 30%, dropping to 15% after a year; Patreon charges no processing fee on those but its platform fee applies.
  US members can use web checkout instead. In-app payouts can be pending up to 75 days.
  https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/28801582599181
- **AI:** AI-generated work is allowed. Banned: manipulated or deepfake media made to deceive, sexualised likenesses of
  real people, synthetic non-consensual intimate imagery. https://www.patreon.com/policy/guidelines

## Telegram

- **Star subscriptions:** a private channel's invite link can "Require Monthly Fee"; the period is 30 days. Default
  maximum 10,000 Stars a month. https://core.telegram.org/api/subscriptions and https://core.telegram.org/api/config
- **Paid posts:** photos and videos unlocked with Stars; default maximum 25,000 Stars per post.
- **Withdrawal:** via Fragment, minimum 1,000 Stars, after a 21-day hold; Telegram's config lists 1,000 Stars = $13 at
  withdrawal. Stars expire after 3 years. https://telegram.org/tos/content-creator-rewards
- **Ads:** public channels with 1,000+ subscribers can receive 50% of ad revenue (Telegram may change the share).
- **Bots selling digital goods** must use Stars. https://core.telegram.org/bots/payments-stars

## Discord Server Subscriptions

- **Where:** United States only. https://creator-support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10424143128343
- **Owner:** 18+, good standing, no safety violation in 30 days, verified email and phone, 2FA, US bank account and US ID
  through Stripe. **Server:** Community enabled, MFA for moderation, not primarily NSFW.
- **Tiers and payouts:** 1–3 tiers; 90/10 split; first payout at $100, then $25.
  https://creator-support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10423011974551
- **Fees:** processing on desktop and web plus the platform fee; on iOS and Android the app store's fee is added to the
  member's price. https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/5330075836311-Monetization-Terms
- **Server Shop** (one-time products, premium roles) is a limited US experiment.
- **Not allowed to monetize:** gambling, "facilitation of financial asset transfers or securities ownership", MLM,
  scams, sexual content, politics, third-party promotion, impersonation unless labelled parody, misrepresentative or
  manipulated content, and more. https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10575066024983-Monetization-Policy

## Comparing take-home on a $5 membership (illustrative)

| Platform | Roughly what reaches the operator | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | 70% of net | before currency and tax effects |
| Patreon (web, card) | $5 − 10% − (2.9% + $0.30) ≈ $4.06 | iOS in-app sign-ups lose more |
| X | up to ~97% after in-app fees, below $50k lifetime | in-app fees vary by store |
| Discord (web) | 90% minus processing | US only |
| Telegram | Stars at withdrawal rate | 21-day hold; rate set by Telegram |

These are planning numbers, not a promise of income.
