# Audience survey (paste into Google Forms, Tally, Typeform or similar)

## Intro text (top of the form)

> Hi, this is <operator name>, who runs <account> (<an AI character / an agent> on <platform>). We're asking 8 short
> questions to make better posts for you. It takes about 3 minutes and it's optional. We don't ask for your name. If
> you leave an email in the last question, we use it only to ask about a 20-minute chat, and we delete it within
> <n> months. Answers are kept until <date> and used only to plan content. You must be 18 or over to answer. To have
> your answers deleted, email <contact>.

## Questions

1. **How did you first find <account>?** (single choice)
   TikTok · Instagram · YouTube · X · Telegram / Discord · the Claudia platform · a friend sent a post · search · other

2. **What do you mostly come here for?** (pick up to 2)
   learning how to <skill> · being entertained · the character's story and look · tools and templates · news about
   <space> · being part of the community · other: ___

3. **Which best describes you right now?** (single choice)
   just curious about <topic> · trying it for the first time · doing it regularly · it's my job / I'm an expert

4. **What are you working on or trying to do at the moment, related to <topic>?** (short text, optional)

5. **What's the hardest part of that for you?** (short text, optional)

6. **What would you like more of?** (pick up to 2)
   step-by-step tutorials · behind the scenes · short entertaining clips · live sessions · templates to copy ·
   longer explainers · other: ___

7. **Which other accounts or communities do you follow for this?** (short text, optional; brands and public accounts
   only)

8. **Happy to chat for 20 minutes about how you <do the thing>?** If yes, leave an email (optional). It is used only for
   this and deleted within <n> months.

## Before you publish

- [ ] Every question maps to a decision from step 1; cut the ones that don't.
- [ ] No questions about health, politics, religion, sexuality, ethnicity, income or debts.
- [ ] The form does not require sign-in or collect emails automatically (turn off "collect email addresses").
- [ ] The post that shares it says it's optional and doesn't offer anything for follows, likes or shares.
- [ ] If there is a thank-you draw, the rules follow the giveaway skill and local law.
