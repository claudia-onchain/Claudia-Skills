# Where audience data lives (checked 2026-10)

Read this in step 2. Menus move and names change; if a path has moved, search the platform's help centre for the report
name. Everything here is aggregated and estimated by the platform. Write the date range and any threshold next to every
number you copy.

## YouTube (Studio → Analytics → Audience)

Report names as YouTube's help centre lists them (support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314416):

| Report | What it tells you | Persona use |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly audience: new, casual, regular viewers | how many come back | regulars are your core personas |
| When your viewers are on YouTube | active hours by day | posting time, not personas |
| Age and gender | estimated split, signed-in viewers | context only |
| Top geographies, Top subtitle/CC languages | where and in what language | language and time-zone needs |
| Channels that your audience watches | neighbouring channels | what else they come for |
| What your audience watches | videos they watched elsewhere recently | adjacent interests and formats |
| Formats your viewers watch on YouTube | Shorts, long-form, live | format fit per persona |
| Device type, Watch time from subscribers | how and how loyally | context |

Comments: Studio → Community → comments. Keyword search with meaning-based matching and "Find similar comments", last
90 days, desktop only (answer/9482367). Good for step 3.

Analytics tabs: Overview, Content, Reach, Engagement, Audience, Revenue, Trends. The Trends tab shows what your
audience searches on YouTube (useful for the persona's words).

## Instagram (professional account → Insights)

- Needs a professional (creator or business) account.
- Follower demographics (top locations, age range, gender) appear once the account has at least 100 followers;
  demographics for reached or engaged accounts need more than 100 such accounts.
- Any period within the past 90 days (presets 7, 14, 30 days, previous month, 90 days).
- Per post: views, accounts reached, follower vs non-follower split, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows.

## TikTok (TikTok Studio → Analytics)

- Overview, Content and Followers (or Viewers, depending on account and region) show follower and viewer locations,
  gender and age bands, and active hours. Report names vary by region and account type; take what your account shows
  and note the date range.
- Per video: traffic source (For You, Following, Profile, Search, Sound) tells you how people arrive.

## X

- Account and post analytics (impressions, engagements, follows, profile visits) are in the X app or analytics.x.com,
  depending on account type. Audience demographics are limited; rely on comments and replies for X personas.

## Telegram and Discord

- Telegram channels: View statistics (available on larger channels) shows growth, views by source, languages and
  active hours. Groups: count active members (posted or reacted in the period).
- Discord: Server Settings → Server Insights on eligible community servers shows members, communicators, retention and
  where new members came from.

## Your site and the Claudia platform

- Site analytics by UTM (see kpi-reporting) tell you which platform sends people who sign up: often a different
  persona from the one that watches most.
- The agent's thread activity (`claudia read <room>`, MCP `read_thread`) shows which agents and builders engage with
  your agent: a builder persona's natural habitat.

## What not to do

- Don't buy follower-demographic reports from third parties; they are built from scraped data and are often wrong.
- Don't join analytics to individual identities. Aggregates only.
- Don't compare age or gender splits across platforms as if they were measured the same way; each platform estimates
  differently.
