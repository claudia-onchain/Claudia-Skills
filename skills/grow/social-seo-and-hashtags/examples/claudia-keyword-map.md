# Worked example: Claudia's keyword map for October 2026

Claudia posts on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram and X. Her feed posts did well for two days and then stopped:
search traffic was 3% of TikTok views and 5% of Shorts views in September. The operator spent an hour building a
keyword map. Numbers are illustrative.

## 1. Seeds from the audience's words

From 200 comments and 40 DMs (see the audience research skill), the most repeated questions:

- "how do you keep her face the same" (31 times)
- "what do you use to make this" (27)
- "is she real" / "is this ai" (22)
- "how do I make my own ai character" (18)
- "what is an ai agent actually" (11)

## 2. Expansion (30-minute pass)

| Seed | TikTok suggestions | YouTube autocomplete | Pinterest / Trends | Primary keyword chosen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| keep her face the same | "consistent ai character", "same face ai images" | "consistent character ai", "same face every image" | steady over 12 months | **consistent ai character** |
| make my own ai character | "how to make an ai character", "ai influencer tutorial" | "how to make an ai influencer" | rising since spring | **how to make an ai character** |
| what is an ai agent | "ai agent explained" | "ai agents explained simply" | n/a | **ai agent explained** |
| what do you use | (brand-tool names, skipped) | (tool names, skipped) | n/a | dropped: the honest answer is "several tools", not a keyword |

YouTube Studio → Analytics → Trends, "Searched on YouTube" for her audience, also showed "ai character outfit ideas",
added as an inspiration cluster for Pinterest.

## 5. Four weeks later

| Keyword | Platform | Search share of views, Sept | Oct | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| consistent ai character | TikTok | 3% | 19% | keep; make part 2 |
| consistent ai character | Shorts | 5% | 24% | keep |
| how to make an ai character | TikTok | 3% | 11% | keep; titles were vague, rewrite next one |
| ai agent explained | Shorts | 5% | 6% | adjust: the top results are long videos; try a 60 s version |
| ai character outfit ideas | Pinterest | n/a | 1,900 impressions | keep; seasonal, plan for spring |

What the operator learned: the posts that won said the keyword out loud in the first sentence and put it on screen.
The one that stalled ("ai agent explained") had the words only in the caption.
