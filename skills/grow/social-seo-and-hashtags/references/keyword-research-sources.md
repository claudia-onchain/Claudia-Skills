# Keyword research sources

Read this in step 2. Every source here is either the operator's own logged-in app or an official tool. Agents don't
scrape search results or run headless browsers against the apps; the operator browses and pastes, or exports.

## The 30-minute pass (per month)

1. Take the 20–40 seed phrases from step 1.
2. For each seed, in this order, write down what you see in the keyword map's `evidence` column.
3. Stop when a cluster has a primary keyword that shows up in at least two places.

| # | Source | How | What to record | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | TikTok search bar | Type the seed slowly; read the suggestions; open results; scroll to "Others searched for" | Suggestions, the top 5 results' format and age | 5 min |
| 2 | TikTok Creator Search Insights | Search "Creator Search Insights" in the app (select regions) | Searches by topic, content-gap flags | 5 min |
| 3 | YouTube search bar | Type the seed; read autocomplete; check the top Shorts and videos | Suggestions; whether results are old (an opening) | 5 min |
| 4 | YouTube Studio → Analytics → Trends | "Searched on YouTube", top searches, content gaps for your audience (last 28 days) | Phrases your own viewers search | 5 min |
| 5 | Instagram search | Type the seed; read suggested keywords and accounts | Suggestions; which accounts own the term | 3 min |
| 6 | Pinterest search + Pinterest Trends | Suggestions under the search bar; trends.pinterest.com for seasonality by region | Rising vs steady terms; best months | 4 min |
| 7 | Google Trends | Compare 3–5 phrasings; filter to YouTube Search where useful | Which phrasing people use more | 3 min |

Tips:

- Search logged in as the brand account and also logged out or in a private window; suggestions are personalised.
- Set region to the audience's top countries (from analytics), not your own.
- Old top results (a year or more) on a phrase with suggestions = a gap you can fill with a fresh answer.
- Treat AI idea generators in studio tools as prompts to think, not keywords: they don't show search volume.

## Intent labels

| Intent | Looks like | Best format |
|---|---|---|
| how-to | "how to make…", "how do I…" | step-by-step short or carousel, spoken steps |
| what-is | "what is an ai agent", "… meaning" | 30–45 s explainer, one definition up front |
| comparison | "x vs y", "best … for …" | side-by-side, honest about trade-offs, no paid ranking without `#ad` |
| inspiration | "… ideas", "… aesthetic", "… outfit" | image sets, Pins, photo dumps |
| news | "… update", "… new feature" | fast, dated post; goes stale quickly |

## What not to use

- Third-party tools that sell "TikTok search volume" built from scraped data.
- Other creators' names or handles as keywords.
- Coin search terms with return language ("next 100x", "will it pump"). They pull the wrong audience and push captions
  toward promises.
