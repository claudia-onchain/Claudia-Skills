# Portrait lighting recipes

Read this when building a new portrait set-up or when a portrait looks flat, glossy or "generated". Each recipe gives
the physical set-up (so you can describe it like a photographer), the prompt sentence, and when it suits Claudia.

## Classic patterns

| Pattern | Set-up | Prompt sentence | Suits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Loop** | key 30–45° to one side, slightly above eye level; small nose shadow loops toward the cheek | "soft key light 40 degrees to camera left, slightly above eye level, a small loop shadow from the nose" | default flattering portrait, studio |
| **Rembrandt** | key 45° side, higher; triangle of light on the far cheek | "Rembrandt lighting: key high at 45 degrees left, a small triangle of light on her shadowed cheek" | moody editorial, B&W |
| **Butterfly / paramount** | key straight ahead and above; butterfly shadow under the nose | "large beauty dish directly above the lens, a small butterfly shadow under the nose, white bounce below the chin" | beauty close-ups, avatars |
| **Split** | key 90° side | "hard side light from camera left splitting her face in half" | dramatic posters only; reads harsh for her brand |
| **Broad vs short** | light the side of the face toward / away from camera | "short lighting: the side of her face turned away from the lens is lit" | short lighting slims and adds age — useful against teenage drift |
| **Clamshell** | key above + fill below | "clamshell lighting with a softbox above and a silver reflector below" | glossy press shots |

## Natural and practical light

| Recipe | Prompt sentence | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| Window daylight | "soft overcast daylight from a large window at camera left, gentle falloff to the right, no fill" | her day room, desk mornings |
| Golden-hour backlight | "the low sun behind her rims her hair in copper, a soft flare, window bounce lights her face" | sunset bedroom, rooftop |
| Blue hour | "cool ambient sky light, city windows glowing behind, a warm practical lamp on her face" | balcony, rooftop |
| Tungsten lamp key | "a brass table lamp with a pleated shade lights her from the left, warm 2700K glow, deep soft shadows" | vintage lounge |
| Screen glow | "the laptop screen lights her face from below in cool white, a rose-pink desk lamp rim behind her" | desk at night, network banner |
| Candle / dinner | "candlelight from below frame, warm flicker, background in deep shadow" | wine-glass wink tile |
| Overcast exterior | "flat overcast light, wet surfaces bouncing a soft fill up into her face" | rain lot |
| On-camera flash | "direct on-camera flash, hard shadow behind her on the wall, slightly blown highlights, snapshot feel" | party, mirror, photo-wall tiles |

## Coloured / club light

| Recipe | Prompt sentence |
|---|---|
| Club haze | "blue-violet ambient haze, moving-head beams slicing through smoke behind her" |
| Rose rim | "a rose-pink rim light #ff6fa5 from behind camera right outlining her hair and shoulder" |
| Neon key | "a pink neon sign just out of frame casts colour on one side of her face, cool blue on the other" |
| Strobe freeze | "a strobe freezes her mid-laugh, crisp against motion-blurred lights" |

Keep skin readable under coloured light: "her skin tone stays natural in the key; colour only in the rims and background."

## Modifiers vocabulary

softbox · octabox · beauty dish · umbrella · strip light (rim) · grid (controls spill) · scrim / diffusion frame ·
silver/white/gold reflector · negative fill (black flag) · gel (CTO for warm, CTB for cool) · haze machine.

## Studio backdrops for her

| Backdrop | Prompt | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Cobalt seamless | "seamless saturated cobalt-blue paper backdrop" | the owner's portrait; contrasts the copper hair |
| Warm grey | "warm mid-grey seamless backdrop" | neutral ref-pack shots |
| Olive | "muted olive-green painted canvas backdrop" | matches the cami |
| Near-black | "near-black backdrop with a faint gradient" | press dark mode, matches site #0a0b0d |

## Colour temperature cheat sheet

candle 1900K · tungsten lamp 2700K · halogen 3200K · golden hour 3500K · daylight 5600K · overcast 6500K · shade 7500K.
Mixing a warm key with a cool ambient (lamp + blue hour) is the most "her" palette.
