# Announcement kit: copy and alt text for each visual

Replace every `<placeholder>`. Every post gets: what it is, that the art is AI-generated, the chain and full contract
address, "Not financial advice.", no promises. Add `#ad` and who paid if anything is paid or gifted. Draft through
`@useclaudia/social` or the `claudia post` command with `--dry-run` first; a person approves before it publishes.

## X (16:9 card)

```text
<Name> ($<TICKER>) is live on <launchpad> (<chain>).
<one line about the idea, no price words>
CA: <full contract address>
Art is AI-generated. Not financial advice.
```

Alt text: `AI-generated image: launch card for <Name> ($<TICKER>), <mascot> on a <background>, ticker as the only text.`

## 9:16 clip caption (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

Check the platform's crypto rules first: some platforms restrict or remove coin promotion
([crypto-marketing-compliance](../../../grow/crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md)). If in doubt, post the clip on X
and Telegram only.

```text
<Name> is out. <one line about the mascot or idea>. AI-generated. Not financial advice.
```

On-screen text: none, or the name only.
