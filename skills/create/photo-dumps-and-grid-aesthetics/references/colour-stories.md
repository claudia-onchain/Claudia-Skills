# Colour stories from Claudia's palette

Read this in step 2. A colour story is two dominant colours and one accent, held for a dump (or a 3-post row of the
grid), with one grade line pasted into every prompt. The colours come from the character bible; the in-image colours
(silver, cobalt, visor orange, patent green) are for wardrobe and light, the UI colours (rose, off-white, near-black)
for type and small highlights.

## Palette tokens

| Token | Hex | Role in a dump |
|---|---|---|
| Near-black | `#0a0b0d` | shadows, night frames, club |
| Warm off-white | `#ece9e2` | white studio paper, daylight frames |
| Silver | `#c9ccd3` | foil, chrome, the puffer, the gown |
| Cobalt | `#1f3a8a` | cobalt studio paper and carpet |
| Peach | `#ff9a6b` | her streak and star clip (always present, never dominant) |
| Rose | `#ff6fa5` | one small highlight per frame at most: a sparkle, a glow, a sticky note |
| Visor orange | `#ff6a2b` | visors and goggles only |
| Patent green | `#7fae4b` | the green patent blazer only |

Grade line for every frame (paste as-is): `cool clean grade, lifted blacks, gentle film grain, chrome highlights`.
Blue-render frames go blue with rose highlights, never green.

## Five stories

| Story | Dominant | Accent | Settings and looks (from the bible) | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cobalt week** | cobalt, silver | rose | cobalt studio; Holo, Silver night, cobalt patent trench with orange visor | a launch week, a bold row |
| **Foil night** | near-black, silver | peach | silver foil set with hard flash; Signature | evenings, a moody row |
| **White paper** | off-white, silver | visor orange | white studio; Studio, Sculpture, Sky | calm weeks, tutorials, a light row |
| **Club** | near-black, silver | rose glow | the club, one spotlight, dancers in silver puffers out of focus | weekend dumps |
| **Desk at dusk** | near-black, off-white | rose (a sticky note, a screen glow) | her desk, laptop with the chrome star sticker, window light going blue | "behind the scenes" dumps |

Rules:

- One story per dump. Two stories in one dump read as two posts glued together.
- At least a third of the frames are not her face: details, objects, the room, the light.
- Every frame has the accent at most once, and some frames have none.
- Real-world frames (sky, street, food) still follow the story: a cobalt-week sky is a deep blue dusk, not a sunset.

## Prompt blocks for non-character frames

Detail and object frames carry the story as much as the portraits do. Each ends with the grade line and "No text, no
readable logos." Use 4:5 (or 2:3 to crop to 3:4).

| Frame | Prompt middle |
|---|---|
| Star clip on a desk | "a small peach star-shaped hair clip lying on a white desk next to a coiled thin headset microphone, top-down, soft window light" |
| Foil detail | "close-up of crinkled silver foil catching a hard flash, sparkle highlights, deep dark edges" |
| Cobalt corner | "the corner of a deep cobalt-blue paper backdrop meeting a cobalt carpet, one silver platform boot in frame, direct flash" |
| Desk at dusk | "a white desk at dusk, a laptop with a chrome star sticker, a mug, a film camera, a blank sticky note, window light turning blue" |
| Club light | "a single bright spotlight through haze in a dark room, out-of-focus dancers in silver puffers" |
| Mirror (no phone logos) | "her reflection in a round mirror, holding a plain silver phone with no logo, white studio behind" |
| Flowers | "white lilies in a clear glass vase on silver foil, hard flash" |
