# Music and SFX prompt bank

Copy, adjust BPM/length, generate 2–3 variations. Every music prompt ends with "instrumental, no vocals" unless the
post needs singing (rare — vocals fight her voice-over and raise claim risk). No artist, song or label names, ever.

## Music — Claudia's core clip types

1. **Studio vlog** — `warm lo-fi hip-hop bed, 82 BPM, dusty Rhodes chords, soft vinyl crackle, brushed drums, round sub bass, gentle and unobtrusive, steady loop with no build, leaves room for a voice, instrumental, no vocals`
2. **Studio vlog, brighter** — `sunny synth-pop bed, 96 BPM, clean chorus guitar, soft claps, warm bass, playful glockenspiel accents, light and conversational, loops every 4 bars, instrumental, no vocals`
3. **Club / DJ booth** — `deep house club groove, 124 BPM, four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat open hats, rolling bassline, short filtered chord stabs, big-room reverb as heard from the dancefloor, 8-bar build into a drop at 6 seconds, instrumental`
4. **Club, darker** — `hypnotic tech-house, 126 BPM, punchy kick, clipped percussion, acid-tinged bassline, rising noise sweep into a drop at 4 seconds, dark and euphoric, instrumental`
5. **Visor reveal** — `late-70s disco-soul lounge groove, 108 BPM, wah guitar, warm bass, string pad swell, light tambourine, intimate and playful, a small lift on bar 3 for a reveal moment, instrumental, no vocals`
6. **Visor reveal, slower** — `smoky jazz-lounge vamp, 88 BPM, brushed snare, upright bass, muted trumpet-like synth lead, soft studio warmth, a single cymbal swell at 5 seconds, instrumental`
7. **Club night** — `bright indie-pop run, 140 BPM, jangly clean electric guitars, driving floor-tom beat, airy synth pad, euphoric and a little chaotic, starts immediately at full energy, instrumental, no vocals`
8. **Silver foil, wistful** — `dreamy shoegaze-lite, 92 BPM, washed reverb guitars, soft drum machine, shimmering foil mood, bittersweet but warm, slow fade-in over 1 second, instrumental`
