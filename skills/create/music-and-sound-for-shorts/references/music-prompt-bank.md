# Music and SFX prompt bank

Copy, adjust BPM/length, generate 2–3 variations. Every music prompt ends with "instrumental, no vocals" unless the
post needs singing (rare — vocals fight her voice-over and raise claim risk). No artist, song or label names, ever.

## Music — Claudia's core clip types

1. **Bedroom vlog** — `warm lo-fi hip-hop bed, 82 BPM, dusty Rhodes chords, soft vinyl crackle, brushed drums, round sub bass, gentle and unobtrusive, steady loop with no build, leaves room for a voice, instrumental, no vocals`
2. **Bedroom vlog, brighter** — `sunny bedroom-pop bed, 96 BPM, clean chorus guitar, soft claps, warm bass, playful glockenspiel accents, light and conversational, loops every 4 bars, instrumental, no vocals`
3. **Club / DJ booth** — `deep house club groove, 124 BPM, four-on-the-floor kick, offbeat open hats, rolling bassline, short filtered chord stabs, big-room reverb as heard from the dancefloor, 8-bar build into a drop at 6 seconds, instrumental`
4. **Club, darker** — `hypnotic tech-house, 126 BPM, punchy kick, clipped percussion, acid-tinged bassline, rising noise sweep into a drop at 4 seconds, dark and euphoric, instrumental`
5. **Lounge reveal** — `late-70s disco-soul lounge groove, 108 BPM, wah guitar, warm bass, string pad swell, light tambourine, intimate and playful, a small lift on bar 3 for a reveal moment, instrumental, no vocals`
6. **Lounge, slower** — `smoky jazz-lounge vamp, 88 BPM, brushed snare, upright bass, muted trumpet-like synth lead, tungsten-lamp warmth, a single cymbal swell at 5 seconds, instrumental`
7. **Rain run** — `bright indie-pop run, 140 BPM, jangly clean electric guitars, driving floor-tom beat, airy synth pad, euphoric and a little chaotic, starts immediately at full energy, instrumental, no vocals`
8. **Rain, wistful** — `dreamy shoegaze-lite, 92 BPM, washed reverb guitars, soft drum machine, rain-window mood, bittersweet but warm, slow fade-in over 1 second, instrumental`

## Music — other recurring moods

9. **Sunset bedroom / NYC skyline** — `golden-hour chillwave, 100 BPM, warm analog synth pads, gated snare, soft arpeggio, nostalgic city-dusk feeling, instrumental`
10. **Desk at night / building the platform** — `focused downtempo electronica, 90 BPM, soft pulsing synth bass, clicky percussion like keyboard taps, glassy plucks, calm and determined, instrumental`
11. **Launch / announcement** — `uplifting electronic build, 120 BPM, rising arpeggios, claps on 2 and 4, bright pad, one clean impact hit at 7 seconds then a held chord, instrumental`
12. **Travel montage** — `breezy Mediterranean house, 118 BPM, nylon guitar riff, soft shaker, warm sub, sun-bleached and easygoing, instrumental`
13. **Airplane window** — `ambient drone, slow, airy, high soft pads, distant piano notes, weightless, no percussion, instrumental`
14. **Cat chaos / comedy** — `bouncy pizzicato strings and bassoon, 112 BPM, cartoonish but gentle, quick stops for comic timing every 2 bars, instrumental`
15. **Storytime tension** — `minimal suspense bed, 70 BPM, ticking hi-hat, low pulsing synth, a suspended chord, room for narration, instrumental`
16. **Outro / loop** — `soft lo-fi tag, 85 BPM, 4 bars, ends on the tonic chord with a clean tail so it loops back to the start, instrumental`
