# UGC shot checklist

Run per clip, before it goes to editing.

## Feels filmed
- [ ] Phone-camera lens words (24mm front camera / propped phone), no dolly/crane/studio words
- [ ] One imperfection cue (handshake, exposure breathing, focus hunt, raindrop) — not three
- [ ] Light matches the place (window, lamp, street) — no studio rim light indoors
- [ ] She looks away at least once; she isn't posing
- [ ] Action starts at 0 s (hook frame is moving)

## Still her
- [ ] "28-year-old woman (adult)" in both prompts; reads 26–30 in every frame
- [ ] Bob + heavy bangs, copper panels only, orange clip, thin gold hoops
- [ ] Mirror/selfie flip accepted; two copper panels rejected
- [ ] Selfie hand: five fingers, bracelet intact, phone edge not melting

## Sound
- [ ] Ambient named; one short line max; "no music" unless the music is in the scene
- [ ] If the clip will be lip-synced: generated silent (`meta.audio: false`)

## Safe and honest
- [ ] No fake platform UI, counts, badges, notifications
- [ ] No testimonial/review framing; paid content has `#ad` and stays obviously Claudia
- [ ] Background people blurred/anonymous; no real private address or recognisable private home
- [ ] Nothing sexualised; tongue-out/lip moments brief and playful
- [ ] AI label will be set (TikTok AIGC · YouTube synthetic · Meta AI info · X made_with_ai)
