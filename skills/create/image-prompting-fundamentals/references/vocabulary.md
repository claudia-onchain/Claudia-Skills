# Prompt vocabulary that models respond to

Read this when you need the precise word for a lens, light, film look or composition. Each row says what the word does
to the image, so you can pick on purpose.

## Shot size

| Term | Frame | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| extreme close-up | eyes/lips only | detail inserts, thumbnails with one emotion |
| close-up | face to chin, a little hair | banners, reaction stills |
| medium close-up | head and shoulders | talking-to-camera, portraits |
| medium shot | waist up | outfit + expression, UGC stills |
| cowboy / three-quarter shot | mid-thigh up | tartan skirt + top together |
| full shot | head to toe | wardrobe, mirror selfies |
| wide / establishing | subject small in the place | travel, rooftops, rain lot |

## Angle and position

eye level (neutral, honest) · slightly high angle (softer, more intimate; overuse reads young — avoid for Claudia
close-ups) · low angle (confident, club energy) · mattress height / table height (banner intimacy) · over-the-shoulder
(desk scenes, screens) · Dutch tilt 5–10° (club chaos; use rarely) · top-down / flat lay (desk props) · selfie arm's
length, slightly above eye level (UGC) · mirror selfie, phone covering part of the face.

## Lenses (full-frame equivalent)

| Lens | Effect | When |
|---|---|---|
| 16–24 mm | wide, stretches edges and noses | rooms, crowds; never for beauty close-ups |
| 24–26 mm | phone front camera look | UGC selfies |
| 35 mm | natural, environmental, slight intimacy | banners, lifestyle |
| 50 mm | "human eye", flattering | medium shots |
| 85 mm | compression, creamy background | portraits, headshots |
| 100–135 mm | strong compression, isolates face | studio beauty, telephoto candid |
| macro 100 mm | texture detail | jewellery, pendant, freckles |

Aperture: f/1.4–2 (very shallow, dreamy) · f/2.8 (portrait default) · f/5.6–8 (group, scene sharpness) · f/11+ (landscape).
Focus words: "focus on her eyes", "background softly out of focus", "rack focus" (video), "deep focus".

## Camera bodies (they set the "texture")

Fujifilm X-T5 / X100VI (crisp, warm colour science) · Sony A7 IV (clean modern) · Canon EOS R5 (glossy commercial) ·
Leica M11 (contrasty, editorial) · iPhone front camera (UGC realism: slight noise, HDR skies, wide) · Contax T2 (point-and-
shoot flash snapshots) · disposable camera with flash (party nights, red-eye, hard shadows).

## Light

| Term | What it does |
|---|---|
| golden hour backlight | warm rim on hair, flare, glowing skin |
| blue hour | cool ambient, city lights start to pop |
| soft window light from camera left | gentle, directional, natural |
| overcast daylight | even, low contrast, honest (her day room) |
| tungsten lamp key | warm, cosy pools of light (lounge) |
| practical lights | lamps, screens, neon in frame that light the subject |
| moving-head beams, haze | club volume light |
| neon rim light (rose #ff6fa5 / blue-violet) | tech and club edges |
| on-camera flash | party snapshot realism, hard shadow |
| Rembrandt / loop / butterfly lighting | classic portrait patterns (see photoreal-portrait-prompts) |
| three-point softbox setup | studio, clean |
| bounce fill, negative fill | open shadows / deepen one side |

## Colour and grade words

warm teal-and-amber (avoid for her; reads generic) · warm skin, lifted blacks · rose and copper accents ·
blue-violet neon with rose highlights · desaturated greens · split-toned · HEX codes tied to objects (FLUX.2:
"the jumper is #111111", "rim light #ff6fa5").

## Composition

rule of thirds · centre-framed symmetry · negative space on the left for a wordmark · leading lines (rain lot bollards,
loading-dock doors) · foreground framing (plants, a laptop lid, a wine glass) · layered depth (foreground prop, subject,
skyline) · face in the upper third for 9:16 (leave room for captions and UI).

## Texture and realism words that help

natural skin texture with visible pores · fine flyaway hairs · slight lens imperfections · fabric weave visible ·
condensation on glass · wet asphalt reflections · fingerprints on a phone screen. Words that hurt: flawless, perfect,
hyper-detailed, 8k, ultra-HD, masterpiece, octane render (for photoreal work).
