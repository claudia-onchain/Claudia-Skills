# Credits, real brands and disclosure for AI lookbooks (checked 2026-10)

Read this in step 7, and before any lookbook that features a real brand's product. Orientation, not legal advice.

## Three kinds of garment, three kinds of credit

| What's in the image | Credit line | Labels |
|---|---|---|
| **Fictional** wardrobe (Claudia's looks, your character's own designs) | `wardrobe: <character>'s own (AI-designed, not for sale)` | AI label |
| A **real product**, rendered by AI from the brand's own product photos, with the brand's written OK | `wearing: <Brand> <item name> (AI-generated image; the real item may differ)` + `gifted` / `paid partnership` where true | AI label + `#ad` and the paid-partnership toggle if paid, gifted or affiliate |
| A real product you were **not** given and have **no** deal for | don't render it. Write about it, link it, or photograph the real item | — |

Never credit a real brand on a garment it didn't make ("wearing <luxury brand>" on a generated gown). That is a false
claim about the product and uses the brand's name to sell your post.

## Real products: render them accurately

- Use the brand's product photos as refs only with written permission, and keep it with the files.
- Colour, cut, fabric, length and details must match the real item. An AI render that makes a product look better than it
  is can mislead buyers. The UK ASA ruled in 2021 (Skinny Tan and Tanologist influencer stories) that beauty filters
  which exaggerate a product's effect make an ad misleading, even when the filter is named. Treat AI rendering of a real
  product the same way: show it as it is.
- No generated logos. If the brand wants its logo visible, place the brand's official logo file in the edit, as the
  brand's guidelines allow.
- Fit claims ("true to size", "flattering on everyone") can't come from a generated body. Don't make them.

## Paid, gifted, affiliate

- US FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255, revised 2023): a material connection (payment, free product, affiliate
  commission, family or employment ties) must be disclosed clearly and close to the claim; virtual influencers count as
  endorsers. Use `#ad` at the start and the platform's paid-partnership tool.
- UK CMA / ASA and EU consumer law expect the same: clear, upfront, not buried in hashtags.
- `@useclaudia/social`: set `labels.ad: true` to add `#ad` and the native paid-partnership flag where the network has one.
  Details: [brand-deals-and-sponsorships](../../../grow/brand-deals-and-sponsorships/SKILL.md).

## AI disclosure: prominent, not a footnote

- In August 2025, US Vogue ran a Guess ad with AI-generated models disclosed in small print; readers and creators pushed
  back hard. A footnote is technically a disclosure and practically a trust problem. Put "AI-generated" where people
  read: the image tag, the first line of the caption or the credit, and the platform label.
- Platform labels: Instagram "AI info" (`is_ai_generated`), TikTok "AI-generated content" (`is_aigc`), YouTube altered or
  synthetic content, text label elsewhere ([ai-disclosure-and-provenance](../../ai-disclosure-and-provenance/SKILL.md)).
- EU AI Act Article 50(4), applying from 2 August 2026: realistic content that could pass as a real person must be
  disclosed as AI-generated.

## Custom characters and real models

- A custom character for a lookbook is invented: an adult (25+), a face described from scratch, never "looks like" a
  real model, celebrity or creator, never built from a real person's photos. Distinctness test: change at least 4 of the
  5 anchors if starting from Claudia's template ([claudia-character-bible](../../claudia-character-bible/SKILL.md) step 7).
- Working with a real model's likeness is a different job with its own contracts. In New York, the Fashion Workers Act
  (in force since 19 June 2025) requires separate, explicit written consent before a model's digital replica is created
  or used, stating the use, purpose, duration and pay. This skill doesn't cover digital replicas of real people.

## Credits block (copy into the caption or the last slide)

```text
look <n>: <look name>
wardrobe: <character>'s own, AI-designed, not for sale            ← fictional
wearing: <Brand> <item> · gifted · AI-generated image; the real item may differ   ← real, with permission
set: <studio / world name> · images: AI-generated
#ad (only if paid, gifted or affiliate)
```
