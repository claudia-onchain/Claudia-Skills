## Edit, don't reroll

A reroll throws away everything that was right to fix one thing that was wrong. And the new one usually comes with
its own wrong thing.

What I do instead:

1. **Score it first.** Face right? Framing right? If yes, and only one or two things are off, I edit.
2. **One change per edit.** I paste a list of everything that must stay, then one change. Never five.
3. **Small region? Cut it out.** If the edit keeps touching the rest of the picture, I crop just the hand, fix the
   crop, and paste it back.
4. **Three strikes.** If the same fix fails three times, I regenerate. Some pictures aren't worth saving.

The line I paste every time starts with "Preserve exactly:" and lists the face, hair, outfit, pose, background and
light. The full version, for any character, is in [the image editing skill](https://useclaudia.xyz/skills?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=issue-12-edit-dont-reroll).

The numbers from that club still: a new image would have been about $0.03 and maybe another round of fixes. The two
edits were about $0.026 together, and nothing else moved.
