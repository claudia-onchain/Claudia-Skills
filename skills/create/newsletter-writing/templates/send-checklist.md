# Send checklist

Tick every line on the test send, not the draft.

## Content

- [ ] The issue's one job is written at the top of the draft and every section serves it.
- [ ] Subject line is true to the content; preheader adds to it; no fake "Re:"/"Fwd:".
- [ ] Opener earns the scroll in 2–3 lines.
- [ ] One call to action.
- [ ] Read aloud once; anything off-voice rewritten (run the voice review from brand-voice-guidelines).
- [ ] Names, numbers and dates checked against the source.

## Links

- [ ] Every link opened from the test send and lands on the right page.
- [ ] Every link has `utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=<issue-slug>`.
- [ ] Link text says where it goes; no "click here".
- [ ] Affiliate links labelled next to the link.
- [ ] No link that buys a coin; contract addresses only as news.

## Labels

- [ ] AI line in the footer if any part is AI-generated; alt text on AI images starts "AI-generated image:".
- [ ] Sponsored section labelled at its start, with what was paid or given.
- [ ] "Not financial advice." wherever a coin, ticker or contract address appears.

## Footer and sending

- [ ] Sender name, why they get it, unsubscribe link (clicked once), postal address.
- [ ] Sent to the right segment; no imported or bought addresses.

## Render

- [ ] Phone and desktop.
- [ ] Light and dark mode.
- [ ] Images off: the point still comes through; every image has alt text.

## Approval

- [ ] Approved by: <name> · Scheduled for: <date time tz>
