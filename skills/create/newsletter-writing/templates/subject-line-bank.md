# Subject-line bank

Write five, pick one, keep one as a test variant. Every subject must be true to what is inside.

## Formulas

| Formula | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Specific number | `<number> <things>, <surprising part>` | `41 videos. 6 did 80% of the views` |
| The question readers asked | `<their question, in their words>` | `"how do you keep her face the same?"` |
| Before / after | `<old way> → <new way>` | `4 hours of edits → 20 minutes` |
| The one thing | `the one <thing> I'd keep` | `the one caption rule I'd keep` |
| Behind the scenes | `what <event> looked like from <inside>` | `what launch week looked like from the queue` |
| Plain announcement | `<thing> is out: <what it does>` | `new skill: fix an image instead of remaking it` |
| Story cliff | `<moment>, and then <turn>` | `my agent posted at 3am, and then it worked` |
| Mistake | `I got <thing> wrong` | `I got the posting time wrong for a month` |

## Preheaders

The preheader is the grey line after the subject in most inboxes. It adds a second reason to open:

| Subject | Preheader |
|---|---|
| `41 videos. 6 did 80% of the views` | `here's what those six share, and the calendar I rebuilt around them` |
| `new skill: fix an image instead of remaking it` | `one change per edit, and why rerolls cost more than you think` |
| `I got the posting time wrong for a month` | `the test, the boring result, and what I changed anyway` |

If you leave it empty, many inboxes show the first line of the body instead, so make the opener count either way.

## Never

- Fake replies or forwards: `Re:`, `Fwd:` on a first send.
- Urgency that isn't real: `last chance`, `ending tonight` when nothing ends.
- Money or coin hype: prices, `100x`, `moon`, `don't miss this coin`, `$TICKER is about to`.
- All caps, three exclamation marks, or emoji walls. Claudia uses one emoji at most, from her set.
- A subject the issue doesn't deliver.
