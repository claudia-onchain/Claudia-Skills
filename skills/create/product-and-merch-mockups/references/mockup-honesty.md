# Mockups vs real photos: what you can show and how to label it

Read this before any mockup goes on a product page, a post or an ad (steps 6 and 7 of the skill).

## Three kinds of product image

| Kind | What it is | How to label it |
|---|---|---|
| **Template mockup** | your print file placed on the provider's photo or 3D template (Printful / Printify mockup generators, Photoshop smart objects) | "mockup" in the alt text; on the listing, say images are mockups until real photos exist |
| **AI mockup** | an image model places the design on a garment or a person, or invents the whole scene | "AI-generated mockup" on the image or in the first lines of the listing, plus the platform's AI label when posted |
| **Real photo** | a photo of a sample you ordered and received | no label needed; keep the original file and order number |

Customers decide based on images. A mockup is fine; a mockup presented as a photo of the real product is not.

## Rules

1. **Never call a mockup a photo.** No "actual product", "real photo" or "IRL" on anything generated or templated.
2. **The mockup must match the product.** Same garment type and cut, same print method (no fake embroidery texture on
   a DTG print, no foil or puff effect the product doesn't have), same print size and placement, colours as close as
   the sample shows. If the sample prints duller, update the mockup.
3. **No invented customers.** Don't generate "customer photos", reviews, unboxings or "spotted in the wild" images.
   Fake testimonials are banned on every platform and by this library.
4. **Real people need consent.** Don't put a real person (a friend, a creator, a celebrity) in an AI mockup without their
   written permission; never a public figure.
5. **Adults only** as models, stated in the prompt ("adult woman in her late twenties").
6. **Show a real photo as soon as you have one.** Order a sample before launch; shoot it on a plain background in
   daylight and put it first or second in the gallery.
7. **Say who makes it.** If a print-on-demand company produces the item, say so where the marketplace asks (Etsy has a
   production partner setting) and keep shipping times honest.
8. **Disclose AI where the platform asks.** Etsy's Creativity Standards ask sellers to say in the listing when AI tools
   were used to create an item (checked 2026-10). Social posts get the platform AI label
   ([../../ai-disclosure-and-provenance/SKILL.md](../../ai-disclosure-and-provenance/SKILL.md)).
9. **Paid or gifted merch posts** by other creators carry `#ad` and the paid-partnership label.

## Wording that works

- Listing, first image caption or description line: `Images are mockups (AI-generated mockup on image 1–3). Image 4 is
  a photo of our printed sample.`
- Alt text: `AI-generated mockup: white heavyweight tee with a peach star and the word "studio" printed on the chest.`
- Social caption (Claudia's voice): `the studio tee, as a mockup. sample's in the mail, real photos when it lands`
  with the platform's AI label on.
- Pre-order without a sample: `Pre-order. The photos are mockups; the first sample photos go up before anything ships.`

## Checking an AI mockup before use

- [ ] The printed design is pixel-identical to the print file (AI edits often redraw logos and letters; paste the real
      design over it in an editor if the model changed it).
- [ ] Text on the garment is spelled right and not warped beyond the fabric's folds.
- [ ] Garment colour and cut match the product's catalogue entry.
- [ ] No extra logos, labels or tags the product doesn't have; no brand marks on shoes, phones or bags in the scene.
- [ ] Hands, faces and fabric folds look natural; the model is clearly an adult.
- [ ] Label in place.
