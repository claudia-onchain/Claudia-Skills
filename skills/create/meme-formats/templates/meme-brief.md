# Meme brief and go / no-go

One per meme. Any "no-go" answer stops the meme; rewrite or drop it.

```text
IDEA (one line):        <the joke in plain words>
WHO IS THE JOKE ON:     <the character herself | a shared situation | an idea or habit>     (never: a person, a group, a community)
FORMAT FAMILY:          <caption+reaction | reject/approve | labelled scene | expectation/reality | POV | macro | starter pack | escalation | dialogue | native remix>
TREND (if any):         <name> · first seen <date> · peaked <date or "rising"> · origin checked at <source>
PLATFORMS:              <instagram 4:5 | tiktok 9:16 | x 1:1 | threads | bluesky>
WINDOW:                 post by <date/time>; if later, skip or make it evergreen

IMAGES:                 <generated: prompt ids> · <licensed: licence file> · <in-app remix: creator allowed it yes/no>
REAL PEOPLE IN IT:      <none>                                                                (anything else: no-go)
LOGOS / BRANDS IN IT:   <none | named brand, with written OK>
CAPTION ON IMAGE:       <≤ 20 words caption layout · ≤ 8 words per panel · ≤ 6 words per macro line>
POST CAPTION:           <line 1> · <soft question or nothing> · <2–4 hashtags>
ALT TEXT:               AI-generated image: <scene>. Text: '<all text on the image>'.
LABELS:                 ai=true · ad=<paid/gifted/affiliate?> · nfa=<coin mentioned?>
```

## Go / no-go

| Check | Go | No-go |
|---|---|---|
| Target | herself, a shared situation, an idea | a person, a group, a protected trait, a smaller creator, a community |
| Topic | studio, agents, fashion, music, creator life | tragedy, war, election, death, health scare, a live crisis |
| People | none, or the character only | any real face, voice, name in a fake quote, or "looks like" |
| Images | generated by you, or licensed, or in-app remix allowed by the creator | a downloaded frame, a stock photo you didn't license, fan art |
| Trend | origin checked, meaning understood, still rising or evergreen | unknown origin, contested meaning, peaked over a week ago |
| Money | no coin, or news/education with NFA | price calls, "moon", "buy", returns, a cashtag punchline |
| Honesty | clearly a joke, AI label on | could pass as a real event, a real screenshot or a real quote |
| Approval | a person read it cold and laughed, or at least didn't wince | only the author has seen it |
