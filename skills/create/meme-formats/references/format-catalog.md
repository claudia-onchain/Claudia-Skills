# Meme format catalog: structures you can rebuild with your own images

Read this in step 2. Specific trends change weekly (find them with
[trend-research](../../../grow/trend-research/SKILL.md)); these families keep coming back because they are structures,
not pictures. Every recipe below is rebuilt with the character's own generated images, so no one else's photo, film
still or artwork is reused. The structure of a joke is not owned; the famous frame usually is.

| # | Family | Shape | Layout in `meme-card.html` | Claudia version |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Caption + reaction** | "me when…" / "me after…" line above one reaction image | `caption` | her side-eye at a laptop: "me after my agents ask if I want to 'review anything'" |
| 2 | **Reject / approve** | two rows: a thing she turns away from, a thing she points at | `panels` | "posting 31 times a week" / "posting 4 times and answering every reply" |
| 3 | **Labelled scene** | one picture, 2–4 objects labelled with short text | `caption` with labels set in the image layer, or a carousel slide | desk with labels: "the prompt", "the 14 refs", "me", "the deadline" |
| 4 | **Expectation / reality** | two panels, same pose, different outcome | `panels` | "the shoot plan: silver foil, one flash" / "the shoot: foil on the ceiling" |
| 5 | **POV** | "POV: …" over a still or a 5-second clip | `caption`, or on-screen text in a short | "POV: you asked your agent for one post" |
| 6 | **Image macro** | short top line + bottom line on one image | `macro` (Anton) | "visor on / do not perceive me" |
| 7 | **Starter pack / tier list** | a grid of objects with a title | carousel or a 3×3 collage | "studio day starter pack": headset mic, foil, star clip, cold brew |
| 8 | **Escalation** | 3–4 rows, each step "bigger" than the last | `panels` extended to 4 rows | "posting" → "scheduling" → "an agent scheduling" → "an agent asking me how I feel about it" |
| 9 | **Dialogue / "nobody:"** | text-only exchange | quote card ([quote-cards-and-text-posts](../../quote-cards-and-text-posts/SKILL.md)) | "nobody: / my agent at 3am: 'small idea'" |
| 10 | **Native remix** | Stitch / Duet (TikTok), Remix (Instagram) of a post whose creator allowed it | in the app only | reacting to a public creator tutorial with her own try |

## Prompt recipes for reaction images

Reaction images work when the expression reads at thumbnail size. Use the identity block from
[claudia-character-bible](../../claudia-character-bible/SKILL.md) and ref pack images, then one of these middles:

| Reaction | Prompt middle (after the identity block) | Ref to add |
|---|---|---|
| Side-eye | "looking sideways at the camera without turning her head, eyebrows slightly raised, mouth closed in a flat line, holding a coffee cup" | `refs/claudia/01-front-neutral.jpg` |
| Deadpan stare | "staring straight into the lens, completely neutral face, one slow blink implied, sitting very still" | `01-front-neutral.jpg` |
| Full laugh | "laughing with her head tipped back, eyes squeezed shut, one hand on her chest" | `refs/claudia/05-laugh.jpg` |
| Pointing approval | "pointing at something off-frame to the right with one finger, a small satisfied smile" | `refs/claudia/08-hand-collar.jpg` |
| Turning away | "turning her head away with one hand raised palm-out as if to say no, eyes closed" | `08-hand-collar.jpg` |
| Visor on | "sliding on an orange shield visor, chin up, unbothered" | `refs/claudia/06-visor.jpg` |
| Over it | "lying face-down on a white desk next to a laptop, arms limp, the headset mic still on" | `01-front-neutral.jpg` |

Framing: medium close-up, plain or studio background (white studio, cobalt, silver foil), empty space at the top for a
caption or at top and bottom for a macro. Add "No text, no logos." to every prompt and set type in the template.

## Formats to skip

- Any format built on a real person's face (celebrity reaction stills, politicians, streamers, "random guy" photos).
  Rebuild the structure with the character instead.
- Formats that only work by mocking a group, an accent, a body or a disability.
- Formats tied to a tragedy, a war, an election or a death. Claudia's voice rules rule these out completely.
- "Fake news screenshot" formats (a headline, a chyron, a fake post from a real account). They read as real out of context.
- Formats whose origin you can't trace. Look it up first (step 3 of the skill); some come from hate communities.
