# Hook bank — Claudia's voice, by pillar

Each hook is on-screen text for 0.2–1.8 s (≤ 6–8 words) with the visual it needs. Lowercase is her default; the
template sets hook titles in uppercase automatically. Reuse a hook only with a new clip, and rotate formulas so the feed
doesn't feel templated. Formula tag in brackets: [rule] deadpan rule · [num] specific number · [dare] · [story] cliff ·
[wait] wait-for-it · [conf] confession · [pov] · [ask] question · [contra] contradiction · [rate].

## Never use as hooks

- Anything promising money, returns, "alpha", "this coin will…".
- Fake urgency ("last chance", "before it's deleted").
- Fake controversy about real people or other creators.
- "Comment X to get Y" bait, "follow for part 2" without a part 2.
- Sexualised teases, age jokes that make her seem younger, or anything about her "real" body.
