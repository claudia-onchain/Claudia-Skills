# Claudia series catalogue — beats, hooks, shots

Read this when writing an episode. Each series has: the promise, the beat sheet with timings, a hook bank (10+), the shot
list with prompt hooks into the other skills, and what "done" looks like. All clips are 9:16 1080×1920 unless noted;
keep captions and faces inside the centred ~900×1400 safe area (checked 2026-10, see
[../../thumbnails-and-covers/SKILL.md](../../thumbnails-and-covers/SKILL.md)).

---

## "talk to me" (P1 · bedroom talks)

**Promise:** one small story or opinion, told like she's on FaceTime with you.

**Beats (15–25 s)**

| t | Beat | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–1.5 s | Hook line, the weird detail first | face fills the upper-middle; on-screen text = hook |
| 1.5–10 s | The story in 2–3 sentences | one gesture, one look away and back |
| 10–18 s | Twist or punchline | laugh — her open laugh is the series signature |
| last 1–2 s | Soft question or loop-back to line 1 | "anyway. the cat says hi." |

**Hook bank**
- "ok so the cat filed a complaint"
- "storytime: the 3am post"
- "things I'm bad at: a list"
- "unpopular opinion about sunsets"
- "I need to tell someone this and you're here"
- "my agent did something and I'm proud and scared"
- "the comment that made my week"
- "I tried a morning routine (again)"
- "rating my week out of ten, honestly"
- "the most New York thing happened"
- "small confession"
- "you're not going to believe what the queue did"

**Shots:** single locked-off front-camera shot in her room by day (white walls, smoke detector, doorway), signature look.
Prompts: [../../selfie-and-ugc-video-prompts/SKILL.md](../../selfie-and-ugc-video-prompts/SKILL.md) ("bedroom vlog").
Audio: voice-over or native dialogue + lip-sync ([../../voice-and-lip-sync/SKILL.md](../../voice-and-lip-sync/SKILL.md)).

**Done when:** one story, one laugh, ≤ 25 s, captions word-by-word, AI label set.

## "agent diaries" (P5 · desk, platform & agents)

**Promise:** a real, specific thing an agent did today — funny, useful or a lesson — told by her.

**Beats (20–30 s)**

| t | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–2 s | A number or a verb: "my agents posted 14 times today" |
| 2–12 s | Screen insert (dashboard, queue, a draft preview) with her face as a corner bubble |
| 12–22 s | What it means / the lesson ("spend caps are love") |
| last 2 s | Punchline with the cat, or "what should they do tomorrow?" |

**Hook bank:** "my agents posted 14 times today" · "an agent asked me for a raise" · "the agent that wouldn't reply" ·
"my spend cap said no at 2am" · "I let an agent write my caption and…" · "what my approval queue looked like this morning" ·
"the kill switch, explained by someone who uses it" · "my agent found a bug before I did" · "one rule I gave every agent" ·
"why my agents can't approve their own jobs"

**Shots:** desk at night, cosy black jumper, butterfly laptop, cat asleep; screen captures must show only your own
account's data, no keys, no wallet balances you wouldn't publish. Never show a coin chart with a "buy" framing.

**Done when:** a concrete number or event, a useful lesson, no promises, no tickers on TikTok, AI label set.

## "skill of the week" (P5 · value)

**Promise:** you can do this today in 3 steps.

**Beats (30–45 s):** 0–2 s outcome first ("a banner in four prompts") → 3 steps, each a 6–10 s screen beat with a big
step number → result reveal → "the skill's linked in the thread". Keep on-screen step text ≤ 6 words.

**Hook bank:** "a banner in four prompts" · "captions people actually read" · "label your AI posts in 30 seconds" ·
"keep your character the same person" · "make a 9:16 from a 16:9 without losing the face" · "a month of posts in one
afternoon" · "the cheapest way to test a video idea" · "stop your character looking 17" · "how I name prompts so I can
find them" · "music you're actually allowed to use"

**Shots:** screen recording + one Claudia still or 5 s clip as the open and close. Voice-over via
`elevenlabs/v3` or `google/gemini-3.8-flash-tts` with her designed voice.

## "after midnight" (P2 · club & music)

**Promise:** 8 seconds of the night, no talking.

**Beats (6–10 s):** frame 1 already moving (arm up, light hit) → one move on the beat → light sweep → loops cleanly.
Caption carries the voice: "this set > my entire to-do list".

**Hook bank (captions):** "sunglasses indoors, as the DJ intended" · "this set > my entire to-do list" · "the drop was so
good I forgot I can't technically hear it" · "rate the transition 1–10" · "halloween set. costume: the same sunglasses" ·
"friday mode: on" · "the DJ played the one" · "lights hit different at 1am" · "saturday plans: dance; nap; repeat" ·
"ears ringing in the best way (metaphorically)"

**Shots:** DJ booth, blue-violet haze, crowd with raised hands — **background people anonymous and soft-focus**, the DJ
generic (never a real DJ), no readable mixer brand logos. Music: commercially cleared only
([../../music-and-sound-for-shorts/SKILL.md](../../music-and-sound-for-shorts/SKILL.md)).

## "sunglasses on / off" (P3 · style)

**Promise:** one 8-second reveal loop with an outfit detail.

**Beats:** 0–0.5 s sunglasses on, half-smile → 0.5–4 s fingers lower the glasses slowly → 4–6 s eye contact, the
half-smile widens → 6–8 s glasses back up = seamless loop.

**Captions:** "sunglasses on = do not perceive me. sunglasses off = ok fine, hi" · "grandma's lamp, my sunglasses, our
secret" · "sunglasses off. ok fine. hi" · "I came as myself. it's a strong costume" · "the outfit is thrifted. the
confidence is generated" · "lamp-lit and unbothered"

**Shots:** vintage lounge, brass lamp left of frame, botanical prints behind, dark wood dresser; she leans on the dresser.
Prompt family: [../../selfie-and-ugc-video-prompts/SKILL.md](../../selfie-and-ugc-video-prompts/SKILL.md) ("lounge reveal").

## "forecast dared me" (P4 · rain & city)

**Promise:** weather as a dare; she always wins.

**Beats (10–15 s):** 0–1 s on-screen text with the forecast ("40 % chance of rain") → she's already running, phone at
arm's length, laughing → spin or jump splash at ~6 s → slows, catches breath, grins → end on a wink or the next dare.

**Captions:** "rain check? never heard of her" · "running in the rain because the forecast dared me" · "warehouse lot,
6pm, zero umbrellas, full serotonin" · "wind advisory. respectfully no" · "november rain. not the song. the actual weather"

**Shots:** warehouse lot, wet asphalt, loading-dock doors, bollards, overcast dusk; selfie-arm framing with the chain
bracelet visible. Watch for melting hands/phone at the arm — check middle frames.

## "window seat" (P6 · travel)

**Promise:** somewhere beautiful, at the right hour.

**Format:** 5–8 tile carousel (4:5, 1080×1350) or a 3 × 3 s clip stack. Tile 1 = the strongest face-plus-place image;
tile 2 = place only; alternate. Places: Santorini at golden hour, airplane window over sunset clouds, city-night balcony,
a train window, a café terrace. Never claim she's at a real event or with real people; places are fine, crowds are extras.

**Captions:** "airplane window. no thoughts. just clouds" · "Santorini is just a very bright screensaver you can stand in" ·
"balcony, city lights, one glass of something. the algorithm can wait" · "a month in pictures. most of them are the cat"

## "the CTO" (P7 · cat cameos)

**Promise:** the cat has a job title and takes it seriously.

**Format:** 5–8 s loop or a 4:5 still; the cat (grey tabby, white paws) does an "office" thing — sleeps on the keyboard,
sits on the laptop, stares at the monitor, knocks over the butterfly mug. Claudia optional (hands only or out of focus).

**Captions:** "the CTO is reviewing my PR" · "the CTO found the kill switch" · "weekend standup. attendance: one cat" ·
"the CTO walked across the keyboard and shipped a typo" · "CTO monthly review: exceeds expectations at sleeping" · "the
CTO has been briefed on halloween. the CTO declines"

Keep the cat identical: same tabby markings, white paws, green-gold eyes — add a cat line to the ref pack.
