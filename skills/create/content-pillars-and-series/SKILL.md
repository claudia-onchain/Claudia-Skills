# Content pillars and series

A feed people follow is a set of promises: "you'll get rainy-city clips, club nights and a peek at how the agents work."
Pillars are those promises; series are the repeatable formats that keep them. This skill gives Claudia's pillars and
series, the ratio that keeps platform talk from turning into ads, a cadence per network, and a filled 4-week calendar an
agent can copy straight into a batch run.

## When to use this

- Planning next month (or next week) of posts before a batch run.
- "What should I post today?" — pick the pillar that's furthest behind its target share.
- Naming or redesigning a recurring series.
- A launch or feature announcement needs a home that doesn't wreck the feed's vibe.
- Building pillars for a new character (step 7).

## What you need

- The character bible: [../claudia-character-bible/SKILL.md](../claudia-character-bible/SKILL.md) (worlds, wardrobe, voice).
- Last month's numbers per pillar if you have them ([../../grow/kpi-reporting/SKILL.md](../../grow/kpi-reporting/SKILL.md)).
- The posting slots from [../../grow/posting-schedule/SKILL.md](../../grow/posting-schedule/SKILL.md).

- Read [references/series-catalogue.md](references/series-catalogue.md) when writing an episode — it has the beat sheet,
  hook bank and shot list for every series.

## Steps

### 1. Know the seven pillars

Each pillar maps to a world in the bible, so the visuals are already defined.

| # | Pillar | What it promises | World / look | Formats | Target share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Bedroom talks** | storytime, opinions, behind-the-scenes, "talk to me" energy | her room by day; signature or cosy black | talking-to-camera 9:16, 10–25 s | 20 % |
| P2 | **Club & music nights** | energy, taste, the weekend | DJ booth, blue-violet haze; signature + sunglasses | dancing clips 6–10 s, set picks | 15 % |
| P3 | **Style in the lounge** | outfits, thrift finds, sunglasses moments | vintage lounge, brass lamp | 6–8 s reveal loops, carousels | 10 % |
| P4 | **Rain & city** | mood, weather, NYC (in the story) | warehouse lot in rain, rooftop dusk, streets | running selfies, ambient walks | 15 % |
| P5 | **Desk, platform & agents** | how she "runs this place": skills, agent tips, what shipped | desk at night, butterfly laptop, monitors, cat | screen-and-face shorts, threads, banners | 20 % |
| P6 | **Travel dumps** | escape, golden hour, light | Santorini, airplane window, balcony | carousels / collage, 3–5 s clip stacks | 10 % |
| P7 | **Cat cameos** | comfort, running jokes ("the cat is the CTO") | bed, desk | 5–8 s loops, reaction inserts | 10 % |

### 2. Apply the ratio rule

- **70 / 20 / 10:** 70 % lifestyle and personality (P1–P4, P6, P7), 20 % platform value (P5: tips, skills, how-tos that
  help the viewer even if they never sign up), 10 % direct asks (launches, "try this skill", collabs).
- Never two direct asks in a row on the same network. A direct ask always sits between two lifestyle posts.
- Coins: at most one quiet mention a week, always educational (how to read a safety score), always "not financial
  advice", never on TikTok (TikTok removes crypto promotion, checked 2026-10 — see
  [../../grow/crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md](../../grow/crypto-marketing-compliance/SKILL.md)).
- Paid or gifted anything: `#ad` and the platform's paid-partnership toggle ([../ai-disclosure-and-provenance/SKILL.md](../ai-disclosure-and-provenance/SKILL.md)).

### 3. Run named series, not one-offs

A named series gives the audience a reason to come back and gives the agent a template. Claudia's eight:

| Series | Pillar | Format | Cadence | Hook pattern |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **"talk to me"** | P1 | 15–25 s talking to camera, one story, one laugh | 2×/week | "ok so…" + the weird detail first |
| **"agent diaries"** | P5 | 20–30 s: her face + a screen insert of what an agent did today | 2×/week | a number: "my agents posted 14 times today" |
| **"skill of the week"** | P5 | 30–45 s how-to, 3 steps on screen, link in bio/thread | 1×/week | the outcome: "make a banner in 4 prompts" |
| **"after midnight"** | P2 | 6–10 s club loop + set pick in caption | 1×/week (Fri/Sat) | no words, motion first frame |
| **"sunglasses on / off"** | P3 | 8 s lounge reveal loop | 1×/week | the reveal at 0.5 s |
| **"forecast dared me"** | P4 | 10–15 s rain or weather selfie run | when it fits, ≤ 1×/week | the weather fact as on-screen text |
| **"window seat"** | P6 | 5–8 tile carousel or 3×3 s clip stack | 2×/month | the place, no caption hook needed |
| **"the CTO"** | P7 | 5–8 s cat loop + one-line caption | 1×/week | the cat doing a "job" |

Episode beats, hook banks and shot lists for each are in [references/series-catalogue.md](references/series-catalogue.md).
New series: fill [templates/series-card.md](templates/series-card.md), run it 4 episodes, then keep or kill (step 6).

### 4. Set cadence per network (checked 2026-10)

These are sustainable defaults for one character with a weekly batch, inside `@useclaudia/social` daily caps (X 10,
TikTok 5, YouTube 5, Instagram 20 per account per 24 h). Adjust with your own data.

| Network | Posts / week | What goes there | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 5–7 | P1, P2, P3, P4, P7 shorts; P5 only as pure how-to | no coins at all; AIGC label on every post |
| Instagram Reels | 4–5 + 1 carousel | same shorts, P6 carousels, P3 | Reels cover safe area 1080×1440 centre (3:4 grid) |
| YouTube Shorts | 3–5 | best performers from TikTok/Reels, "skill of the week" | ≤ 60 s if any music might be claimed (Content ID blocks > 1 min Shorts with claims) |
| X | 7–14 | captions, threads for P5, clips, banners | Automated label on the account; one `$cashtag` per API post max |
| Telegram / Discord | 3–5 | P5 updates, behind-the-scenes, polls | community, not broadcast spam |

Cross-posting rule: the same clip may go to TikTok, Reels and Shorts, but re-cut the first second and rewrite the caption
for each (near-duplicate text to the same account within 24 h is blocked by `@useclaudia/social` anyway).

### 5. Fill the 4-week calendar

Start from [templates/content-calendar-4wk.csv](templates/content-calendar-4wk.csv) — it is already filled for a
real month (2026-10-12 → 2026-11-08) with pillar, series, platform, look, setting, hook, caption draft, model plan and
status. Process:

1. Lock fixed beats first: launches, collabs, holidays (Halloween 2026-10-31 sits in week 3 — a costume episode of
   "sunglasses on / off").
2. Place series episodes at their cadence.
3. Fill gaps with the pillar furthest under its target share.
4. Check the ratio per week: count rows with `type=ask` — ≤ 10 % of the week.
5. Check the ratio **per network**, not only overall. In the sample month P5 is 28 % of all rows but 0 % of TikTok/Reels
   rows: the platform talk lives on X, Telegram and Shorts how-tos, the video feeds stay lifestyle. That's by design.
6. Hand the week's rows to the batch: [../batch-content-production/SKILL.md](../batch-content-production/SKILL.md).

```sh
# pillar share overall, then on TikTok/Reels only (column 4 = pillar, 7 = networks, 14 = type)
awk -F, 'NR>1 {n[$4]++; t++} END {for (p in n) printf "%s %d%%\n", p, n[p]*100/t}' templates/content-calendar-4wk.csv | sort
awk -F, 'NR>1 && $7 ~ /tiktok|reels/ {n[$4]++; t++} END {for (p in n) printf "%s %d%%\n", p, n[p]*100/t}' templates/content-calendar-4wk.csv | sort
awk -F, 'NR>1 {c[$14]++} END {for (k in c) print k, c[k]}' templates/content-calendar-4wk.csv
```

Keep commas out of CSV cells (use `;`) so these one-liners and spreadsheet imports stay reliable.

### 6. Review monthly: keep, fix or kill

Per series, look at 3 numbers over its last 4 episodes: average watch-through (or 3-second hold), saves+shares per 1k
views, follows per episode. Then:

- **Keep** if it's at or above the account median on 2 of 3.
- **Fix** (new hook pattern or length) if 1 of 3.
- **Kill** if 0 of 3 after 4 episodes. Retire its name; don't let zombie series clutter the calendar.

Write the decision into the series card. Experiments go through [../../grow/growth-experiments/SKILL.md](../../grow/growth-experiments/SKILL.md).

### 7. Derive pillars for a new character

1. List the character's 5–8 settings from its bible. Each strong setting is a pillar candidate.
2. Add exactly one "what I do / what I help with" pillar (the P5 equivalent). It's the reason the account exists beyond vibes.
3. Add one comfort pillar (pet, ritual, recurring object). Running jokes live here.
4. Cut to 5–7. More than 7 and the audience can't tell what the account is.
5. For each pillar write one promise sentence and two series ideas. Test the name: would someone say "did you see the new
   ___ episode"?
6. Make sure no pillar or series name copies Claudia's ("talk to me", "agent diaries", "the CTO" are hers).

Worked example: [examples/pillars-for-a-new-character.md](examples/pillars-for-a-new-character.md).

## Check before you finish

- [ ] Every calendar row has a pillar, a series (or `one-off` with a reason) and a network.
- [ ] Weekly ratio ≈ 70/20/10; no two direct asks back to back on one network.
- [ ] TikTok rows contain no coin, ticker or buy language.
- [ ] Every row's look and setting exist in the character bible.
- [ ] Each network's weekly count is within its cap and cadence.
- [ ] Rows that are paid/gifted are marked `ad`; every row is marked for an AI label.
- [ ] Series older than 4 episodes have a keep/fix/kill decision.

## Pitfalls

- **Platform creep.** P5 swells to 50 % because it's "important". The audience came for the person. Hold the ratio.
- **Same clip, same caption everywhere.** Blocked as near-duplicate on one account, and lazy across accounts.
- **Series without a name.** "Another rain video" can't build anticipation; "forecast dared me" can.
- **Trend-chasing outside the pillars.** If a trend doesn't fit a pillar, skip it or reshape it into one
  ([../../grow/trend-research/SKILL.md](../../grow/trend-research/SKILL.md)).
- **Holiday content that breaks character.** Halloween is a costume in her style, not a different person.
- **Calendar without status.** Always track `idea → scripted → generated → approved → scheduled → posted`.
