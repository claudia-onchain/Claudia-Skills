# Worked example: pillars and series for "Mara Vex"

Mara Vex is the analogue-synth, night-train character built in
[../../claudia-character-bible/examples/building-a-distinct-character.md](../../claudia-character-bible/examples/building-a-distinct-character.md).
Her operator runs step 7 of the skill.

## 1. Settings → pillar candidates

| Setting from her bible | Candidate pillar |
|---|---|
| sleeper-train compartment at night | **Night trains** |
| synth studio with patch cables | **Patch notes** (the "what I do / help with" pillar) |
| station platforms at night | folded into Night trains |
| record shop basement | **Crate digging** |
| her kitchen at 6am with a kettle | **Kettle hour** (comfort pillar; running joke: the kettle is out of tune) |
| city rooftops in fog | **Fog walks** |

Five pillars — inside the 5–7 range.

## 2. Promise sentences and series

| Pillar | Promise | Series 1 | Series 2 | Share |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Night trains | "the quietest 20 minutes of your day, on rails" | **"the 23:40"** — 20 s window + one sound | **"platform 9"** — station stills carousel | 25 % |
| Patch notes | "one sound you can make tonight" | **"one-cable patch"** — 30 s how-to | **"what this knob does"** — 15 s explainer | 25 % |
| Crate digging | "a record you haven't heard" | **"bin 4"** — 15 s find + why | — | 15 % |
| Fog walks | "a city you can't see" | **"low visibility"** — ambient 10 s | — | 15 % |
| Kettle hour | "the morning, slowly" | **"out of tune"** — kettle note vs synth note, 8 s | — | 20 % |

Name test: "did you see the new 'one-cable patch'?" — yes, people would say that. No name overlaps Claudia's series.

## 3. Ratio and cadence

70 / 20 / 10 still applies: Patch notes is the value pillar (20–25 %), direct asks (her operator's sample-pack shop,
marked `#ad` when sponsored by a gear brand) ≤ 10 %. Cadence: TikTok 5/week, Shorts 3/week, Instagram 3/week + 1
carousel; no X at launch (the audience lives elsewhere).

## 4. Music rule specific to her

Every track she plays is either her own generated audio from a model whose terms allow commercial use, or library music
cleared for the account type. No real artists' songs, no "in the style of <artist>" prompts
([../../music-and-sound-for-shorts/SKILL.md](../../music-and-sound-for-shorts/SKILL.md)).

## 5. First month result (operator's notes)

"the 23:40" became the anchor series (best hold rate); "what this knob does" was killed after 4 episodes (0 of 3
metrics above median) and replaced by "one-cable patch" part 2s.
