# Moment rubric

Read this in step 3, before proposing or picking moments. Score each candidate 1–5 on five lines. An agent proposes
the scores; a person watches the moment and sets the final ones.

| Line | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hook** (first 3 s) | starts with setup, "so, um" | a clear topic line | a surprising claim, number or question in the first sentence |
| **Standalone** | needs the previous 5 minutes | needs one line of context in the caption | makes sense to someone who never heard of the show |
| **Payoff** | trails off | answers the hook | answers it and adds a twist, laugh or concrete takeaway |
| **Length** | over 90 s with no way to cut | 45–90 s | 15–45 s with a natural end |
| **Risk** (reverse: 5 = safe) | a guest's private detail, legal or medical claims, a price call | strong opinion that needs care | nothing that could hurt anyone or mislead |

Keep a moment when **Hook ≥ 4, Standalone ≥ 4 and Risk = 5**, and the total is 20 or more. Everything else stays in
the log as "not used" with the reason, so the next pick learns from it.

## Signals in a transcript that usually mean a good moment

- A number with a unit: "we lost 40% of viewers at minute two".
- A contrast: "everyone says X. we did the opposite."
- A list announced out loud: "three things killed that launch".
- A story with a turn: "and then the agent posted it at 3am".
- A laugh from the other person (in the audio, not the transcript: listen).
- A question from chat or a caller that many people share.

## Signals that look good but aren't

- Loud moments with no content (shouting, a song drop, a raid alert).
- Inside jokes from earlier in the stream.
- A guest's line that only works with their face reacting off-camera.
- Price talk, "this coin will", or any promise of results. Never clip it, even as a joke.

## Rules the picker keeps

1. Watch every candidate in full at 1×; transcripts miss tone and sarcasm.
2. Check the 30 s before and after: is the clip fair to what was meant?
3. Guests: if in doubt, send them the clip before it goes out.
4. Write who picked it and when in the moment log.
